### 28-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 24 x Gigabit RJ45, 4 x RJ45/SFP Combo Ports, with 4 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

S5810-28TS is a high-performance Layer 3 switch designed for campus networks, SMBs, branch offices, and distributed sites. With 28x 10/100/1000BASE-T RJ45 downlinks (including 4x combo ports) and 4x 10G SFP+ uplinks, it provides high-density access and non-blocking forwarding. Equipped with a Broadcom chip, it ensures reliable performance with hot-swappable redundant power supplies and smart fans. Pre-installed with the [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) system (perpetual license included), it supports comprehensive Layer 2/3 features, OpenFlow, and NETCONF with no additional licenses and includes five years of hardware and software support.

The switch is managed through the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), enabling Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, real-time telemetry monitoring, and end-to-end lifecycle management from Day 0 to Day 2. AmpCon simplifies switch onboarding, configuration, monitoring, and ongoing management, improving network visibility and operational efficiency.
