### S5850-24XMG, 24-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 24 x 10GBASE-T, with 2 x 100Gb QSFP28, Support MPLS&MLAG

The S5850-24XMG is a Layer 3 switch designed for flexible and high-performance deployments, equipped with 24x 10/100/1000M/2.5G/5G/10GBASE-T ports and 2x 100Gb uplinks. It ensures backward compatibility with existing Ethernet cabling and standard RJ45 connectors, enabling cost-effective network upgrades.

This switch offers high density, low latency, and high availability with MLAG, stacking and redundant hot-swappable power supplies and fans, ensuring resilient operation. It also supports advanced L2/L3 features, including BGP, OSPF, VRF, and MPLS, along with key security features such as MACsec, 802.1X, and ACL. Ideal for enterprise aggregation, Wi-Fi 6/6E high-speed access, and high-end wiring closet deployments.
