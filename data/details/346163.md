### T5570-48S6C, 48 x 10G SFP+ with 6 x 100G QSFP28 Ports, Network Packet Broker (NPB), PicOS®

The FS T5570-48S6C is a high-density Network Packet Broker (NPB), equipped with 48x 10G SFP+ and 6x 100G QSFP28 ports, bringing you the flexibility and scalability network traffic management with a total throughput of 1.08 Tbps in a 1U rack space. The device runs PicOS® under a perpetual license and includes five years of bundled service.

The NPB also supports advanced features such as traffic aggregation, replication, filtering, and load balancing. Additionally, GRE and VXLAN tunneling are included without per-port license fees, thereby enhancing the performance of network analysis and security tools. It delivering a scalable network visibility solution for on-premises data centers, financial institutions, government agencies, and ISP networks.

## Software Spotlights

## Pervasive Visibility (图标: https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/2022113017252970eiku.svg)

- Aggregate network traffic to a single or multiple tools (1:1, 1:N, N:1, N:N)
- Support traffic replication and port distribution
- Ingress and egress filter support IPv4, IPv6
- Drop filter support IPv4, IPv6
- 9K filtering rules
- Configurable hash-based load balancing
- Support uploading and downloading files via TFTP, FTP, SFTP, and WebUI
- Support OpenFlow/SDN

## Advanced Functionality (图标: https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20221130172533h8j9rj.svg)

- VxLAN, GRE, NvGRE, Tunnel Encapsulate and Decapsulate
- Support jumbo frames
- Support UDF L2, L3 and L4
- Support sFLOW、NTP、SNMP Get/Trap
- Support VLAN tagging and stripping
- Support GRE de-tunneling, VXLAN de-tunneling

## Operation & Management (图标: https://resource.fs.com/mall/ckfinder/ftp_images/product/20211113115011_341.svg)
