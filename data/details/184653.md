### S5500-48T6S, 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 48x Gigabit RJ45, with 6x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Support Stacking

The S5500-48T6S is a next-generation stackable access switch ideal for enterprise branches, campus networks, and SMBs. Powered by a Realtek chipset, it delivers 216 Gbps switching capacity and 162 Mpps forwarding performance. With 48 Gigabit RJ45 downlink ports and 6x 1/10G SFP+ uplink ports, it supports up to 8-unit virtual stacking for seamless interconnection and centralized management.

Designed for high availability and simplified operations, it features redundant hot-swappable AC power supplies and variable-speed fans to enhance reliability. Multiple management options—via Web, CLI, SNMP, and SSH—enable flexible O&M, improved visibility, and stronger network security. These capabilities make it a scalable and cost-effective solution for access-layer deployments.
