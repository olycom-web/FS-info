### IES5120-48T4S, 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Industrial Switch, 48 x 10/100/1000BASE-T, with 4 x 1/10Gb SFP+

The IES5120-48T4S features 48x 10/100/1000BASE-T copper ports, 4x 1/10Gb SFP+ ports, which enables high-density connectivity for large-scale industrial networks and extended enterprise deployments. Equipped with an IP40-rated metal enclosure and a wide operating temperature range of -40 to 75°C (-40 to 167°F), this switch is ideal for harsh environments, such as traffic control cabinets, factory floors, and outdoor places.

This Layer 2+ industrial managed switch supports IEEE 1588v2 PTP for precise time synchronization. It also enables transparent transmission of PROFINET, EtherNet/IP, and Modbus TCP, ensuring reliable and seamless industrial communication.
