### 24-Port Ethernet L3 PoE++ Switch, 24x 10GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit, 4x 10Gb SFP+, with 4x 25Gb SFP28 uplinks, Support MLAG & Perpetual PoE, Broadcom Chip, PicOS®

S5860-24XB-U, next-gen L3 multi-gigabit switch featuring 24x 100M/1000M/2.5G/5G/10GBase-T, up to 90W PoE++ per port, with 4x 1/10G SFP+ and 4x 10G/25G SFP28 uplinks. Ideal for enterprise campuses, hotels, and retail, it supports high-density PoE deployments for Wi-Fi 6E/Wi-Fi 7 access points and other devices, with multi-gigabit access, high-power PoE, and high-speed uplinks. It also supports perpetual PoE with power protection, keeping devices powered through reboots for reliable operation.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports OpenFlow, MLAG, LACP, VRRP, VLAN and DHCP Relay without requiring additional licenses, and includes five years of hardware and software support.

Managed by the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html), enabling end-to-end campus networks automation. It offers automated ZTP, real-time telemetry monitoring, topology auto-discovery and lifecycle management, delivering centralized visibility and simplified wired and wireless operations for reliable enterprise networks.
