### 64-Port Ethernet L3 Switch , 64 x 100Gb QSFP28, PicOS®, Broadcom Tomahawk 2 Chip

The S5590-64C is a next-generation Layer 3 switch powered by the Broadcom Tomahawk2 chipset, delivering up to 12.8 Tbps switching capacity. It features a compact 2U design with 64x 40/100G QSFP28 ports and supports 4x 10/25G breakout. Running PicOS® with a perpetual license, it provides comprehensive L2/L3 features, including MLAG, BGP, and OSPF, plus EVPN-VXLAN virtualization (supporting L2VNI only, not L3VNI) and advanced congestion control technologies such as RoCEv2, PFC, and ECN. Integrated security features and five years of complimentary hardware and software technical support ensure reliable operation for enterprise and SMB data centers.

Managed by the [AmpCon-Campus management platform](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html), the switch enables Day 0 to Day 2+ automated lifecycle management with Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology discovery, and real-time telemetry, simplifying operations, improving visibility, and accelerating network deployment.
