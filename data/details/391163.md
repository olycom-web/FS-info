### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 24 x PoE+ Ports @370W, with 4 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

S3540L-24P, a next-gen switch featuring 24x 10/100/1000Mbps PoE ports with 370W total PoE budget and up to 30W per port and 4x 1/10Gb SFP+ uplink ports. It delivers a compact, cost-effective solution for small to medium-sized campus environments such as enterprise offices, educational institutions, and healthcare, facilitating seamless interconnection of IP phones, security cameras and Wi-Fi 6 access points.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG, OSPF, and QoS without requiring additional licenses and also includes five years of hardware and software support.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html?now_cid=5513) (Support for this model is available from AmpCon V3.0.0), enabling Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and end-to-end lifecycle management from Day 0 to Day 2. AmpCon simplifies switch onboarding, configuration, monitoring, and ongoing management, improving network visibility and operational efficiency.
