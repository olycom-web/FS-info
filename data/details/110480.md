### N8560-32C, 32-Port Ethernet L3 Data Center Switch, 32 x 100Gb QSFP28, Support MLAG/Stacking, Broadcom Chip, Front-to-Back Airflow

The N8560-32C is a compact 1U switch delivering high-density 40/100GbE for data center spine-leaf networks. Its 100GbE ports can be channelized into 4ⅹ 25GbE, 2x 50GbE, and 40GbE ports can be channelized into 4ⅹ 10GbE via breakout cables, increasing the total number of 10/25GbE logical ports to 128.

N8560-32C supports PFC, DCBX, ECN and RoCEv2 to deliver low-latency, zero packet loss, non-blocking lossless Ethernet, VXLAN, EVPN for virtual network expansion, and supports features like GR, BFD mechanisms to ensure normal operation when the network bears abundant services and heavy traffic.
