### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 48x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 2.5Gb SFP Uplinks, Broadcom Chip

S3270-48TM, 48-port access switch is equipped with 48x 10/100/1000M RJ45 ports, 4x 2.5Gb SFP uplinks. The Ethernet all-optical network adopts point-to-point transmission. The high-quality signal is transmitted without external interference and the data is forwarded more steadily. This switch delivers 116 Gbps switching capacity and 86.30 Mpps forwarding rate.

Based on the high-performance hardware and FSOS platform, it supports functions such as OSPF, Voice VLAN, LACP, and QoS. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. These are simple, flexible, and secure switches that can connect to more terminals in HD monitoring scenarios and better cope with burst data.
