### 24-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 20 x 10Gb SFP+, with 4 x 25Gb SFP28 and 2 x 40Gb QSFP+, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5860-20SQ is a next-generation L3 switch featuring Broadcom chip, with 760 Gbps switching capacity and 565 Mpps forwarding performance. It features 20× 1/10G downlinks, 4× 10/25G SFP28 uplinks, and 2× 40G QSFP+ uplinks (4× 10G breakout) in a 1U form factor, with redundant hot-swappable power supplies and smart fans for reliability.

Running [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, the switch supports MLAG, SSH, ACLs, BGP, OSPF, PBR, and sFlow without extra licensing and includes five years of hardware and software support. Ideal for enterprise aggregation, SMB cores, and low-density data center ToR deployments.

The switch is managed through the [AmpCon Management Software for Campus Networks](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), enabling centralized network management and automated lifecycle operations from Day 0 to Day 2+. It delivers Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), automated topology discovery, and real-time telemetry, providing end-to-end visibility across the campus network and simplifying operations.
