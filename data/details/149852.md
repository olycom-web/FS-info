### 8-Port 100Gb QSFP28 Line Card for SC9405 Chassis Enterprise Switch

The SC9400-8C-E Ethernet line card supports 8x 25G/40G/100G QSFP28, offering line-rate forwarding from all ports, and backward compatible with SFP modules. Loaded with SC9405 chassis, it delivers high-performance switching capacity and forwarding rate powered by Broadcom chips. With redundant hot-swappable power supplies, a hot-swappable smart fan, and a dual-flash chip, it ensures high performance, reliability, and carrier-class efficiency while reducing cost and complexity.

FS SC9405 switch supports virtual stacking and virtual switching technology to achieve simplified management and flexible resource allocation, ideal for deployment in virtualized environment and cloud platform. The switch also integrates rich IPv4/Ipv6 features such as OSPF, BGP, VRRP, supports QoS for better experience, and VXLAN for network scalability. Compact design with diverse line card options, high performance, and scalability—ideal for deployment in data centers, metro networks, campus networks, or converged data center-campus environments.
