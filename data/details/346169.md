### T8520-32D, 32 x 400G QSFP-DD Ports, Network Packet Broker (NPB), PicOS®

The FS T8520-32D Network Packet Broker (NPB) is equipped with 32x 400G QSFP-DD ports. The device runs PicOS® under a perpetual license and includes five years of bundled service. It is designed for high-bandwidth monitoring and analysis environments. It supports advanced features such as traffic aggregation, replication, filtering, and load balancing. Additionally, GRE and VXLAN tunneling are included without per-port license fees, thereby enhancing the performance of network analysis and security tools.
