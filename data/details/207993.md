### N5570-48X6C, 48-Port Ethernet Data Center Switch, 48 x 10G RJ45, with 6 x 100G QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Broadcom Trident 3 Chip

The N5570-48X6C is a high-performance leaf switch with perpetual license and 5-year service included. It delivers rich, low-latency Layer 2/Layer 3 features and advanced EVPN-VXLAN capabilities. With 48 native 10G downlink ports and 6 40/100GbE uplink ports, it is ideal for leaf roles in data centers and data center interconnect (DCI) deployments.

Featuring L3 gateway functionality for seamless routing between virtualized and bare-metal servers, the switch is designed for extremely agile data centers that require support for overlay/underlay network architectures.
