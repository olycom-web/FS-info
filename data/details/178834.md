### 24-Port Ethernet L3 PoE++ Switch, 24 x PoE++ Ports @1000W, with 2 x 100Gb QSFP28 uplinks, Support MPLS & MLAG

The S5850-24XMG-U multi-gigabit PoE++ switch features 24x 10/100/1000M/2.5G/5G/10GBASE-T ports and 2x 100Gb uplinks. With 1000W power budget, it efficiently powers attached IP phones, wireless access points, or other standards-compliant PoE, PoE+, and PoE++ end network devices. In addition, the 2x 100G QSFP28 ports, can provide high-speed uplink and server/storage/high-performance workstation connectivity. Offering high performance, high port density, and low latency, S5850-24XMG-U is well-suited to meet the requirements of the next-generation enterprise, Wi-Fi 6 Access, Metro, and HCI (Hyper-Converged Infrastructure) networks. This switch is ideal for aggregation in large-scale campus networks and as a core switch for small to medium-sized enterprise networks.
