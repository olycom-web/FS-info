### S3700-24T4F, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 24x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, Fanless

S3700-24T4F is an enterprise-class access switch with robust hardware, featuring 24x 10/100/1000BASE-T RJ45 ports and 4x 100/1000M uplink ports in a 1U form factor, ideal for branch offices, midsize businesses, and small enterprises. Its fanless design ensures noise-free operation, low cost, and reduced power consumption, making it ideal for quiet, small workspaces.

Designed for reliable, simple and secure access, the switch provides advanced features such as ACL, QinQ, QoS functions, and L2+ static routing, providing enhanced access security, traffic prioritization, and IPv6 support. Additionally, it offers simple device and network management via CLI and an intuitive on-box web UI, while supporting SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced visibility.
