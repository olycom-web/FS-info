### N8610-64DB, 64-Port Ethernet HPC/AI Data Center Switch, 64 x 400Gb QSFP-DD, PicOS®, Broadcom Tomahawk 4 Chip

The N8610-64DB switch supports high-speed, high-density, spine-and-leaf IP fabrics. Use cases include AI data center networking deployments with AI/ML workloads where scalability, performance, and low latency are critical.

The switch offers 400GbE, 200GbE, 100GbE connections and features such as advanced Layer 2/Layer 3 capabilities and secure zero-touch provisioning. The N8610-64DB switch enables network operators to build large, next-generation IP fabrics based on a proven, Internet-scale software suite with best-in-class automation capabilities.
