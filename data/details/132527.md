### S1900-8T, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 Unmanaged Switch, Metal, Fanless, Desktop/Wall-Mount

This cost-effective, plug-and-play L2 unmanaged switch is ideal for SOHO offices, intelligent communities, and parks with limited IT know-how and support.

The S1900-8T is equipped with 8x 10/100/1000M Auto-Negotiation RJ45 ports, all ports support the Auto MDI/MDIX function. Its fanless design ensures noise-free switch operation, while supporting Energy Efficient Ethernet (IEEE 802.3az) standard, which optimizes power use to conserve energy without compromising performance. And the S1900-8T switch can be used as a desktop or wall-mounted switch. The LED on the front panel can dynamically display the operating status of the network.
