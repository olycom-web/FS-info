### N5580-32C, 32-Port L3 Data Center Switch, 32 x 100Gb QSFP28, 2 x 10Gb SFP+, PicOS®, Broadcom Trident 3 Chip, Front-to-Back Airflow

The N5580-32C is a compact 1U switch delivering high-density 40/100GbE for data center spine-leaf networks. Its 100GbE ports can be channelized into 4x 25GbE, and 40GbE ports can be channelized into 4x 10GbE via breakout cables, increasing the total number of 10/25GbE logical ports to 124. Powered by PicOS® with a perpetual license, it supports a rich set of Layer 2/Layer 3 features such as EVPN-VXLAN, MLAG, MPLS, and L3 gateway for leaf/spine roles in data centers and data center interconnect (DCI) deployments.

Manage your switch and fabric with the AmpCon-DC Management Platform, which automates the entire network lifecycle to simplify design, deployment, and operations, reducing complexity and boosting overall efficiency.
