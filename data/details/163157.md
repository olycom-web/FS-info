### S3150-8T2F, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Compact Switch, 8x Gigabit RJ45, with 2x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, Fanless

The S3150-8T2F 8-Port Ethernet L2+ Switch offers an economical, entry-level solution designed for access layer deployments in branches, retail, and workgroup environments. It boasts 8x Gigabit RJ45 and 2x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, comes with an impressive 20 Gbps switching capacity, and a 15 Mpps forwarding rate. Featuring a compact and fanless design, it is ideal for use in quiet work spaces.

Based on the high-performance hardware and FSOS platform, it facilitates essential functions such as QoS, static routing, and IPv6, and supports simple device management and network management via a Command-Line Interface (CLI) as well as an on-box web UI. This switch delivers enhanced network security, network reliability, and operational efficiency for small organizations.
