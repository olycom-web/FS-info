### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 48x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S3410-48TS is a Gigabit Layer 2+ managed switch designed for SMBs, enterprise branches, and campus access networks, delivering secure, reliable, and scalable connectivity. It features 48x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports and 4x 10Gb SFP+ uplinks, providing high-density access and non-blocking forwarding. Built on a Broadcom chip with redundant hot-swappable power supplies and a variable-speed axial fan, it ensures reliable operation. Pre-installed with the [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) system (perpetual license included), it supports MLAG and OSPF with no additional licenses and includes five years of hardware and software support.

Managed by [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), the S3410-48TS simplifies Day 0 onboarding, Day 1 provisioning, and Day 2+ operations through centralized lifecycle management. With Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and end-to-end lifecycle management, AmpCon improves network visibility while simplifying deployment and operations.
