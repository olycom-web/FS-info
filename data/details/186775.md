### S5860-48XMG-U, 48-Port Ethernet L3 PoE++ Switch, 48 x 10GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit Ports, 4 x 25Gb SFP28 Uplinks, with 2 x 40Gb QSFP+ Uplinks, Support Stacking, Broadcom Chip

The FS S5860-48XMG-U multi-gigabit PoE++ switch features 48x 100M/1000M/2.5G/5G/10GBase-T ports, 4x 25Gb SFP28 ports, and 2x 40Gb QSFP+ uplinks. With a 1600W power budget, it efficiently powers Wi-Fi 6 access points and IP cameras. Equipped with a Broadcom BCM56170 chip, redundant hot-swappable power supplies, and smart fans, it ensures high performance and reliability. The switch supports physical stacking of up to 2 units and provides robust IPv4/IPv6 routing features for future scalability. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. FS Airware facilitates remote configuration debugging for cloud-based networks. This switch is ideal for aggregation in large-scale campus networks and as a core switch for small to medium-sized enterprise networks.
