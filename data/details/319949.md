### T5630-64C, 64x 100G QSFP28 Ports, Network Packet Broker (NPB), PicOS®

The FS T5630-64C Network Packet Broker (NPB) is equipped with 64 QSFFP28 ports (100Gbps). The device runs PicOS® under a perpetual license and includes five years of bundled service. It supports high-bandwidth monitoring and analysis with advanced features such as traffic aggregation, replication, filtering, and load balancing, delivering a one-stop visibility solution for on-premise data centers and enterprise campus/brandth networks. Additionally, GRE and VXLAN tunneling are included without per-port license fees, thereby enhancing the performance of network analysis and security tools.

## Software Spotlights

## Pervasive Visibility (图标: https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/2022113017252970eiku.svg)

- Aggregate network traffic to a single or multiple tools (1:1, 1:N, N:1, N:N)
- Support traffic replication and port distribution
- Ingress and egress filter support IPv4, IPv6
- Drop filter support IPv4, IPv6
- 1k filters
- Session/flow aware load balancing
- Configurable hash-based load balancing
- Support OpenFlow/SDN

## Advanced Functionality (图标: https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20221130172533h8j9rj.svg)

- Tunnel Encapsulate GRE, l2GRE and VxLAN
- Tunnel Decapsulate GRE, l2GRE and VxLAN
- Supports jumbo frames
- Support UDF L2, L3 and L4
- Support VLAN tagging and stripping
- Support GRE de-tunneling, VXLAN de-tunneling
