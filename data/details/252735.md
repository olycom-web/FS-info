### S2500L-24T, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 Unmanaged Switch, Fanless, Desktop/Rackmount

The S2500L-24T is a plug-and-play Layer 2 unmanaged switch, offering simple, cost-effective networking for SOHO offices, smart communities, and campuses.

This switch is equipped with 24x 10/100/1000M Auto-Negotiation RJ45 ports, all ports support Auto MDI/MDIX function. Its fanless design ensures noise-free switch operation, while the IEEE 802.3az energy-saving technology adjusts power consumption based on network activity to conserve energy. The switch features professional integrated lightning protection circuit design to prevent damage from lightning strikes and surges, ensuring stable operation.
