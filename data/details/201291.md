### IES3220-8T4F-U, 8-Port Gigabit L2+ Managed Industrial PoE++ Switch, 8 x 60W PoE++ Ports, 360W Budget, with 4 x 1Gb SFP, -40 to 75°C Operating Temperature

The IES3220-8T4F-U is an 8-port Gigabit L2+ managed industrial PoE++ switch built for harsh edge networks that need both data connectivity and simplified power delivery. It provides 8x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE++ ports with up to 60W per port and a 360W PoE budget, plus 4x 1000BASE-X SFP uplinks for flexible fiber access. With an IP40-rated metal enclosure, fanless design, dual power inputs, and a -40 to 75°C operating range, it is designed to deliver reliable performance in manufacturing, mining, transportation, and energy. Support for MRP and ERPS provides network resilience with sub-20ms recovery, while PROFINET, EtherNet/IP, Modbus, and CC-Link passthrough enable seamless integration with PLCs and automation systems, significantly reducing deployment complexity and maintenance costs.
