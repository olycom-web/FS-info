### S5850-48S8C, 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48x 10Gb SFP+, with 8x 40G/100G QSFP28, Support MPLS&MLAG

The S5850-48S8C 48-port 10Gb Ethernet layer 3 switch features 48x 10G downlinks and 8x 40G/100G QSFP28 (can be spilt into 4x 10G/25G) uplinks. This managed enterprise switch can deliver 2.56 Tbps switching capacity and 1905 Mpps forwarding rate. Redundant hot-swappable power supplies and intelligent fans provide continuous operation and efficient cooling.

MLAG and stacking enable link-level redundancy and load balancing, keeping critical services online while simplifying network expansion. Ideal for campus aggregation, remote offices, and WAN extension requiring high-speed, long-distance connectivity.
