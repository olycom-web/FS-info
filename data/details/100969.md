### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 48x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 10Gb SFP+, Support MLAG

The S5800-48T4S is a high-performance, secure, and easy-to-manage enterprise-grade Gigabit access switch with 48x 10/100/1000BASE-T RJ45 access ports and 4x 10Gb uplink ports for flexible network deployment. It offers comprehensive Layer 2/3 capabilities, including MLAG/stacking, [MPLS & VXLAN-BGP-EVPN](https://www.fs.com/products/100590.html), and [PTP](https://www.fs.com/products/100590.html), along with MACsec for point-to-point link encryption to ensure scalable performance and robust security for modern networks.

The S5800-48T4S is equipped with hot-swappable, redundant power supplies and smart fans for enhanced resiliency. It supports simple device management and network management via a Command-Line Interface (CLI) as well as an on-box web UI. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. It is ideal for high-density access deployments in branch offices, campuses, data centers, and small to medium-sized enterprises.
