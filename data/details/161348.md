### IES5100-24TS-P, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Industrial PoE++ Switch, 8 x PoE++, 12 x PoE+ Ports @720W, with 4 x 1Gb RJ45/SFP Combo, 8 x 1Gb SFP and 4 x 10Gb SFP+ Ports

The IES5100-24TS-P features 8x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE++ ports, 12x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE+ ports, 4 x 1G PoE+ RJ45/SFP combo ports, 8x 1G SFP ports, 4 x 10G SFP+ ports, which is designed especially for a broad spectrum of industrial and extended enterprise connectivity needs. All Gigabit PoE ports support a power budget of up to 720W for PoE/PoE+/PoE++, it is ideal for connecting PoE-powered end devices such as IP cameras, phones, wireless access points, sensors, and more. Equipped with an IP40 rated metal enclosure and fanless design, it can operate within a wide temperature range of -40 to 75°C (-40 to 167°F). This switch is ideal for harsh environments, and can be widely used in energy, transportation, industrial production, environmental protection, and industrial industry. This layer 2+ industrial managed switch supports Layer 3 static routing, RIP/OSPF dynamic routing, and VRRP virtual routing, ensuring continuous network operation with optimized routing and gateway redundancy.
