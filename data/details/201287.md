### IES3220-4T2F, 4-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ managed Industrial Switch, 4 x 10/100/1000BASE-T, with 2 x 1Gb SFP

The IES3220-4T2F features 4x 10/100/1000BASE-T copper ports, 2x 1000BASE-X SFP ports, which is designed especially for a broad spectrum of industrial and extended enterprise connectivity needs. Equipped with an IP41 rated metal enclosure and fanless design, it can operate within a wide temperature range of -40 to 75°C (-40 to 167°F). This switch is ideal for harsh environments, and can be widely used in public transport, traffic management, smart buildings, and other extreme temperature scenarios. This layer 2+ industrial managed switch supports multiple redundancy protocols, ERPS and MRP, ensuring sub-20ms convergence. It also supports industrial protocols such as Profinet, Ethernet/IP (CIP), Modbus, and CC-Link, enabling fast integration into existing industrial networks while reducing deployment complexity and maintenance costs.
