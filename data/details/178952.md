### S3950-4T12S-R, 12-Port Ethernet L2+ Managed Switch, 12x 10Gb SFP+, with 4x Gigabit RJ45, Support Stacking/MLAG

The S3950-4T12S-R is a Layer 2+ switch with wire-speed 12x 10Gb SFP+ and 4x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports in a compact 1U platform, delivering up to 248 Gbps switching capacity and 184.5 Mpps forwarding rate. It supports comprehensive Layer 2 features such as LACP, MLAG/stacking, and ARP, along with basic Layer 3 capabilities including VRRP, RIP, and static routing. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. With a rich set of security and management features, it is ideal for large-scale campus access, small to medium-sized networks, and branch office aggregations.
