### 48-Port Ethernet L3 PoE++ Switch, 48x 5GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit, 4x 25Gb SFP28, with 2x 40Gb QSFP+ uplinks, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip, PicOS®

S5860-48MG-U is a next-gen L3 switch featuring 48x 100M/1000M/2.5G/5GBase-T multi-gigabit ports, up to 90W PoE++ on ports 1–24 and up to 30W PoE+ on ports 25–48, with 4x 25G SFP28 and 2x 40G QSFP+ uplinks. Ideal for enterprise campuses, hotels, and retail, it supports high-density PoE deployments for Wi-Fi 6E access points and other devices, with multi-gigabit access, high-power PoE, and high-speed uplinks. Perpetual & Fast PoE with power protection keeps devices powered during reboots.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports OpenFlow, MLAG, LACP, VRRP, VLAN and DHCP Relay without extra licenses, including five years of hardware and software support.

Managed by the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html), enabling end-to-end campus networks automation. It offers automated ZTP, real-time telemetry monitoring, topology auto-discovery and lifecycle management, delivering centralized visibility and simplified wired and wireless operations for reliable, future-ready enterprise networks.
