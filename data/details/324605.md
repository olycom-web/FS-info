### IES5200W-8U2F2SD, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Managed Industrial PoE++ Switch, 8 x PoE++ Ports @360W, with 2 x 1/2.5Gb SFP, 2 x 10Gb SFP+

The IES5200W-8U2F2SD industrial switch is equipped with 8x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE++ ports, 2 x 1/2.5G SFP ports, and 2 x 10G SFP+ ports, specifically designed for heavy industrial applications. With each PoE++ port delivering up to 90W power (360W total budget), this switch reliably powers high-demand devices including remote digital signage displays, PoE lighting systems, and thin clients. Featuring a rugged, fanless design with IP30-rated metal housing, it operates reliably in extreme temperatures from -40°C to 75°C. Its robust construction makes it ideal for harsh environments such as rail transit systems, smart manufacturing facilities, factory automation, and other extreme-temperature scenarios.

This Layer 3 managed industrial switch supports L3 routing functions RIP and OSPF, which can facilitate the deployment of applications across networks, making them ideal for flexible PoE in heavy-industrial and railway wayside access networks.
