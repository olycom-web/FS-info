### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48 x 25Gb SFP28, 8 x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5580L-48Y is a Layer 3 switch for enterprise campus distribution and aggregation. Built on Broadcom Trident 3 silicon, it provides 25GbE and 100GbE (split into 4× 10/25G via breakout cables) ports in a compact 1U form factor. It supports power-efficient and low-noise operation, while redundant hot-swappable power supplies and smart fans enhance high availability.Running PicOS® with a perpetual license, the switch supports MLAG, BGP, OSPF, PBR, and ACLs without additional licensing and includes five years of hardware and software support. Ideal for midsize to large campus aggregation and small campus core deployments.The switch is managed through the AmpCon Management Software for Campus Networks, enabling centralized management and automated lifecycle operations from Day 0 to Day 2+. It provides Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), automated topology discovery, and real-time telemetry to simplify campus management and improve operational efficiency.
