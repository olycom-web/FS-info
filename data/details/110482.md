### NC8200-4TD, 4-Slot 2U Ethernet L3 Data Center Chassis Switch Unloaded, Supports 4 x 25/40/100Gb Line Cards, Support MLAG/Stacking, Broadcom Chip

The NC8200-4TD switch supports a maximum of 96x 25G with 8x 100G, 64x 40G, or 32x 100G high-density full line rate ports through flexible line cards combinations of NC8200-8C, NC8200-16Q and NC8200-24BC. The NC8200-4TD can operate as leaf and spine switch for medium-sized data centers and cloud-computing data centers deployments.

The switch employs an advanced cache scheduling mechanism to maximize the device's cache capability. With RoCEv2, PFC and ECN to implement the low-latency, zero packet loss, high throughput and service forwarding rate, ensuring non-blocking transmission in the increasingly demanding data center environment.
