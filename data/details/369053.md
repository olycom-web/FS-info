### S5440-48MU, 48-Port Ethernet L3 PoE++ Switch, 40 x 2.5G, 8 x 10GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit, with 4 x 25Gb SFP28 and 2 x 40Gb QSFP+ Uplinks, Support VXLAN & MLAG

The S5440-48MU features 40x 2.5Gb PoE++, 8x 10Gb PoE++ access ports and 4x 10G/25G SFP28, 2x 40G QSFP+ uplink ports. It provides a total PoE power budget of 2000W, with up to 90W available per port. The switch delivers advanced Layer 2/3 features, including MLAG and BGP, as well as EVPN-VXLAN for scalable overlay networking, and integrates MACsec for line-rate point-to-point encryption, ensuring both performance and security.

It supports Web, CLI, SNMP, and SSH management interfaces, simplifying operations while enhancing visibility and control. With high performance, scalability, and ease of management, the S5440-48MU is ideal for large campus access networks and small to medium-sized enterprise deployments.
