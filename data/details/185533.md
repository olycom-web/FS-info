### 12-Port Ethernet L3 Managed Switch, 12x 10Gb SFP+, Support Stacking

The S5300-12S Ethernet layer 3 switch features 12x 10Gb SFP+. It also supports strong ACL, selective QinQ, 1:1 and N:1 VLAN switching, Ethernet OAM, carrier QoS, and the industrial 10G Ethernet ring network. These functions make the product meet the need of various complex situations.

FS S5300-12S is a new generation of full gigabit smart switch for IP metropolitan area networks, government and enterprise networks, and can be used for scenarios that require high-speed transmission and long-distance connectivity, such as data centers, remote offices and WAN extensions.
