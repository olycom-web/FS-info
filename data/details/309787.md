### N8610-32D, 32-Port Ethernet HPC/AI Data Center Switch, 32 x 400Gb QSFP-DD, PicOS®, Broadcom Tomahawk 4 Chip

N8610-32D switch is a high density and power efficient fixed configuration data center switch, delivering wirespeed layer 2 and layer 3 with flexible resources, robust traffic management and load balancing features, optimized for AI generated content lossless network.

N8610-32D switch is designed for multiple deployment roles. As the data center leaf, it provides 100G and 400G connectivity, addressing today's high end storage and HPC requirements as well as future server connectivity. Implemented at the spine, the N8610-32D switch provides up to 32 ports of 400G in a single rack unit, enabling efficient high radix clusters that maximize 12.8 Tbps bandwidth performance of compute and storage by eliminating bottlenecks between leaf and spine tiers. All ports support 2x 200GbE, 4x 100GbE, up to 128 interfaces.
