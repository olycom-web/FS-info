### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48x 2.5Gb RJ45/Multi-Gigabit, with 4x 25Gb SFP28 and 2x 40Gb QSFP+ Uplinks, Support EVPN-VXLAN&MLAG

The S5440L-48M is a compact 1U multi-gigabit switch featuring 48 flexible 100M/1G/2.5G Base-T RJ45 access ports and built-in wirespeed 10/25/40G uplinks, delivering high-speed access and efficient core aggregation. It offers comprehensive Layer 2/3 capabilities, including EVPN, VXLAN, BGP, and MLAG, along with MACsec for point-to-point link encryption to ensure scalable performance and security for modern networks.

The switch supports WEB, CLI, SNMP, and SSH management interfaces, simplifying operations while enhancing visibility and control. With high performance, scalability, and ease of management, the S5440L-48M is ideal for enterprise campuses, branch offices, and data center top-of-rack deployments.
