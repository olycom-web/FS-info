### 16-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 16x 1Gb RJ45, with 2x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip, Fanless

The S3410C-16TF is a Gigabit Layer 2+ managed switch designed for education, business, healthcare, and office park networks. It features 16x 10/100/1000BASE-T RJ45 ports and 2x 1Gb SFP uplinks, providing high-density access and non-blocking forwarding. Powered by Broadcom chips, it delivers 36 Gbps switching capacity and a 26.8 Mpps forwarding rate. Its fanless design enables silent operation, lower power consumption, and reduced operating costs. Pre-installed with the [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) system (perpetual license included), it supports MLAG and OSPF with no additional licenses and includes five years of hardware and software support.

The switch is managed through the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), enabling Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and end-to-end lifecycle management from Day 0 to Day 2. AmpCon simplifies switch onboarding, configuration, monitoring, and ongoing management, improving network visibility and operational efficiency.
