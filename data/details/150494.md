### S5850-24S2C, 24-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 24x 10Gb SFP+, with 2x 100Gb QSFP28, Support MPLS&MLAG

S5850-24S2C is a high-performance Layer 3 Ethernet switch with 24x 1/10Gb SFP+ and 2x 40/100Gb QSFP28 ports in a compact 1U form factor. The switch comes with complete system software, comprehensive protocols and applications to facilitate the rapid service deployment and management for traditional Layer 2 and Layer 3 networks. It is ideally designed for demanding workloads.

Supporting advanced features, including MLAG/stacking, EVPN, SR-MPLS, VXLAN, IPv4/IPv6, sFlow, SNMP etc., FS S5850-24S2C meets the requirements of next generation metro and enterprise network of high-speed transmission and long-distance connectivity, and is ieal for scenarios such as remote office interconnection and WAN extension.
