### S2800S-48T4F, 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Smart Managed Switch, 48 x Gigabit RJ45, with 4 x 1Gb SFP Uplinks

S2800S‑48T4F is a Gigabit Layer 2+ smart managed switch for retail stores, small and medium‑sized offices, and campus access networks. It delivers high‑density edge connectivity with 48× 10/100/1000BASE‑T RJ45 ports and 4× 1G SFP uplinks, making it easy to extend networks and build flexible copper‑to‑fiber topologies.

With an intuitive web‑based interface plus CLI and SNMP, the switch simplifies daily configuration and troubleshooting. Security features such as ACL, IP‑MAC‑Port‑VID binding, port security and DHCP snooping help block unauthorized access. Backed by 104 Gbps switching capacity, 77.38 Mpps forwarding rate and enhanced L2+ functions including VLAN, QoS, LACP and IGMP, S2800S‑48T4F delivers reliable performance for converged data, voice and video services.
