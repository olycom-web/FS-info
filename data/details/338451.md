### 12-Port Ethernet L3 Managed Switch, 12 x 10Gb SFP+, with 4 x Gigabit RJ45, PicOS®, Support MLAG

The S5440-12S is a L3 switch with wire-speed 12x 10Gb SFP+ and 4x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports in a compact 1U platform, delivering up to 248 Gbps switching capacity and 184.5 Mpps forwarding rate. Running [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG, BGP, OSPF, SSH, ACLs, PBR, and sFlow without requiring additional licenses and includes five years of hardware and software support. Ideal for large-scale campus access, small to medium-sized networks, and branch office aggregations.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus management platform](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html) (Support for this model is available from AmpCon-Campus V3.0.0), offering Day 0 to Day 2+ automated lifecycle management with Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology discovery. AmpCon-Campus automates workflows, simplifies operations, and enhances visibility into the performance of connected devices.
