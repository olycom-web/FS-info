### 10-Port Ethernet L3 Managed Switch, 10 x 10Gb SFP+, with 2 x Gigabit RJ45, Support Stacking

The S3260-10S is a compact 1U Layer 3 switch featuring 10×10Gb SFP+ and 2×10/100/1000BASE-T ports, delivering wire-speed performance with up to 204 Gbps switching capacity and 153 Mpps forwarding rate. It supports advanced Layer 2/3 protocols including Stacking, BGP, OSPF, and LACP, along with comprehensive security and management features, making it ideal for large-scale campus access, small to medium-sized networks, and branch office aggregations.
