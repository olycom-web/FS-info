### N8650-64OD, 64-Port Ethernet HPC/AI Data Center Switch, 64 x 800Gb OSFP, PicOS®, Broadcom Tomahawk 5 Chip

The N8650-64OD leverages Broadcom Tomahawk 5 silicon to provide 800GbE—twice the capacity of the previous generation, meeting the advanced AI data center networking requirements of large-scale clusters. It works with AmpCon Management Software to assure daily operation in AI and ML workload training and access.

The switch delivers high-density 800GbE ports in a 2U fixed form factor with PicOS® to provide advanced network services tuned to the specific needs of AI/ML workloads, and its 1us (64B) latency ensures fast job completion time (JCT) to speed training through high GPU utilization.
