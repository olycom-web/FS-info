### IES3100-16TM, 16-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 Managed Industrial Switch, 16 x 10/100/1000BASE-T, with 4 x 1G/2.5G SFP

The IES3100-16TM features 16 x 10/100/1000BASE-T, with 4 x 1G/2.5G SFP, which is designed especially for a broad spectrum of industrial and extended enterprise connectivity needs. It adopts aluminum profile and aluminum alloy composite shell, which is corrosion-resistant, oxidation-resistant and never rusty. The protection level is not lower than IP40. The working temperature is -40 to 85°C (-40 to 185°F), and it supports relay alarm. This switch is ideal for harsh environments, and can be widely used in Industrial control, road and bridge, rail transit, electric power, energy, chemical industry, industrial communication. This layer 2 industrial managed switch supports enhanced L2+ functions such as 802.1Q VLAN and port-based VLAN, QoS, QinQ, LACP link aggregation, etc., which can meet the requirements of many scenarios in industrial environments.
