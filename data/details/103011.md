### S3260-16T4FP, 16-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 16 x PoE+ Ports @250W, with 2 x 1Gb SFP Uplinks and 2 x Combo SFP Ports

16-Port Managed PoE+ Switch is equipped with 16x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports, 2x RJ45/SFP combo ports and 2x SFP uplinks, 16x RJ45 ports support both IEEE 802.3af PoE and IEEE 802.3at PoE+ (up to 30W per port) for powering attached IP phones, wireless access points, or other standards-compliant PoE and PoE+ end network devices. 2x SFP uplink ports are provided to support connections to higher layer devices. 2x RJ45/SFP combo ports meet the different network expansion needs.

This access switch delivers a compact, cost-effective solution for carrier's IP MAN, campus and small business networks. Based on the high-performance hardware and FSOS platform, it supports functions such as ACL, QinQ and QoS. It also simplifies device and network management with both a Command-Line Interface (CLI) and a web UI. With its ability to enhance network security, reliability, and operational efficiency, this switch is an ideal choice for small organizations.
