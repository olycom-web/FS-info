### 24-Port Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 24 x PoE+ Ports @370W, with 4 x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, Broadcom Chip, PicOS®

S3410L-24TF-P, a next-gen switch featuring 24x 10/100/1000Mbps PoE ports and 4x 1Gb SFP uplinks ports. It delivers a compact, cost-effective solution for access-layer deployments in branches, retail, campus networks and enterprise edge, facilitating seamless interconnection of security cameras and Wi-Fi 6 access points.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG, OSPF, RIP and QoS without requiring additional licenses, and includes five years of hardware and software support.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus Management Platform](https://www.fs.com/products/258683.html?now_cid=5513), which offers automated Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and automated lifecycle management with 1, 3, or 5 years of service. AmpCon-Campus automates routine workflows, minimizing downtime and manual interventions to enable efficient orchestration and management of highly-available networks at scale.
