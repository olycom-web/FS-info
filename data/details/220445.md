### S3250-16TF-U, 16-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 PoE++ Switch, 16 x PoE+ Ports @240W, with 2 x 1Gb Combo Uplinks

S3250-16TF-U 16-port managed PoE+ switch is equipped 4x 10/100/1000MBASE-T PoE++ ports, 12x 10/100/1000MBASE-T PoE+ ports, with 2x 1Gb combo ports, which can be used to link to higher bandwidth upstream equipment. It provides a max power budget of 240W. 4 ports (13-16 Ports) support both IEEE 802.3af/at/bt (up to 90W per port) and 12 ports support both IEEE 802.3af/at (up to 30W per port) for powering attached IP phones, wireless access points, or other standards-compliant PoE, PoE+ and PoE++ end network devices.

With high switching performance and reliability, the product features a large cache and low transmission delay, which supports access to Ultra HD video.
