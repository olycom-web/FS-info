### 16-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 16 x 40Gb QSFP+, 8 x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG

The S5470-16Q is an Ethernet L3 switch of 16x 40Gb QSFP+ and 8x 100Gb QSFP28 ports which can be split to 4x 10G and 4x 10/25G respectively, offering line-rate switching across all ports and is loaded with hot-swappable and redundant power supplies and fans for enhanced reliability. Running [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports OpenFlow, MLAG, BGP, OSPF without requiring additional licenses and includes five years of hardware and software support. Ideal for large campus high-performance and high-speed distribution, SMB core networks, and low-density data center ToR deployments, the Switch delivers enterprise-grade performance and scalability.

Managed by the [AmpCon-Campus management platform](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html), the S5470-16Q enables Day 0 to Day 2+ automated lifecycle management with Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology discovery, workflow automation, and real-time telemetry, helping simplify operations, accelerate deployment, and improve network visibility.
