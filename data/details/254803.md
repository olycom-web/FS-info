### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48 x 10GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit, with 8 x 100Gb QSFP28, Support MLAG&EVPN-VXLAN

The FS S5850-48XMG8C is a high-performance Layer 3 switch designed for next-generation campus and enterprise networks. It features 48x 100M/1G/2.5G/5G/10GBASE-T Multi-Gigabit ports and 8x 100G QSFP28 uplinks, delivering up to 2.56 Tbps switching capacity and 1904 Mpps forwarding performance for high-density Wi-Fi 6/6E/7 deployments.

Built with 1+1 hot-swappable redundant power supplies and 4 smart hot-swappable fans ensures high availability and optimal cooling. Advanced Layer 2/Layer 3 features, MLAG, and EVPN-VXLAN support enable scalable, secure, and highly available network architectures for enterprise campus aggregation/access and data center Top-of-Rack deployments.
