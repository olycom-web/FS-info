### 8-Port Ethernet L2+ PoE++ Switch, 8 x 2.5GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit, with 4 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks

The S3260-8MU is a L2+ managed switch, equipped with 8x 2.5Gb RJ45 and 4x 10Gb SFP+ uplink ports support both IEEE 802.3at PoE+ and IEEE 802.3bt PoE++ (up to 90W per port) for powering attached Wi-Fi 6/Wi-Fi 6E access points, IP cameras or other network devices.

The switch features intelligent fan speed control scheme and silent design to create a green and energy-saving network. It provides secure desktop connectivity with enhanced Layer 2 functions and simplified management, delivering a flexible and cost-effective solution for small business.
