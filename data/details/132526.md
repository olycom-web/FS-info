### S1900-5T, 5-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 Unmanaged Switch, Metal, Fanless, Desktop/Wall-Mount

This cost-effective, plug-and-play L2 unmanaged switch is ideal for SOHO offices, intelligent communities, and parks with limited IT know-how and support.

The S1900-5T is equipped with 5x 10/100/1000M Auto-Negotiation RJ45 ports, all ports support the Auto MDI/MDIX function. The switch supports both desktop and wall-mount installation. Its compact metal housing and fanless design enable virtually anywhere placement with silent operation. Additionally, the switch automatically reduces power consumption on unconnected or inactive ports, which saves energy. The front-panel LEDs provide real-time network status indication, making this desktop switch ideal for both home and office deployments.
