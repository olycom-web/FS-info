### S3260-24M, 24-Port Ethernet L2+ Switch, 24x 100M/1G/2.5GBase-T/Multi-Gigabit, with 6x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Stackable Switch

The S3260-24M 24-Port Ethernet L2+ Switch is equipped with 24x 100M/1G/2.5G multi-rate RJ45 ports, and 6x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks. This switch delivers 240 Gbps switching capacity and 180 Mpps forwarding rate. Low-noise fan design (<40dB) enhances user comfort in quiet environments. Based on the high-performance hardware, it supports functions such as Stacking, OSPF, Voice VLAN and QoS, and supports simple device management and network management via a Command-Line Interface (CLI) as well as an on-box web UI. With these features, the switch provides a cost-effective solution for enterprise campuses, hotels, education institutions, and internet cafes.
