### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 48x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S3410L-48TF is a gigabit Layer 2+ managed switch designed for flexible deployment across various networking environments, such as SMB, enterprise, midmarket, and branch office networks. It features 48x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports and 4x 1Gb SFP uplinks, ensuring high-density access and non-blocking transmission. Equipped with advanced Broadcom chips, the switch delivers a 104 Gbps switching capacity and a 78 Mpps forwarding rate, ensuring superior network performance. Running on PicOS® with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG and OSPF without requiring additional licenses, and includes five years of hardware and software support.

PicOS® enterprise switches are managed by the [AmpCon-Campus Management Platform](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html), which offers automated Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and end-to-end lifecycle management from Day 0 to Day 2. With AmpCon-Campus, the PicOS® enterprise switches can be onboarded, configured, and managed effortlessly and seamlessly.
