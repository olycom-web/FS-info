### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 24x Gigabit RJ45, with 8x 10Gb SFP+, Support MLAG/Stacking

The S5800-24T8S is a high-performance, highly secure, and easily managed enterprise-grade gigabit access switch. It features 24 10/100/1000BASE-T RJ45 access ports and 8 10Gb uplink ports, meeting the needs of multi-service support and high-density access. It provides comprehensive Layer 2/3 functionality, including MLAG/stacking, [MPLS & VXLAN-BGP-EVPN, PTP](https://www.fs.com/products/100590.html), and comprehensive security policies to ensure scalable performance and security for modern networks.

The S5800 Series switches provide uncompromising simplicity, ensuring security, constant availability, and streamlined IT management. It supports simple device management and network management via a Command-Line Interface (CLI) as well as an on-box web UI, making it ideal for branch offices, campus networks, data centers, and SMB network deployments.
