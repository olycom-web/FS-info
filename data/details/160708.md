### S3100-8TMS-P, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 6 x PoE+ Ports, 2 x 5Gb PoE+ Ports@125W, 2 x 1Gb RJ45, with 2 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Broadcom Chip, Fanless

The S3100-8TMS-P is a high-performance Layer 2+ managed switch designed to provide reliable and effective networking for educational, business, and hospital environments. Its comprehensive port selection includes 6x 1G PoE+ ports and 2x 5G PoE+ ports (30W max per port) that deliver power to standards-compliant powered devices, plus 2x 10G SFP+ uplinks to accommodate future network expansion demands. This flexible configuration ensures support for a wide range of network applications.

The intuitive Airware management simplifies network deployment and operation, while robust security features protect critical transactions. Advanced L2+ functionality, such as Voice VLAN, LACP, and QoS, enhances network performance and ensures reliable connectivity for fast file transfers and data processing. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. This improves uptime and workforce productivity.
