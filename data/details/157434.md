### S3200-8MG4S, 8-Port Ethernet L2+ Switch, 8x Multi-Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks

The S3200-8MG4S 8-Port Ethernet L2+ Switch has 8x Auto-negotiation 100M/1000M/2.5G Multi-Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks. It can increase the 1G speed by 2.5 times, relying on existing wiring (Cat5e or Cat6). This managed enterprise switch delivers 120 Gbps switching capacity and 89.28 Mpps forwarding rate. It offers powerful QoS, Voice VLAN, LACP, OSPF, and RIP functions for superior processing performance and network reliability and supports simple device management and network management via a Command-Line Interface (CLI) as well as an on-box web UI. With these features, S3200-8MG4S switch provides cost-effective and flexible Layer 2+ static routing solutions for next generation HOME, SMB, Enterprise, Wi-Fi 6 AX Access, high-speed NAS, and 2.5G LAN networks.
