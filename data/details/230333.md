### S2805S-8TF, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 Smart Managed Switch, 8x Gigabit RJ45, with 2x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, Fanless

S2805S-8TF features 8x 10/100/1000MBASE-T ports and 2x 1Gb SFP uplinks. These SFP ports meet the network expand needs, providing improved sustainability and a borderless network experience. Its fanless design ensures silent operation, lower costs, and reduced power consumption. The user-friendly web interface streamlines operations, enhancing efficiency, while robust security safeguards critical transactions.
With a switching capacity of 20 Gbps and a forwarding rate of 14.88 Mpps, this switch offers enhanced L2 functions like VLAN, QoS, LACP and IGMP to bolster network performance. This high-performance L2 smart managed switch is ideal for retail, small business, and campus networks.
