### S3250-24TF-U, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 PoE++ Switch，24 x PoE+ Ports @370W，with 2 x 1Gb Combo Uplinks

S3250-24TF-U 24-port managed PoE+ switch is equipped 4x 10/100/1000MBASE-T PoE++ ports, 20x 10/100/1000MBASE-T PoE+ ports, with 2x 1Gb combo ports, which can be used to link to higher bandwidth upstream equipment. It provides a max power budget of 370W. 4 ports (21-24 ports) support both IEEE 802.3af/at/bt (up to 90W per port) and 20 ports support both IEEE 802.3af/at (up to 30W per port) for powering attached IP phones, wireless access points, or other standards-compliant PoE, PoE+ and PoE++ end network devices.With high switching performance and reliability, the product features a large cache and low transmission delay, which supports access to Ultra HD video.
