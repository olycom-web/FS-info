### N5860-48SC, 48-Port Ethernet L3 Data Center Switch, 48 x 10Gb SFP+, with 8 x 100Gb QSFP28, Support MLAG/Stacking, Broadcom Chip, Front-to-Back Airflow

N5860-48SC is a high density switch in a compact 1U form factor with full line rate 48 1/10GbE and 8 40/100GbE ports. It adopts Broadcom chips to deliver 1.28 Tbps switching capacity and 1920 Mpps forwarding rate.It is packed with redundant hot-swappable power supplies and smart fans for network reliability.

Comprehensive Layer 2 and Layer 3 capabilities with support for stacking, MLAG, and EVPN-VXLAN make it ideal for data centers of all sizes and cloud computing services.
