### IES3110-8TF-R, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Industrial Switch, 8 x 10/100/1000BASE-T, with 2 x 1Gb SFP, -40 to 75°C Operating Temperature

The IES3110-8TF-R features 8x 10/100/1000BASE-T copper ports, 2x 100/1000BASE-X SFP ports, which is designed especially for a broad spectrum of industrial and extended enterprise connectivity needs. Equipped with an IP40 rated metal enclosure and fanless design, it can operate within a wide temperature range of -40 to 75°C (-40 to 167°F ).This switch is ideal for harsh environments, such as distribution centers, warehouses, and outdoor places with extremely low or high temperatures.

This layer 2 industrial managed switch supports enhanced L2+ functions such as ERPS, VLAN, QoS, IGMP Snooping, LACP link aggregation, Static Routing, etc, making it well-suited for a wide range of industrial scenarios.
