### S2805S-24TF-P, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 Smart Managed PoE+ Switch, 24 x PoE+ Ports @370W, with 4 x 1Gb SFP Uplinks

S2805S-24TF-P is a 24-Port Gigabit Layer 2 Smart Managed PoE+ Switch for retail, small business, and campus access deployments. It provides 24x 10/100/1000M RJ45 ports and 4x 1G SFP uplinks, with up to 30W PoE+ per port and a 370W total power budget, delivering stable power and connectivity for IP phones, cameras, and wireless APs.

Featuring an intuitive web management interface, integrated security, and essential Layer 2 capabilities such as VLAN, LACP, and QoS, it enables efficient traffic control, improved network performance, and reliable operation in cost-sensitive environments.
