### N8520-32D, 32-Port Ethernet Data Center Switch, 32 x 400Gb QSFP-DD, PicOS®, Broadcom Trident 4 Chip

N8520-32D is a 400GbE 1U switch based on Broadcom Trident 4 silicon. Delivering ultra-high density with up to 128x 100GbE ports, it simplifies networks and lowers TCO by flattening multi-tier designs. Its flexible 100GbE, 200GbE, and 400GbE support protects investments while enabling future-proofing. Powered by unified PicOS®, it features core capabilities like EVPN-VXLAN, MLAG, and RoCEv2, ensuring consistent operations.

[AmpCon](https://www.fs.com/products/283015.html) provides full Day 0–2+ lifecycle automation with telemetry and topology auto-discovery. This powerful management makes it ideal for data centers, DCI, campus cores, and firewall cluster-to-fabric connections handling extreme workloads and non-bandwidth-constrained scenarios.
