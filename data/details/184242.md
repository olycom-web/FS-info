### S5850-24B4C, 24-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 24 x 25Gb SFP28, with 4 x 100Gb QSFP28, Support MPLS&MLAG

The S5850-24B4C is a layer 3 switch with wire-speed 24x 10G/25G SFP28 and 4x 40G/100G QSFP28 (split to 4x 10G/25G) ports, delivering 2 Tbps switching capacity and 1488 Mpps forwarding rate. It features redundant hot-swappable power supplies, smart fans, and dual-flash chips for enhanced reliability and performance.

With support for MLAG, BGP EVPN VXLAN, PTP v2, MPLS, LACP, and more, the switch meets the scalability, security, and application demands of modern networks. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. It is ideal for next-generation core, aggregation, and data center environments, as well as medium to large campus deployments, offering the flexibility of enterprise top-of-rack and service provider environments with seamless migration from 10GbE to 25GbE or from 10GbE/40GbE uplinks to 100GbE.
