### S5860-48XMG-U, 48-Port Ethernet L3 PoE++ Switch, 48 x 10GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit Ports, 4 x 25Gb SFP28 Uplinks, with 2 x 40Gb QSFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG & Perpetual PoE, Broadcom Chip

S5860-48XMG-U is a next-gen L3 switch featuring 48x 100M/1G/2.5G/5G/10GBase-T ports of multi-gigabit Ethernet, up to 90W PoE++ on ports 1–24 and up to 30W PoE+ on ports 25–48, with 4x 25Gb SFP28 ports, and 2x 40Gb QSFP+ uplinks. Ideal for enterprise campuses, hotels and retail scenarios, it delivers multi-gigabit access and high-density PoE to support Wi-Fi 7 APs and other powered devices. Perpetual & Fast PoE with power protection keeps devices powered during reboots for stable operation.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports static routing, OSPF, RIP, QoS, MLAG, LACP, VRRP and VLAN without extra licenses, including 5-year hardware and software support.

Managed by the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258683.html?now_cid=5513), it enables automated ZTP, real-time telemetry, topology auto-discovery, and lifecycle management, delivering centralized visibility and simplified wired and wireless operations for reliable, future-ready enterprise networks.
