### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 48x PoE+ Ports @740W, with 2x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks and 2x Combo SFP Ports, Broadcom Chip, PicOS®

S3410-48TS-P 48-port managed PoE+ switch is equipped with 48x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports, 2x 1Gb RJ45/SFP combo ports and 2x 1/10Gb SFP+ uplinks. It offers a compact and cost-effective solution for campus networks, SMBs, and enterprise networks, facilitating seamless interconnection of security cameras and Wi-Fi 6 access points.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG, Voice VLAN, OSPF and sFlow without requiring additional licenses, and includes five years of hardware and software support.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus Management Platform](https://www.fs.com/products/258683.html?now_cid=5513), which offers automated Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and automated lifecycle management with 1, 3, or 5 years of service. AmpCon-Campus automates routine workflows, minimizing downtime and manual interventions to enable efficient orchestration and management of highly-available networks at scale.
