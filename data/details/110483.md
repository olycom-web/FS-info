### 8-Port 100Gb QSFP28 Line Card for Data Center Switch NC8200-4TD

The NC8200-8C Ethernet line card supports 8x 100G QSFP28, offering line-rate forwarding from all ports. The QSFP28 ports are backward compatible with QSFP+ modules.
