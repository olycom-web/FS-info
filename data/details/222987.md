### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 48x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 1/2.5Gb SFP Uplinks, PicOS®, Broadcom Chip

The S3270-48TM is a high-performance L2+ switch well-suited for connecting to more terminals in HD monitoring scenarios and for better coping with burst data. It features 48x 10/100/1000M RJ45 ports and 4x 2.5Gb SFP uplinks, ensuring high-density access and non-blocking transmission. Powered by an advanced Broadcom chip, it delivers 116 Gbps switching capacity and 86.30 Mpps forwarding rate. Running on PicOS® with a perpetual license, it support OSPF without requiring additional licenses, and includes five years of hardware and software support.

PicOS® enterprise switches are managed by the [AmpCon-Campus](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html) Management Platform, which offers automated Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and end-to-end lifecycle management from Day 0 to Day 2. With AmpCon-Campus, the PicOS® enterprise switches can be onboarded, configured, and managed effortlessly and seamlessly.
