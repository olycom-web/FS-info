### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 24 x Gigabit RJ45, with 8 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG

The S5440-24T is a high-performance next-gen L3 switch designed to deliver a cost-effective and efficient solution for access layer deployments in campus networks, SMBs, branch offices, and distributed sites. It offers 208 Gbps of switching capacity and 155 Mpps of forwarding performance, with 24x Gigabit ports and 8x 10G Uplinks, ensuring high-density access and non-blocking transmission. Pre-installed with the PicOS® system (with a perpetual license), the switch supports L2/L3 and SDN features like MLAG and NETCONF, without requiring additional licenses, and includes five years of free hardware and software technical support.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus management platform](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html), which offers automated Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), real-time telemetry monitoring, topology auto-discovery, and end-to-end lifecycle management from Day 0 to Day 2. With AmpCon-Campus, PicOS® enterprise switches can be onboarded, configured, and managed effortlessly and seamlessly.
