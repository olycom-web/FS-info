### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48 x 25Gb SFP28, 8 x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Support EVPN-VXLAN&MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5580-48Y is a Layer 3 switch for enterprise campus distribution and aggregation. Built on Broadcom Trident 3 silicon, it provides 48 x 25GbE access ports and 8 x 100GbE uplinks with 4 Tbps switching capacity in a compact 1U design. Support for 100GbE breakout enables flexible network upgrades, while redundant hot-swappable power supplies and smart fans ensure high availability.

Running [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, the switch supports MLAG, SSH, ACLs, OSPF, PBR, and sFlow without additional licensing and includes five years of hardware and software support. Ideal for large campus distribution, campus core, and enterprise data center ToR deployments.

The switch is managed through the [AmpCon Management Software for Campus Networks](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), enabling centralized management and automated lifecycle operations from Day 0 to Day 2+. It provides Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), automated topology discovery, and real-time telemetry to simplify campus management and improve operational efficiency.
