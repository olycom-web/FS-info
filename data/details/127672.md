### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48x 100M/1000M/2.5GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit, with 4x 25Gb SFP28 and 2x 40Gb QSFP+, Support MLAG

The S5800-48MBQ is a high-performance, secure, and easy-to-manage multi-gigabit access and aggregation switch. It features 48 flexible 100M/1G/2.5GBASE-T ports with line-rate 10/25/40G uplinks, delivering 600 Gbps switching capacity and 445.76 Mpps forwarding performance to meet bandwidth demands for Wi-Fi 6/6E/7 access and high-density terminal aggregation. Hot-swappable redundant power supplies and fans enhance system resiliency.

With comprehensive Layer 2/3 features, including MLAG, MPLS, VXLAN-BGP-EVPN, PTP, and MACsec for secure point-to-point links, the S5800-48MBQ provides scalable performance and strong security. It supports management through the command-line interface (CLI) and built-in web UI, plus SNMP-based monitoring with AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. The switch is ideal for enterprise campuses, branch networks, data center edge environments, and multi-site deployments requiring high-density multi-gigabit access.
