### S5440H-24MU, 24-Port Ethernet L3 PoE++ Switch, 16 x 2.5G, 8 x 10GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit, with 8 x 25Gb SFP28 Uplinks, Support VXLAN & MLAG

The S5440H-24MU features 16x 2.5Gb PoE++, 8x 10Gb PoE++ access ports and 8x 10G/25G SFP28 uplink ports. It provides a total PoE power budget of 1000W, with up to 90W available per port. The switch delivers advanced Layer 2/3 features, including MLAG and BGP, as well as EVPN-VXLAN for scalable overlay networking, and integrates MACsec for line-rate point-to-point encryption, ensuring both performance and security.

It supports Web, CLI, SNMP, and SSH management interfaces, simplifying operations while enhancing visibility and control. With high performance, scalability, and ease of management, the S5440H-24MU is ideal for large campus access networks and small to medium-sized enterprise deployments.
