### 24-Port Ethernet L2+ PoE++ Switch, 24 x 2.5GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit, with 6 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks

The S3260-24MU is a L2+ managed switch, equipped with 24x 2.5Gb RJ45 and 6x 10Gb SFP+ uplink ports support both IEEE 802.3at PoE+ and IEEE 802.3bt PoE++ (up to 90W per port) for powering attached Wi-Fi 6/Wi-Fi 6E access points, IP cameras, and other high-power devices.

The switch features intelligent fan speed control scheme and silent design to create a green and energy-saving network. With enterprise high-reliability design, enhanced Layer 2 functions and simplified management, it delivers a flexible and cost-effective solution for medium and large enterprises, campus networks, and especially high-density office or video conferencing environments.
