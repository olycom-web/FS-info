### T5580-32C, 32 x 100G QSFP28 with 2 x 10G SFP+ Ports, Network Packet Broker (NPB), PicOS®

The FS T5580-32C Network Packet Broker (NPB) is equipped with 32x 100G QSFP28 and 2× 10G SFP+ ports. The device runs PicOS® under a perpetual license and includes five years of bundled service. It is designed for high-bandwidth monitoring and analysis environments. It supports advanced features such as traffic aggregation, replication, filtering, and load balancing. Additionally, GRE and VXLAN tunneling are included without per-port license fees, thereby enhancing the performance of network analysis and security tools.

## Software Spotlights

## Pervasive Visibility (图标: https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/2022113017252970eiku.svg)

- Aggregate network traffic to a single or multiple tools (1:1, 1:N, N:1, N:N)
- Support traffic replication and port distribution
- Ingress and egress filter support IPv4, IPv6
- Drop filter support IPv4, IPv6
- 1k filters
- Configurable hash-based load balancing
- Support uploading and downloading files via TFTP, FTP, SFTP, and WebUI
- Support OpenFlow/SDN

## Advanced Functionality (图标: https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20221130172533h8j9rj.svg)

- VxLAN, GRE, NvGRE, Tunnel Encapsulate and Decapsulate
- Support jumbo frames
- Support UDF L2, L3 and L4
- Support sFLOW、NTP、SNMP Get/Trap
- Support VLAN tagging and stripping
- Support GRE de-tunneling, VXLAN de-tunneling

## Management (图标: https://resource.fs.com/mall/ckfinder/ftp_images/product/20211113115011_341.svg)

- Remote management GUI, SSH and WebUI
