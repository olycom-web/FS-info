### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48x 10Gb SFP+, with 8x 100Gb QSFP28, Support Stacking, Broadcom Chip

S5860-48SC is a high-performance stackable switch for campus networks, offering 48x 1G/10G downlinks and 8x 40G/100G QSFP28 uplinks in a 1RU form factor. Powered by Broadcom chip, it delivers 2.56 Tbps switching capacity and 1904 Mpps forwarding, ensuring stable performance under heavy traffic. Redundant hot-swappable power supplies and intelligent fans provide continuous operation and efficient cooling.

Rich L2/L3 features, including BGP, OSPF, VRRP, DHCP Relay/Snooping, QoS, ACLs, and security policies, ensure scalable, reliable, and secure networking. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. Ideal for campus aggregation, remote offices, and WAN extension requiring high-speed, long-distance connectivity.
