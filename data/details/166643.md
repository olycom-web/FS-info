### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 24 x PoE+ Ports @370W, with 4 x 2.5Gb SFP Uplinks, Broadcom Chip

S3270-24TM-P, 24-port switch is equipped with 24x 10/100/1000M RJ45 ports, 4x 2.5Gb SFP uplinks. The Ethernet all-optical network adopts point-to-point transmission. The high-quality signal is transmitted without external interference and the data is forwarded more steadily. This switch delivers 68 Gbps switching capacity and 50.59 Mpps forwarding rate.

This access switch delivers a compact, cost-effective solution for carrier's IP MAN and enterprise networks. Based on the high-performance hardware and FSOS platform, it supports functions such as ACL, QinQ and QoS. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. Its simple management mode and flexible installation can meet the requirement of any complicated scenario.
