### S2805S-24TF, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 Smart Managed Switch, 24 x 10/100/1000M Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, Fanless

S2805S‑24TF is a Gigabit L2 smart managed switch designed for retail stores, small business offices, and campus edge networks. It provides 24x 10/100/1000MBASE-T ports and 4x 1Gb SFP uplinks that meet the network expand needs, providing improved sustainability and a borderless network experience.

Its fanless design ensures silent, energy‑efficient operation and lower maintenance costs in noise‑sensitive environments. A user‑friendly web‑based management interface simplifies configuration, monitoring, and troubleshooting, enabling IT staff to deploy and operate the switch with minimal effort. Enhanced L2 features such as VLAN, QoS, LACP, and IGMP optimize traffic handling, while security capabilities including SSH, ACL, and AAA protect critical services and management access from unauthorized users, making S2805S‑24TF a reliable and secure choice for modern SMB networks.
