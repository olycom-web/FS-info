### N5580-48Y8C, 48-Port Ethernet Data Center Switch, 48 x 25Gb SFP28, 8 x 100Gb QSFP28, with 2 x 10Gb SFP+ Port, PicOS®, Broadcom Trident 3 Chip

The N5580-48Y8C is a high-performance leaf switch with perpetual license and 5-year service included. It delivers rich, low-latency Layer 2/Layer 3 features and advanced EVPN-VXLAN capabilities. With 48 native 25G downlink ports and 8 40/100GbE uplink ports, it is ideal for leaf roles in data centers and data center interconnect (DCI) deployments.

Featuring L3 gateway functionality for seamless routing between virtualized and bare-metal servers, the switch is designed for extremely agile data centers that require support for overlay/underlay network architectures.
