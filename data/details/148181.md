### IES3110-8TFP-R, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Industrial PoE+ Switch, 8 x PoE+ Ports @240W, with 2 x 1Gb SFP

The IES3110-8TFP-R, designed for rugged environment and -40 to 75°C wide operating temperature, features 8x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE+ ports and 2x 1G SFP ports. With 8 PoE+ in a compact form factor, the IP40-rated rugged switch is designed for power and space-constrained areas like traffic control cabinets, factory floors, and extreme outdoor environments. 8x RJ45 ports support IEEE 802.3at PoE+ (up to 30W per port) for powering IP cameras, wireless APs, and sensors. This layer 2 industrial managed switch supports enhanced L2+ functions such as VLAN, QoS, IGMP Snooping, LACP, etc.
