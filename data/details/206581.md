### N5680-32D, 32-Port Ethernet HPC/AI Data Center Switch, 32 x 400Gb QSFP-DD, PicOS®, Broadcom Tomahawk 3 Chip

Built for HPC/AI and modern data center networks, the N5680-32D supports large-scale, high-density, and high-performance fabrics based on proven Internet-scale architecture. It offers flexible 100GbE, 200GbE, and 400GbE interfaces for server connectivity and intra-fabric interconnection.

The N5680-32D is well suited for spine-and-leaf deployments in enterprise, service provider, and cloud data centers, as well as routing applications in metro environments.
