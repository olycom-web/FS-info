### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Enterprise Switch, 48 x Gigabit RJ45, with 4 x 25Gb SFP28 and 2 x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Support EVPN-VXLAN, Broadcom Chip

The S5870-48T6BC is a next-generation Layer 3 switch built for secure, scalable campus networks. Featuring a Broadcom chipset, 48x Gigabit RJ45 ports, 4x 25G SFP28, and 2x 100G QSFP28 uplinks, it supports high-density access, Wi-Fi 6/7 expansion, and growing bandwidth needs. Dual hot-swappable power supplies and 3 fan modules help ensure service continuity. Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, the switch supports MLAG, BGP, OSPF, OpenFlow, and EVPN-VXLAN for secure service segmentation, resilient campus fabrics, and simplified expansion without additional licensing, plus five years of hardware and software support.

Managed through [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), the S5870-48T6BC streamlines Day 0 to Day 2+ lifecycle management with Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and real-time telemetry visibility, helping simplify operations across enterprise, education, healthcare, and regional campus environments.
