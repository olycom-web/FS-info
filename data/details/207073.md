### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48 x 10GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit Ports, 4 x 25Gb SFP28, with 2 x 40Gb QSFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5860-48XMG is a high-performance Layer 3 multi-gigabit switch designed for enterprise campus and branch networks, ideal for access, distribution, and SMB core deployments. Powered by the Broadcom BCM56170, it delivers up to 1.32 Tbps switching capacity and 565 Mpps wire-speed forwarding, with 48x 1/2.5/5/10GBASE-T ports, 4x 25G SFP28, and 2x 40G QSFP+ uplinks for flexible, scalable connectivity.

Running [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, the switch provides Layer 2 and Layer 3 features, including MLAG, OSPF, ACLs, SSH, RoCE, and sFlow without additional licensing. The switch is managed by the [AmpCon management software](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html), which enables unified wired and wireless management, automated Day 0 to Day 2+ lifecycle management, and end-to-end network visibility, simplifying campus operations while improving efficiency and reducing operational complexity.
