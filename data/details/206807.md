### 24-Port Ethernet L3 PoE++ Switch, 24x 5GBASE-T/Multi-Gigabit, with 4x 25Gb SFP28, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip, PicOS®

S5860-24MG-U is a next-gen L3 switch featuring 24x 100M/1G/2.5G/5GBase-T multi-gigabit PoE++ ports supporting up to 90W per port, with 4 10G/25G SFP28 uplink ports. Ideal for enterprise campuses, hotels and retail scenarios, it supports high-density PoE terminal deployments for devices including Wi-Fi 6E, and offers multi-gigabit access, high-power PoE power supply and high-speed uplink capabilities. Perpetual & Fast PoE with power protection keeps devices powered during reboots for stable operation.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports static routing, OSPF, RIP, QoS, MLAG, LACP, VRRP and VLAN without extra licenses, including five years of hardware and software support.

Managed by the [Ampcon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html?now_cid=5513), it enables automated ZTP, real-time telemetry, topology auto-discovery, and lifecycle management, delivering centralized visibility and simplified wired and wireless operations for reliable, future-ready enterprise networks.
