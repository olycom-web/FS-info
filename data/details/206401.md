### N5630-64C, 64-Port Ethernet L3 Data Center Switch, 64 x 100Gb QSFP28, PicOS®, Broadcom Tomahawk 2 Chip

The N5630-64C is a compact 2U switch featuring 64 line-rate QSFP+/QSFP28 ports supporting 10/25/40/100GbE connections, with 40/100GbE interfaces offering 4x 10GbE or 4x 25GbE breakout options that provide up to 64 logical 10/25GbE ports. Powered by PicOS® with a perpetual license and 5-year service, it delivers advanced Layer 2 and Layer 3 capabilities for spine-leaf deployments in service providers and cloud provider data centers, as well as for routing use cases in metro environments.

Manage your N5630-64C deployment with [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/283015.html), which automates the entire network lifecycle to simplify design, deployment, and operations from Day 0 to Day 2+.
