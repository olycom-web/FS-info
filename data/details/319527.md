### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48x 10Gb SFP+, with 8x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5580-48S Ethernet switch offers high scalability, availability, and performance for midsize to large enterprise campus distribution and core deployments. Built on TD3-X7 chip, it provides wire-speed 10GbE and 100GbE (split into 4× 10/25G via breakout cables) ports in a compact 1U form factor, delivering 2.56 Tbps of Layer 2/3 connectivity to secure routers, servers, and switches. Supporting advanced features like MLAG, BGP, and OSPF, it is also well-suited for enterprise data center top-of-rack (TOR) deployments.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus management platform](https://www.fs.com/products/258683.html) (available in future software releases), offering Day 0 to Day 2+ automated lifecycle management with Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP) and topology discovery. AmpCon-Campus automates workflows, simplifies operations, and enhances visibility into the performance of connected devices.
