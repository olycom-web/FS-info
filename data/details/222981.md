### 16-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 8 x PoE+ Ports@125W, 2 x 5Gb RJ45, with 2 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip, Fanless

The S3410C-16TMS-P is a 16-port PoE+ switch featuring 16x 10M/100/1000MBASE-T ports, 2x 1000M/2.5G/5GBASE-T ports, and 2x 1/10Gb SFP+ uplinks. It is ideal for applications in education, business, hospitals, and office parks, facilitating seamless interconnection of security cameras and Wi-Fi 6 access points. This all-optical Ethernet switch supports future network expansion and reduces operation costs.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG, Voice VLAN, OSPF, LACP and sFlow without requiring additional licenses, and includes five years of hardware and software support.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus Management Platform](https://www.fs.com/products/258683.html?now_cid=5513), which offers automated Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and automated lifecycle management with 1, 3, or 5 years of service. AmpCon-Campus automates routine workflows, minimizing downtime and manual interventions to enable efficient orchestration and management of highly-available networks at scale.
