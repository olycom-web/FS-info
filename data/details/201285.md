### IES2100-5FE, 5-Port Fast Ethernet L2 Unmanaged Industrial Switch, 5 x 10/100BASE-T

The IES2100-5FE features 5x 10/100BASE-T RJ45 ports, which is designed especially for a broad spectrum of industrial and extended enterprise connectivity needs. The Unmanaged plug-and-play design makes for easy deployment, no technician needed. Equipped with an IP41-rated metal enclosure, it can operate within a wide temperature range of -40 to 75°C. The circuit board has a three-proof coating and fanless heat dissipation characteristics, this switch is ideal for harsh environments, such as traffic control cabinets, factory floors, and outdoor places where the temperature is extremely low or high. It also supports DIN-rail mounting, which is very space-saving for cabinets.
