### S3240C-24T, 24-Port Ethernet L2+ Compact Half-Width Switch, 24x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks

The S3240C-24T is a Layer 2+ managed switch with 24x 10M/100M/1000M RJ45 ports and 4x 10Gb SFP+ uplink ports in a compact half-width form factor. It supports single or dual-switch assembly in 1U rack space, wall-mounted deployment, and single or dual-row placement without a cabinet, and can be flexibly deployed according to actual network needs. This powerful switch provides 128 Gbps switching capacity and 190.464 Mpps forwarding rate. It adopts an intelligent fan speed control scheme that automatically adjusts speed based on temperature changes to create a green, energy-saving network.

The switch combines advanced hardware and software and supports features such as OSPF, Voice VLAN, LACP, and QoS, providing cost-effective services with unrivalled ease of use, making it an ideal choice for spaces such as cloud rooms in enterprise and SMB networks that require high flexibility in switch size.
