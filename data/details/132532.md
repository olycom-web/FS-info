### S1900-16TP, 16-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 Unmanaged PoE+ Switch, 16 x PoE+ Ports@135W, Fanless, Desktop/Rackmount

The S1900-16TP is equipped with 16x 10/100/1000M Auto-Negotiation RJ45 ports. All ports support the Auto MDI/MDIX function and IEEE 802.3af/at PoE+ (up to 30W per port) for powering attached IP phones, wireless access points, and other standards-compliant network devices. Its fanless design ensures noise-free switch operation. Additionally, the switch features a professional integrated lightning protection circuit for stable operation.

This cost-effective, plug-and-play L2 unmanaged switch is ideal for SOHO offices, intelligent communities and parks with limited IT know-how and support.
