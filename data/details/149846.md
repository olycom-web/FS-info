### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Managed Switch, 48x Gigabit RJ45, 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Stackable Switch, Broadcom Chip

The S5810-48TS is a next-generation multiservice switch with 48x Gigabit RJ45 downlinks and 4x 10G SFP+ uplinks, supporting stacking of up to 8 switches of the S5810 series for seamless interconnection and unified management. Powered by advanced Broadcom chips, it features redundant hot-swappable power supplies and variable-speed axial fans ensures superior performance and reliability.

S5810-48TS is managed by the Airware cloud management platform, which combines instant actions with centralized visibility to simplify switch provisioning, configuring, and troubleshooting. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. With simplified management and various switching functionality, the S5810 series provides a high-performance, cost-effective solution for access layer deployments in campuses, SMB networks, branch offices, and distributed sites.
