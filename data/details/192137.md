### 16-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 16x Gigabit RJ45, with 8x 10Gb SFP+, 8x 25Gb SFP28 and 2x 40Gb QSFP+ Uplink, Support MLAG/Stacking

The S5850-16T16BS2Q is a high-performance Layer 3 Ethernet routing switch designed for modern enterprise networks. It features 16x Gigabit RJ45, 8x 10G SFP+, 8x 25G SFP28, and 2x 40G QSFP+ ports, delivering wire-speed performance and flexible multi-rate connectivity for diverse deployment scenarios. With comprehensive Layer 2/3 capabilities including MLAG/stacking, [MPLS & VXLAN-BGP-EVPN](https://www.fs.com/products/100590.html), and PTP, as well as MACsec for point-to-point link encryption, it provides scalable performance and robust security for modern networks.

Equipped with redundant hot-swappable power supplies and variable-speed axial fans, the S5850-16T16BS2Q delivers efficient, stable, and reliable operation. It supports Web UI, CLI, SNMP, and SSH management, offering flexible operational options that simplify management, enhance network visibility, and improve security. With its high performance, robust security, and ease of management, it is ideal for enterprise campuses, branch offices, and data centers.
