### IES4100W-8U2FD, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Industrial PoE+ Switch, 8 x PoE+ Ports @240W, with 2 x 1/2.5Gb SFP, -20 to 70°C Operating Temperature

The IES4100W-8U2FD features 8x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE+ ports and 2x 1/2.5G SFP ports. All eight RJ45 ports support IEEE 802.3at PoE+ (up to 30W per port) for powering IP cameras, VoIP phones, and wireless APs. With its user-friendly front-access design and LCD touchscreen, the switch offers convenient management for on-site technicians. Its compact, flat-type design supports wall-mount installation, making it ideal for space-constrained areas such as shallow cabinets or small enclosures.

This layer 2 industrial managed switch supports enhanced L2+ functions such as ERPS, VLAN, QoS, IGMP/MLD Snooping, LACP, etc. In addition, it supports IEEE 1588v2 PTP (transparent clock), making it well suited for industrial automation scenarios.
