### 28-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 20 x 1Gb SFP, 8 x 1G RJ45/SFP Combo Ports, with 4 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5810-28FS is a high-performance Layer 3 switch designed for enterprise campus, SMB core networks, and data center access. With 28x 1Gb SFP ports (including 8 combo) and 4x 10Gb SFP+ uplinks, it provides high-density access and non-blocking forwarding for voice, video conferencing, and HD surveillance. Powered by a Broadcom chip with hot-swappable redundant power supplies and smart fans for enhanced resiliency. Pre-installed with the [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) system (perpetual license included), it supports Layer 2/3 features, OpenFlow, and NETCONF with no additional licenses and includes five years of hardware and software support.

The switch is managed through the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), enabling Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, real-time telemetry monitoring, and end-to-end lifecycle management from Day 0 to Day 2. AmpCon simplifies switch onboarding, configuration, monitoring, and ongoing management, improving network visibility and operational efficiency.
