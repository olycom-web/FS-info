### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 PoE+ Switch, 48x PoE+ Ports @740W, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Broadcom Chip, PicOS®

S5810-48TS-P, a next-generation L3 switch with 48x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE ports and 4x 1G/10G uplink ports, is crafted for high-performance enterprise campus, hospitality, and retail deployments, enabling seamless connectivity of Wi-Fi 6 access points, VoIP phones, and IP cameras while providing PoE power to support connected devices.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports OpenFlow, MLAG, OSPF, BGP, IPv4/IPv6, PBR and STP without requiring additional licenses, and includes five years of hardware and software support.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus Management Platform](https://www.fs.com/products/258683.html), which offers automated Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), real-time telemetry monitoring, topology auto-discovery, and automated lifecycle management with 1, 3, or 5 years of service. AmpCon-Campus automates routine workflows, minimizing downtime and manual interventions to enable efficient orchestration and management of highly-available networks at scale.
