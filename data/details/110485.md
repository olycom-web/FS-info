### 24-Port 25Gb SFP28 Line Card with 2x 100Gb QSFP28 for Data Center Switch NC8200-4TD

The NC8200-24BC Ethernet line card supports 24x 25G SFP28 ports and 2x 100G QSFP28, offering line-rate forwarding from all ports. The SFP28 ports are backward compatible with SFP+ modules. The QSFP28 ports are backward compatible with QSFP+ modules.
