### S2805S-8TF-P, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2 Smart Managed PoE+ Switch, 8 x PoE+ Ports @140W, with 2x 1Gb SFP Uplinks

The S2805S-8TF-P is a high-performance Layer 2 smart-managed switch that provides reliable, cost-effective networking for retail, small business, and campus environments. This 8-port Gigabit PoE+ switch (30W max per port) with 2x 1G SFP uplinks delivers power to standards-compliant PDs (Powered Devices).

Its intuitive web interface simplifies network operations while robust security features protect critical data. Advanced Layer 2 functionality including VLAN, LACP and QoS enhances network performance, accelerates data throughput, maximizes availability, and improves workforce productivity.
