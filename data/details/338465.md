### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48 x 25Gb SFP28, with 8 x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG

The S5470-48Y is a next-generation Layer 3 switch built for high performance and flexibility in modern campus networks. It delivers up to 4 Tbps of switching capacity and 2976 Mpps of forwarding performance, featuring 48x 25Gb SFP28 ports and 8x 100Gb QSFP28 uplinks, splittable into 4x 10/25Gb for flexibility. Running [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG, BGP, OSPF, SSH, ACLs, PBR, and sFlow without additional licenses and includes five years of hardware and software support. Ideal for midsized to large enterprise campus distribution, core, and low-density data center ToR deployments.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus management platform](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html) (Support for this model will be available in the AmpCon‑Campus V3.0.0), offering Day 0 to Day 2+ automated lifecycle management with Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology discovery, and real-time telemetry. AmpCon-Campus automates workflows, simplifies operations, and enhances visibility into connected devices.
