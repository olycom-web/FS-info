### IES3110-8TFM-P, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Industrial PoE+ Switch, 8 x PoE+ Ports @240W, and 2 x 10/100/1000BASE-T, with 2 x 1Gb SFP, 2 x 1/2.5Gb SFP, -40 to 75°C Operating Temperature

The IES3110-8TFM-P features 8x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE+ ports, 2x 10/100/1000BASE-T copper ports, 2x 1G SFP and 2x 1/2.5G SFP ports, which is designed especially for a broad spectrum of industrial and extended enterprise connectivity needs. With an IP30-rated metal casing, fanless design, and a wide temperature range of -40 to 75°C(-40 to 167°F), the switch is suitable for harsh environments. It supports a 240W power budget for connecting PoE-powered end devices such as IP cameras, phones, wireless access points, and more. This layer 2 industrial managed switch supports enhanced L2+ functions such as ACL, QoS, LACP link aggregation, etc, which can meet the requirements of many scenarios in industrial environments. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through [AmpCon-Campus](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html?now_cid=5513) for enhanced network visibility.
