### S5440L-24P, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 PoE+ Switch, 24 x PoE+ Ports @370W, with 6 x 25Gb SFP28 Uplinks, Support VXLAN & MLAG

The S5440L-24P features 24x 10/100/1000 Mbps PoE access ports and 6x 10G/25G uplink ports. It provides a total PoE power budget of 370W, with up to 30W available per port. The switch delivers advanced Layer 2/3 features, including MLAG/stacking and BGP, as well as VXLAN for scalable overlay networking, and integrates MACsec for line-rate point-to-point encryption, ensuring both performance and security.

It supports Web, CLI, SNMP, and SSH management interfaces, simplifying operations while enhancing visibility and control. With high performance, scalability, and ease of management, the S5440L-24P is ideal for large campus access networks and small to medium-sized enterprise deployments.
