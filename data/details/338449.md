### 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48 x 10Gb SFP+, with 6 x 40Gb QSFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG

The S5470-48S is a next-generation Layer 3 switch built for high performance and flexibility in modern campus networks. It delivers up to 1.44 Tbps of switching capacity and 1071.4 Mpps of forwarding performance, featuring 48x 10Gb SFP+ ports and 6x 40Gb QSFP+ uplinks, which can be split into 4x 10Gb SFP+ for added flexibility. Running [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG, BGP, OSPF, SSH, ACLs, PBR, and sFlow without requiring additional licenses and includes five years of hardware and software support. Ideal for large campus distribution, SMB core networks, and low-density data center ToR deployments.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus management platform](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html) (Support for this model will be available in the upcoming AmpCon‑Campus V3.0.0), offering Day 0 to Day 2+ automated lifecycle management with Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology discovery, and real-time telemetry. AmpCon-Campus automates workflows, simplifies operations, and enhances visibility into the performance of connected devices.
