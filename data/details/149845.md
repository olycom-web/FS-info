### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 48x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, Stackable Switch, Broadcom Chip

The S3910-48TF gigabit Layer 2+ stackable managed switch features 48x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports and 4x 1Gb SFP uplinks. This 48-port gigabit managed switch supports virtual stacking of up to 4 units, providing redundancy, simple management, and increased network capacity resilience.

Equipped with the Broadcom BCM56152 switch chip, the S3910-48TF offers a switching capacity of 104 Gbps and a forwarding rate of 78 Mpps, ensuring superior network performance. Furthermore, with support for IPv4/IPv6 and a range of advanced layer 3 routing features (such as static routing, OSPF, RIP, RIPng, etc.), this gigabit switch enables flexible deployment in SMB, enterprise, midmarket, and branch office networks to provide lower total cost of ownership. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility.
