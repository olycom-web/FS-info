### S3240-8T, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 8x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Stackable Switch, Fanless

The S3240-8T 8-Port Ethernet L2+ Switch is equipped with 8x 10/100/1000M RJ45 ports and 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks. This switch delivers 96 Gbps switching capacity and 72 Mpps forwarding rate. Fanless design ensures noise-free switch operation, reduced cost, and power consumption, and is ideal for use in quiet, small work spaces. Based on the high-performance hardware, it supports functions such as Stacking, OSPF, Voice VLAN, and QoS, and supports simple device management and network management via a Command-Line Interface (CLI) as well as an on-box web UI. With these features, the switch provides a cost-effective solution for the IP MAN (metropolitan area network), government and enterprise networks, Internet café, education, and office environment.
