### S5890-32C, 32-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 32 x 100Gb QSFP28, PicOS®, Support EVPN-VXLAN & MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5890-32C is a 32-port 100GbE Layer 3 switch built for campus core, aggregation, and high-density uplink deployments. With 32 x QSFP28 ports supporting 10/25/40/100 GbE, Broadcom Trident 3 switching chipset, and non-blocking performance, it helps simplify 100G network upgrades while maintaining flexibility for existing infrastructures. Running PicOS®, the switch supports MLAG, BGP, OSPF, and EVPN-VXLAN to enable service segmentation, multi-tenant networking, and resilient campus fabrics. It also includes a perpetual PicOS® license and five years of hardware and software support.

When managed with [AmpCom management software for campus networks](https://www.fs.com/specials/ampcon-campus-platform-193.html), the S5890-32C supports automated lifecycle operations from Day 0 to Day 2+, including ZTP, topology discovery, and real-time telemetry. This helps reduce manual deployment work, improve network visibility, and speed up troubleshooting across campus networks.

- Q(02/26/2026): 哪些 FS 交换机可作 S5890-32C 同类部署?A: 可选 S5590-64C(PicOS 100G 高密度)或 [S8550-32C](https://www.fs.com/products/155948.html)(FSOS 100G)。
- Q(01/28/2026): 现用 Edgecore AS6812-32X(32×40G QSFP+),S5890-32C 是否支持 48×25G+8×100G 结构及 L2(STP/VLAN/LACP/MLAG)、L3(BGP multipath/BFD/SVI)?A: 支持 BGP ECMP、BFD、MLAG,但端口为 32×100G QSFP28 可 breakout 25/40/10G;若需固定 48×25G+8×100G 可选 [S5580-48Y](https://www.fs.com/products/338465.html?now_cid=4246)。
