### S3100-16TF-P, 16-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 8 x PoE+ Ports@125W, 8 x 1Gb RJ45, with 2 x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, Broadcom Chip, Fanless

The S3100-16TF-P is a high-performance Layer 2+ managed switch designed to provide reliable and effective networking for educational, business, and hospital environments. This 16-port gigabit switch includes 8 PoE+ ports (30W max per port) that deliver power to standards-compliant powered devices, as well as 2x 1G SFP uplinks to accommodate future network expansion demands.

Its intuitive Airware management simplifies network deployment and operation, while robust security features protect critical transactions. Advanced L2+ functionality, such as Voice VLAN, LACP, and QoS, enhances network performance and ensures reliable connectivity for fast file transfers and data processing. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. This improves uptime and workforce productivity.
