### Customized IES5220-6S-3ES, 3-Slot Modular Ethernet L3 Managed Industrial Switch, 6 x 1/10Gb SFP+

The IES5220-6S-3ES industrial modular switch features 6 x 1/10Gb SFP+ ports, expandable to up to 30 ports, optimized for size and power consumption for a wide range of industrial and high-density connection needs. Equipped with an IP40-rated metal enclosure, it can operate within a wide temperature range of -40 to 70°C. This switch is ideal for large-scale industrial automation backbone networks requiring precise and high bandwidth, especially in environments with extreme temperatures.

As a Layer 3 managed industrial switch, it supports multiple ring protection and redundancy protocols, including ERPS, MRP and VRRP, ensuring fast convergence in under 10ms and compatibility with industrial protocols like Profinet, Ethernet/IP(CIP), Modbus and CC-Link to ensure seamless communication between devices. This facilitates rapid integration with existing industrial networks while reducing deployment complexity and maintenance costs.

Note:

IES5220-8T-E and IES5220-4T4FP-E can be shared with all three slots.

IES5220-4S-E is only applicable to the third slot.
