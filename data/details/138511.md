### IES3100-8TF-P, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Industrial PoE+ Switch, 8 x PoE+ Ports @240W, with 2 x 1/2.5Gb SFP

The IES3100-8TF-P features 8x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE+ ports and 2x 100/1000/2500BASE-X SFP ports, which is designed especially for a wide variety of extended enterprise and industrial applications. With an IP30-rated metal casing, wide temperature range (-40 to 75°C), and redundant power supply, the switch is suitable for use under harsh ambient conditions. It supports a 240W power budget for connecting PoE-powered end devices such as IP cameras, phones, wireless access points, sensors, and more. This layer 2 industrial managed switch supports enhanced L2+ functions such as VLAN, QoS, IGMP Snooping, LACP, etc. In addition, it also supports PTP (Precision Time Protocol), which is very suitable for industrial automation scenarios.
