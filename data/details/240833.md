### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Half-width Switch, 24x 100M/1000M/2.5GBase-T/Multi-Gigabit, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks

The S3400C-24MG4S is a Layer 2+ managed switch with 24x 100M/1000M/2.5GBase-T ports and 4x 10Gb SFP+ uplink ports in a compact half-width form factor. It supports single or dual-machine assembly and single or double-row stacking without cabinets, and can be flexibly deployed according to actual network needs. This powerful switch provides 200 Gbps switching capacity and 148.8 Mpps forwarding rate. It adopts an intelligent fan speed control scheme that automatically adjusts speed based on temperature changes to create a green, energy-saving network.

The switch combines advanced hardware and software and supports features such as OSPF, Voice VLAN, LACP, and QoS, providing cost-effective services with unrivalled ease of use, making it an ideal choice for next-generation SMB, enterprise, Wi-Fi6AX access, and high-speed NAS networks.
