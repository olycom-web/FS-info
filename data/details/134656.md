### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 16x 1Gb SFP, 8x 1Gb RJ45/SFP Combo Ports, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Stackable Switch

The S3900-24F4S-R is an L2+ managed stackable switch designed for SMBs, enterprises, midmarket, and branch office networks, offering a lower total cost of ownership. It features 24x 1G SFP ports (including 8x combo ports) and 4x 10G SFP+ uplinks, delivering a switching capacity of 128 Gbps and a forwarding rate of 96 Mpps. With dual redundant power supplies and a dual-fan design, the switch ensures reliable, uninterrupted operation even during power supply failures.

The switch supports stacking technology, which simplifies operations by interconnecting up to 8 switches that can be managed as a single logical device. It supports advanced features such as OSPF, Voice VLAN, robust QoS, Static Routing, and IPv6. Additionally, it offers simple device and network management through a Command-Line Interface (CLI) and an intuitive on-box web UI.
