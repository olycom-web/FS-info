### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 24 x Gigabit RJ45, with 4 x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip, Fanless

The S3410L-24TF is a Gigabit Layer 2+ managed switch designed for SMB, enterprise, midmarket, and branch office networks. It features 24x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports and 4x 1Gb SFP uplinks, providing high-density access and non-blocking forwarding. Powered by Broadcom chips, it delivers 56 Gbps switching capacity and a 24 Mpps forwarding rate. Its fanless design enables silent operation, lower power consumption, and reduced operating costs. Pre-installed with the [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) system (perpetual license included), it supports MLAG and OSPF with no additional licenses and includes five years of hardware and software support.

The switch is managed through the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), enabling Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and end-to-end lifecycle management from Day 0 to Day 2. AmpCon simplifies switch onboarding, configuration, monitoring, and ongoing management, improving network visibility and operational efficiency.
