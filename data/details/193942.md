### S2805S-48T4S, 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Smart Managed Switch, 48 x Gigabit RJ45, with 4 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Realtek Chip

S2805S-48T4S features 48x 10/100/1000MBASE-T ports and 4x 10Gb SFP+ uplinks. These SFP+ ports facilitate connections to higher-layer devices, boosting network scalability. Its user-friendly web interface streamlines operations, enhancing efficiency, while robust security safeguards critical transactions.

With a switching capacity of 176 Gbps and a forwarding rate of 130.944 Mpps, this switch offers enhanced functions like VLAN, QoS, and IGMP to bolster network performance. This high-performance L3 smart managed switch is ideal for retail, small business, and campus networks.
