### 32-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 32 x 100Gb QSFP28, Support MPLS&MLAG, Hyper-Converged Infrastructure

The S8550-32C 32-port 100Gb Ethernet layer 3 switch is a high-density switch for campus core and distribution, offering redundant hot-swappable power supplies and smart fans for superior performance and redundancy. The switch comes with complete system software supporting comprehensive protocols and features like VLAN, QinQ, BGP, OSPF, IS-IS etc, and delivers high programmability and scalability with EVPN and VXLAN. Supporting various management interfaces and providing WEB/CLI/SNMP/SSH, this enterprise switch simplifies management while maintaining high efficiency. The S8550-32C switch meets requirements of large enterprise and HCI (Hyper-Converged Infrastructure) networks, and it is also ideal for traditional or fully virtualized data centers.
