### 32-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 32 x 400Gb QSFP-DD, PicOS®, Broadcom Trident 4 Chip

The S8520-32D is a high-density, cost-optimized 1U switch built on Broadcom Trident 4 silicon, supporting 400GbE, 200GbE, and 100GbE connectivity for scalable high-performance networks. Powered by PicOS® with a perpetual license, it delivers comprehensive L2/L3 features including MLAG, BGP, and OSPF, with lossless Ethernet capabilities such as RoCEv2, PFC, and ECN. With built-in security and five years of hardware and software support, it ensures reliable operation in mission-critical environments. Designed for high port-density Campus Core/Edge deployments, the S8520-32D also excels in multi–data center interconnects, AI/HPC clusters, and large-scale Spine/Leaf backbones.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus management platform](https://www.fs.com/products/344499.html), enabling automated Day 0–Day 2+ operations such as ZTP, topology discovery, and workflow automation to simplify management and enhance visibility.
