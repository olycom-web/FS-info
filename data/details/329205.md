### S3150-8T2FP, 8-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 8 x PoE+ Ports @130W, with2 x 1Gb SFP, Fanless

The S3150-8T2FP is a compact 8-port Gigabit L2+ PoE+ switch designed for small business access networks. It provides 8 PoE+ RJ45 ports with up to 30W per port and a 130W total PoE budget, making it easy to power wireless access points, IP phones, and IP cameras from one switch. 2x 1G SFP uplinks enable flexible fiber connections to aggregation or core devices.

With a fanless design and low device power consumption, it fits quiet work areas where noise matters. The switch also supports key L2+ features such as VLAN, QoS, static routing, RIP, OSPF, and ERPS, helping simplify network segmentation and improve service continuity. It supports Web GUI, CLI, SSH, SNMP, and sFlow for flexible management and traffic monitoring. In addition, it enables SNMP-based centralized monitoring through AmpCon-Campus, enhancing network visibility while reducing deployment and maintenance complexity for small and medium-sized networks.
