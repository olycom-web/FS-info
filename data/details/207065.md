### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 48x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5810-48TS is a next-generation L3 switch for cost-effective access deployments in branch offices, campus networks, and SMBs. With 48x Gigabit RJ45 ports and 4x 10G SFP+ uplinks, it provides high-density access and non-blocking transmission. Equipped with a Broadcom chip, it ensures reliable performance with hot-swappable redundant power supplies and smart fans. Pre-installed with the [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) system (perpetual license included), the switch supports L2/L3 and programmable capabilities like OpenFlow and NETCONF, without additional licensing, and includes five years of free hardware and software technical support.

The switch is managed through the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), enabling unified deployment and lifecycle operations from Day 0 to Day 2+. With Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and real-time telemetry visibility, AmpCon simplifies onboarding, configuration, and network operations to accelerate network expansion and improve end-to-end visibility.
