### IES5120-28TS-P, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Industrial PoE++ Switch, 8 x PoE++ Ports, 16 x PoE+ Ports @360W, with 4 x 1G/10G SFP+

The IES5120-28TS-P features 8x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE++, 16x 10/100/1000BASE-T PoE+ and 4x 1G/10G SFP+ ports, which is designed for a broad spectrum of industrial and extended enterprise connectivity needs. Equipped with an IP40-rated metal enclosure and a wide operating temperature range of -40 to 75°C (-40 to 167°F), this switch is ideal for harsh environments, such as traffic control cabinets, factory floors, and outdoor places. All 24 Gigabit PoE ports, including 8 PoE++ ports with up to 60W per port, support fast PoE and perpetual PoE to ensure continuous power delivery for critical devices. This Layer 2+ industrial managed switch supports IEEE 1588v2 PTP for precise time synchronization. It also enables transparent transmission of PROFINET, EtherNet/IP, and Modbus TCP, ensuring reliable and seamless industrial communication.
