### 32-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 32 x 100Gb QSFP28, 2 x 10Gb SFP+, PicOS®, Support EVPN-VXLAN & MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5580L-32C is a 32-port 100GbE Layer 3 switch built for campus core, aggregation, and high-density uplink deployments. With 32 x QSFP28 ports supporting 10/25/40/100 GbE, Broadcom Trident 3 switching chipset, and non-blocking performance, it helps simplify 100G network upgrades while maintaining flexibility for existing infrastructures. Running PicOS®, the switch supports MLAG, BGP, OSPF, and rich L2/L3 features to build scalable, resilient, and high-performance enterprise networks. It also includes a perpetual PicOS® license and five years of hardware and software support.When managed with AmpCom management software for campus networks, the S5890-32C supports automated lifecycle operations from Day 0 to Day 2+, including ZTP, topology discovery, and real-time telemetry.
