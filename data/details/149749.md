### S5500-48T6SP-R, 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Fully Managed Plus PoE+ Switch, 48 x PoE+ Ports @760W, with 6 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Support Stacking

S5500-48T6SP-R is a 48-port managed Layer 3 PoE+ switch, featuring 48 × 10/100/1000BASE-T ports and 6 × 1Gb/10Gb SFP+ uplink ports. With a total PoE budget of 760W and up to 30W per PoE+ port, it is well suited for powering IoT devices, security cameras, and wireless access points. It is ideal for access-layer deployments in retail environments, small and midsize campuses, as well as converged access in larger networks.

Supporting a comprehensive set of networking protocols—including stacking, IPv4, IPv6, BGP, OSPF, QoS, and RADIUS/TACACS+ security features—the switch streamlines campus network deployment and operations. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. It also offers flexible management options via Web GUI, CLI, RMON, and other standard management methods.
