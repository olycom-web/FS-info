### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Switch, 24x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Stackable Switch, Broadcom Chip

The S3910-24TS gigabit Layer 2+ stackable managed switch offers 24x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports and 4x 10Gb SFP+ uplinks, providing an economical and high-performance access-layer solution for branch offices and enterprise campus networks. With the Broadcom BCM56150 chip, the switch features 1+1 hot-swappable dual power supplies and a smart fan to ensure reliable, uninterrupted operation. It supports virtual stacking of up to four units, offering simplified management and enhanced scalability.

The S3910-24TS also supports advanced features such as VLAN, LACP, QoS, and DHCP enhance flexibility and traffic control. For management, the switch supports Web GUI, CLI, Telnet, SSH, and integrates with [Airware cloud-based management](https://airware-login.fs.com/), allowing you to configure, monitor, and maintain your network anytime and anywhere with efficiency, flexibility, and security. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility.
