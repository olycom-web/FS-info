### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 24x PoE+ Ports @740W, with 2x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks and 2x Combo SFP Ports, Broadcom Chip

S3410-24TS-P 24-Port managed PoE+ switch is equipped with 24x 10/10/10/100/1000BASE-T ports, 2x 1Gb RJ45/SFP combo ports and 2x 1/10Gb SFP+ uplinks. It offers hot-swappable power supplies and dual smart fans for enhanced resiliency and redundancy, and up to 30W of PoE per port to power the IoT devices, security cameras, and wireless APs. S3410-24TS-P is perfect for access-layer deployments in retail, small and midsize campus, and converged network access in larger networks.

Based on the high-performance hardware, it supports functions such as Stacking, RIP, OSPF, and QoS, and supports simple device management and network management via Airware or a Command-Line Interface (CLI) as well as an on-box web UI, which streamlines deploying and managing your campus fabric. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility.
