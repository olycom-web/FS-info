### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Switch, 24x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 1Gb SFP Uplinks, Stackable Switch, Broadcom Chip, Fanless

The S3910-24TF gigabit Layer 2+ stackable managed switch features 24x 10/100/1000BASE-T and 4x 1Gb SFP uplinks that are best for complex environment deployment, which resist corrosion, dust, and moisture, and support 0°C ~ 50°C operation and 6KV lightning protection. The 24-port gigabit managed switch supports 4 units of virtual stacking to deliver redundancy, simple management, and resilience of network capacity. Further, with Broadcom BCM56152 switch chip, S3910-24TF can provide 56 Gbps switching capacity and 42 Mpps forwarding rate to deliver higher network performance.

The switch supports simple device management and network management via Airware or a Command-Line Interface (CLI) as well as an on-box web UI. It delivers enhanced network security, network reliability, and operational efficiency, and is ideal for small businesses and branch offices. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility.
