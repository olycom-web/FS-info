### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 24 x PoE+ Ports @740W, with 2 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks and 2 x Combo SFP Ports, PicOS®, Support MLAG, Broadcom Chip

S3410-24TS-P, a next-gen switch featuring 24x 10/100/1000Mbps PoE ports with 740W total PoE budget and up to 30W per port, 2x 1/10Gb SFP+ uplink ports and 2x 1G RJ45/SFP combo ports. It delivers a cost-effective solution for small to medium-sized campus environments such as enterprise offices, educational institutions, and retail chains, enabling seamless connectivity for security cameras and Wi-Fi 6 access points.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG, OSPF, RIP and QoS with no additional licensing, while ensuring consistent control plane feature implementation. It also includes five years of hardware and software support.

Managed by [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html?now_cid=5513), it offers automated Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP), topology auto-discovery, and automated lifecycle management. A single centralized management dashboard automates day-N operations for faster scalability and end-to-end visibility — helping IT teams deploy and operate with confidence.
