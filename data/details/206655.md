### 48-Port Ethernet L3 PoE++ Switch, 36 x 2.5Gb PoE++ Ports, 12 x 10Gb PoE++ Ports @2100W, with 4 x 25Gb SFP28 and 2 x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Support EVPN-VXLAN, MLAG & Perpetual PoE, Broadcom Chip

The S5870-48MX6BC-U is a next-generation Layer 3 switch for scalable campus networks. Powered by a Broadcom chipset, it offers 36x 100M/1000M/2.5G and 12x 1G/2.5G/5G/10GBASE-T multi-gigabit ports with up to 90W PoE++ per port, plus 4x 25G SFP28 and 2x 100G QSFP28 uplinks. Designed for enterprise, education, healthcare, and regional campuses, it delivers multi-gigabit access and high-density PoE for Wi-Fi 7 APs and other powered devices. Perpetual & Fast PoE keeps devices powered during reboots.

Running on [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, it supports MLAG, BGP, OSPF, OpenFlow, and EVPN-VXLAN for service segmentation, resilient campus fabrics, and easy expansion without additional licensing. It includes 5-year hardware and software support.

Managed by the [AmpCon Management Software](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html), it enables automated ZTP, real-time telemetry, topology auto-discovery, lifecycle management, and centralized visibility for simplified wired and wireless network operations.
