### N5570-48S6C, 48-Port Ethernet Data Center Switch, 48 x 10Gb SFP+, with 6 x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Broadcom Trident 3 Chip

The N5570-48S6C is a high-performance leaf switch with perpetual license and 5-year service included. It offers 48x 10GbE downlinks and 6x 100GbE uplinks, delivering low-latency, full line-rate switching for modern data center networks. Featuring advanced Layer 2/Layer 3 features, VXLAN overlay, and Layer 3 gateway capabilities, it enables seamless connectivity between virtualized and bare-metal servers while efficiently handling east-west traffic.

Manage your switch and fabric with the AmpCon management software which automates and validates data center networks across the entire lifecycle to simplify design, deployment, and operations, reduce complexity, and boosting overall efficiency.
