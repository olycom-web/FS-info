### N5860-48SC, 48-Port Ethernet Data Center Switch, 48 x 10Gb SFP+, with 8 x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Broadcom Trident 3 Chip

The N5860-48SC is a high-performance leaf switch with perpetual license and 5-year service included. It offers low-latency, full line-rate switching with advanced Layer 2/Layer 3 features and VXLAN overlay support. With 48 10GbE downlinks and 8 100GbE uplinks, it delivers high-density connectivity and supports data center deployments.

The switch delivers the flexibility and features for big data, cloud, virtualized and traditional network designs and accommodates the myriad different applications and east-west traffic patterns found in modern data centers.
