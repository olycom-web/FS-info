### 48-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 48x 1Gb SFP, with 4x 10Gb SFP+, Support MPLS&MLAG

The S5800-48F4SR is a high-performance, secure, and easy-to-manage enterprise-grade Gigabit access switch with 48x 1Gb SFP access ports and 4x 10Gb uplink ports, providing flexible support for high-bandwidth services and diverse access requirements. It offers comprehensive Layer 2/3 capabilities, including MLAG/stacking, , MPLS & VXLAN-BGP-EVPN, and PTP, along with MACsec for point-to-point link encryption to ensure scalable performance and robust security for modern networks.

The S5800-48F4SR is equipped with hot-swappable, redundant power supplies and smart fans for enhanced resiliency. It supports simple device management and network management via a Command-Line Interface (CLI) as well as an on-box web UI, and it is ideal for high-density all-optical access and aggregation deployments across service providers, ISPs, enterprise campuses, and data centers.
