### S5570-48X, 48-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 48 x 10G RJ45, with 6 x 100G QSFP28 Uplinks, PicOS®, Broadcom Trident 3 Chip

The S5570-48X is a next-generation L3 switch featuring the Broadcom Trident 3 chip, with 2.16 Tbps switching capacity and 964 Mpps forwarding performance. It offers 48x 10GBase-T and 6x 100Gb QSFP28 uplinks in a 1U form factor, with redundant hot-swappable power supplies and smart fans for high reliability.

Running [PicOS®](https://www.fs.com/specials/picos-switch-software-167.html) with a perpetual license, the switch supports EVPN-VXLAN, MLAG, SSH, ACLs, OSPF, PBR, and sFlow without additional licensing and includes five years of hardware and software support. The S5570-48X is ideal for high-density 10G RJ45 terminal access in campus networks, SMB aggregation edges, and low-density data center ToR deployments.

The switch is managed by the [AmpCon-Campus management platform](https://www.fs.com/products/258677.html?now_cid=5513) offering Day 0 to Day 2+ automated lifecycle management with Zero Touch Provisioning (ZTP),template-based MLAG and IP Clos, topology discovery, and real-time telemetry. AmpCon-Campus automates workflows, simplifies operations, and improves visibility across connected devices.
