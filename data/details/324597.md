### IES5100W-8U8T4XD, 16-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Managed Industrial PoE+ Switch, 8 x PoE+ Ports @100W, 8 x 10/100/1000BASE-T M12, 2 x 10GBASE-T M12, with 2 x 10Gb Q-ODC

The IES5100W-8U8T4XD industrial switch features 8×10/100/1000BASE-T PoE+ ports, 8×10/100/1000BASE-T M12 ports, 2×10GBASE-T M12 ports, and 2×10G Q-ODC ports. The switch delivers up to 30W power per PoE+ port with 100W total power budget, reliably supporting devices like PTZ cameras and wireless APs. Compliant with EN 50155, this switch integrates rugged, vibration-proof M12/Q-ODC ports and an IP40 metal enclosure for reliable operation from -40°C to 70°C in railway and heavy industrial environments.

This Layer 3 managed industrial switch supports RIP/OSPF routing protocols, which can facilitate the deployment of applications across networks, making it ideal for medium or large-scale industrial aggregation networks.
