### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Switch, 24x Gigabit RJ45, with 4x 2.5Gb SFP Uplinks, Broadcom Chip, Fanless

S3270-24TM, the 24-port access switch is equipped with 24x 10/100/1000M RJ45 ports and 4x 2.5Gb SFP uplinks. This switch delivers 68 Gbps switching capacity and 50.59 Mpps forwarding rate. Fanless design ensures noise-free switch operation, reduced cost, and power consumption, and is ideal for use in quiet, small workspaces. The switch is easy to deploy and use with flexible management choices, allowing the best fit for your business and network environment.

Based on the high-performance hardware and FSOS platform, it supports functions such as OSPF, Voice VLAN, LACP, and QoS. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. These are simple, flexible, and secure switches that can connect to more terminals in HD monitoring scenarios and better cope with burst data.
