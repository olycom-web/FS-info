### N5580-32CB, 32-Port Ethernet Data Center Switch, 32 x 100Gb QSFP28, PicOS®, Broadcom Trident 3 Chip, Front-to-Back Airflow

The N5580-32CB is a compact 1U switch delivering high-density 40/100GbE for data center spine-leaf networks. Its 100GbE ports can be channelized into 4x 25GbE, and 40GbE ports can be channelized into 4x 10GbE via breakout cables, increasing the total number of 10/25GbE logical ports to 128. Powered by PicOS® with a perpetual license, it supports a rich set of Layer 2/Layer 3 features such as EVPN-VXLAN, MLAG, MPLS, and L3 gateway for leaf/spine roles in modern data center.

Manage your switch and fabric with the [AmpCon-DC Management Platform](https://www.fs.com/products/344455.html?now_cid=4227), which automates the entire network lifecycle to simplify design, deployment, and operations, reducing complexity and boosting overall efficiency.
