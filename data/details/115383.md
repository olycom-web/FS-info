### 28-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Switch, 20x 1Gb SFP, 8x 1G RJ45/SFP Combo Ports, with 4x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks, Stackable Switch, Broadcom Chip

The S5810-28FS is a next-generation stackable multi-service switch with 28x 1G SFP (including 8x combo ports) downlinks and 4x 10G SFP+ uplinks. Supporting up to 8-unit stacking, it delivers high-density access, seamless interconnection, and unified management. Powered by an advanced Broadcom chip with hot-swappable redundant power supplies and intelligent fans, it ensures stable operation and secure, reliable, scalable switching performance.

With high-performance hardware and comprehensive Layer 2/3 features, the S5810-28FS is ideal for access and aggregation in enterprise campuses, SMB core networks, and high-performance data center access. It provides long-distance, high-bandwidth, low-latency transmission for voice, video conferencing, and HD surveillance. Integrated with the Airware cloud platform, it enables rapid deployment and centralized management, improving network reliability, visibility, and operational efficiency.
