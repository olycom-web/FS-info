### 18-Port Ethernet L3 Half-Width Switch, 14 x 100Gb QSFP28, with 4 x 25G SFP28, AV over IP, Support MLAG

The S5460C-14C is a high-performance Layer 3 managed switch engineered for audio/video over IP, equipped with 14x 40/100G QSFP28 and 4x 10/25G SFP28 ports in a compact half-width form factor. This powerful switch provides 3.0 Tbps switching capacity and 4464 Mpps forwarding rate. It features two-node power redundancy, and includes intelligent fan speed control with a quiet design, ensuring an energy-efficient and eco-friendly network.

The switch delivers comprehensive L2/L3 features including OSPF, BGP, MLAG and VRRP, along with advanced data center capabilities such as PFC, ECN and RoCE, and offers simple and intuitive management via a Web GUI. It supports key AV-over-IP protocols such as Dante, SDVoE and SMPTE ST 2110, along with IGMP Snooping and Querier, Multicast, QoS, VLAN and PTP, making it ideal for professional audio and video networks.
