### 24-Port Ethernet L3 Switch, 20x 10Gb SFP+, with 4x 25Gb SFP28 and 2x 40Gb QSFP+ Uplinks, Support Stacking/MLAG, Broadcom Chip

The S5860-20SQ is a 24-port L3 managed stackable switch designed for large-scale campus network aggregations, small and medium-sized network cores, and high-speed transmission and long-distance connectivity. It features 20x 1G/10G ports, 4x 10G/25G SFP28 and 2x 40G QSFP+ (can be split into 4x 10G SFP+) uplinks, built-in Broadcom chip to deliver 760 Gbps switching capacity and 565 Mpps forwarding rate. Redundant hot-swappable power supplies and smart fans provide continuous operation and efficient cooling.

The switch supports virtual stacking or MLAG for flexible deployment and high-availability network architectures. It supports [Airware Cloud](https://airware-login.fs.com/?_gl=1*1uicw6s*_gcl_au*MTE3MzkxNzQ5MC4xNzgyMTAwNDIz) and provides a CLI and an intuitive on-box web UI for easy device and network management. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility.
