### IES5100-24FS, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ Managed Industrial Switch, 16 x 1Gb SFP, with 8 x 1Gb RJ45/SFP Combo and 4 x 10Gb SFP+

The IES5100-24FS features 16x 1G SFP ports, 8 x 1Gb Combo ports and 4 x 10G SFP+ ports, which is designed especially for a broad spectrum of industrial and extended enterprise connectivity needs. Equipped with an IP40 rated metal enclosure and fanless design, it can operate within a wide temperature range of -40 to 85°C (-40 to 185°F). This switch is ideal for harsh environments, and can be widely used in energy, transportation, industrial production, environmental protection, and industrial industry. This layer 2+ industrial managed switch supports Layer 3 static routing, RIP/OSPF dynamic routing, and VRRP virtual routing, ensuring continuous network operation with optimized routing and gateway redundancy.
