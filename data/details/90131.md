### S3400-24T4FP, 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 24 x PoE+ Ports @370W, with 4 x 1Gb Combo Uplinks, Support ERPS

24-Port Managed PoE+ Switch is equipped with 24x 10/100/1000BASE-T ports, 4x 1Gb RJ45/SFP combo, 24x RJ45 ports support both IEEE 802.3af PoE and IEEE 802.3at PoE+ (up to 30W per port) for powering attached IP phones, wireless access points, or other standards-compliant PoE and PoE+ end network devices. 4x RJ45/SFP combo ports meet the different network expand needs. This access switch delivers a compact, cost-effective solution for carrier's enterprise networks and IP MAN.

It supports advanced features such as OSPF, ACL, QinQ, and QoS. In addition, it supports SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus for enhanced network visibility. It also offers simple device and network management through a Command-Line Interface (CLI) and an intuitive on-box web UI.
