### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L3 Half-width Switch, 24 x 10Gb SFP+, with 2 x 100Gb QSFP28 Uplinks, AV over IP, Support MLAG

The S5850C-24S2C is a high-performance Layer 3 managed switch engineered for audio/video over IP, equipped with 24x 10Gb SFP+ ports and 2x 100Gb QSFP28 uplink ports in a compact half-width form factor. This powerful switch provides 880 Gbps switching capacity and 654.72 Mbps forwarding rate. It features 1 hot-swappable power supply, supports two-node power redundancy, and includes intelligent fan speed control with a quiet design, ensuring an energy-efficient and eco-friendly network.

The switch provides comprehensive L2/L3 features and supports flexible management through a user-friendly Web GUI and SNMP-based monitoring through AmpCon-Campus, enhancing network visibility. It also supports essential protocols like Dante, SMPTE ST 2110, IGMP Snooping & Querier, Multicast, QoS, VLAN and PTP TC, making it ideal for audio/video applications in fixed installations such as theaters, conference rooms, and theme parks, as well as mobile productions and live events.
