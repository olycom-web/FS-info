### 24-Port Gigabit Ethernet L2+ PoE+ Switch, 24 x PoE+ Ports@380W, with 4 x 10Gb SFP+ Uplinks

The S3240C-24P is a L2+ managed switch, equipped with 24x 1Gb RJ45 and 4x 10Gb SFP+ uplink ports support both IEEE 802.3at PoE+ (up to 30W per port) for powering attached Wi-Fi 6 access points, IP cameras or other network devices. It supports single or dual-switch assembly in 1U rack space, wall-mounted deployment, and single or dual-row placement without a cabinet, and can be flexibly deployed according to actual network needs. It adopts an intelligent fan speed control scheme to create a green energy-saving network.

The switch combines advanced hardware and software and supports features such as OSPF, Voice VLAN, LACP, and QoS, providing cost-effective services with unrivalled ease of use, making it an ideal choice for spaces such as cloud rooms in enterprise and SMB networks that require high flexibility in switch size.
