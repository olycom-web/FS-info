> ts:2026-09-11 · 来源:https://bdcomnetworks.com/news/224.html(ID 探测发现)

Happy New Year! As we welcome 2025 from the BDCOM R&D Center in Bangladesh, we extend our warmest wishes for a year filled with health, happiness, and success. May this new year bring fresh opportunities for innovation and collaboration as we continue to strive for excellence together.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826143737eahds2.jpg)

BDCOM established its Research and Development (R&D) branch in Bangladesh in April 2023, with a vision to promote innovation, teamwork, and technical excellence. This branch is dedicated to harnessing local talent to develop cutting-edge software solutions for a global audience.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826143744jj64dr.jpg)

Currently, the Bangladesh branch employs R&D engineers specializing in software development for embedded platforms. The team focuses on designing high-performance, reliable, and scalable solutions to address advanced networking needs. Additionally, the branch collaborates closely with BDCOM's R&D headquarters, working on projects and completing development tasks together. This collaboration ensures the delivery of high-quality solutions while fostering teamwork and knowledge transfer.

BDCOM R&D Bangladesh is actively recruiting fresh graduates from Computer Science (CS), Electrical and Computer Engineering (ECE), Electronics and Telecommunication Engineering (ETE), and related fields. The company regularly organizes campus recruitment programs at top universities across Bangladesh.

The work environment at BDCOM emphasizes creativity, collaboration, and inclusivity, encouraging employees to take ownership of their projects while supporting their personal and professional growth.
