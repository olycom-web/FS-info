# Sawasdee~ Congratulations on the Grand Opening of BDCOM Thailand Office!— /news/223.html

> ts:2026-09-11 · 来源:https://bdcomnetworks.com/news/223.html

**Bangkok, December 1, 2022:** BDCOM proudly hosted an Open Day event in Bangkok, Thailand, celebrating the official inauguration of our Thailand office. The new office not only showcases BDCOM's latest products, solutions, and technologies but also features a dedicated team of engineers to provide localized technical guidance and training. This initiative aims to deliver even more convenient and personalized services to global clients.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826143751wi62fo.jpg)

As Tao Ran, General Manager of BDCOM International Department, noted, "BDCOM has always prioritized customer-centricity, striving for mutual growth and success with our clients. The establishment of BDCOM Thailand marks a fresh beginning that promises to elevate customer experiences and better serve our local customers."

BDCOM ventured into overseas markets as early as 2007. Our products have been widely adopted by international operators, transportation, power, security, enterprises, and various other sectors. The year 2018 witnessed BDCOM's success in securing projects worth over 100 million RMB in India, marking the initiation of the Belt and Road Initiative's overseas expansion. Over the past decade, BDCOM's relentless commitment and outstanding efforts have enabled our products to reach across Asia-Pacific, Europe, South America, Africa, and beyond, covering more than 100 countries.

With 30 years of technological expertise and profound market insights, BDCOM has continuously received accolades for its exceptional product quality and service excellence.

As further strategizes the overseas marketing network and deepens the localized international services, BDCOM remains committed to advancing towards new horizons.
