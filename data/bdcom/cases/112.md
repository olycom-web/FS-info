**Background:**

Indonesia, with its rapidly growing digital economy, has experienced an increasing demand for high-speed internet connectivity. The need for reliable, high-performance internet has become critical, especially in cities like Bekasi, Bandung, Lombok and other cities where businesses, education, and home entertainment services are flourishing. To address this demand, GPON OLT along with WiFi ONU solutions are deployed to bring fiber-optic internet directly to home users, ensuring seamless connectivity.

**Objectives:**

- Show how GPON OLT and WiFi ONU technology improved internet connectivity in those cities
- Highlight how the solution met the growing need for fast, reliable internet.
- Explain the improvements in speed, reliability, and capacity for home users.
- Show how this technology improved everyday life by making internet access better.
- Emphasize how the network can easily expand to handle more users in the future.

**Results:**

- Improved Internet Speeds: Users in those cities reported significantly improved internet speeds, enabling smoother video streaming, faster downloads, and more reliable online gaming.
- Increased Reliability: The fiber-based infrastructure reduced the frequency of outages and downtime, leading to higher customer satisfaction.
- Scalability for Future Growth: The deployment of GPON OLT and WiFi ONU offers a future-proof solution that can be easily scaled to meet the growing demand for internet access as more users in Indonesia seek high-speed connectivity.
