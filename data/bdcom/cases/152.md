**Project Overview**

As intelligent manufacturing and green production continue to evolve, the security infrastructure at FAW Toyota's Tianjin New Energy Plant faced the need for a comprehensive upgrade. The plant required a highly reliable, scalable, and energy-efficient next-generation network capable of supporting massive high-definition video data. The goal was to achieve unified, intelligent, and visualized management of production safety, environmental protection, and energy operations across the entire facility.

**Core Solution**

To meet these requirements, BDCOM designed an integrated architecture centered on high-performance core switching with deep PON network coverage. At the core layer, the carrier-grade S8510E core switch serves as the powerful "network brain," delivering 10G backbone switching and high-speed data forwarding to ensure robust system performance and stability. At the access layer, a GPON-based network architecture was deployed. Through passive optical splitters, fiber connections extend directly to Gigabit PoE MDUs (such as the GP1702 series) distributed across plant areas. This "Fiber-to-the-Edge" design:

- Significantly simplifies physical cabling
- Reduces failure points
- Integrates data transmission and PoE power delivery over a single fiber link
- Provides stable, flexible, and centrally managed "Fiber-in, Copper-out" connectivity

The result is a fully integrated intelligent perception and security network spanning from core to edge, supporting HD surveillance cameras, intelligent enclosure controllers, and other front-end terminals across the plant.
