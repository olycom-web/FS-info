### Background

The José Celestino Mutis Botanical Garden, located in Bogotá, Colombia, is the country's largest botanical research and conservation center. It plays a vital role in scientific research, biodiversity conservation, environmental education, and public recreation. To support its modernization initiative, the garden required a reliable and scalable network infrastructure capable of supporting campus-wide surveillance and wireless connectivity.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144604oxch7p.webp)

### Customer Requirements

The Botanical Garden required a modern network infrastructure to:

- Provide reliable wired connectivity across the entire campus.
- Enable comprehensive IP surveillance coverage for improved security.
- Support campus-wide wireless network deployment.
- Ensure high network availability for daily operations.
- Build a scalable infrastructure for future expansion

### Solution

- High-performance aggregation and core switching for reliable campus backbone connectivity.
- PoE-enabled Gigabit access switches to power IP surveillance cameras.
- High-speed uplink architecture ensuring efficient traffic aggregation.
- Stable wired infrastructure supporting campus-wide wireless access deployment.
- Scalable network design allowing future expansion without major infrastructure changes.
- Enterprise-grade switching platform delivering reliable and continuous network operation.

The deployed BDCOM solution enabled the Botanical Garden to achieve:

- Complete wired network coverage across the entire campus.
