##### Background

Islami Bank Foundation (lBF) Hospital is a social welfare-oriented organization created by lslami Bank Bangladesh Limited for the uplift and emancipation of the helpless and downtrodden people of the society

They were looking for a cost effective, performance-oriented and one stop WiFi solution. The network administrator felt the BDCOM was the right choice for them.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/202608261446552o1su5.png)

##### Challenges

Medical equipment, other wireless networks, and electronic devices can cause significant interference with the WiFi signal, reducing the network's performance. Integrating the new WiFi solution with existing IT infrastructure and medical devices were another big challenge for IBF.
