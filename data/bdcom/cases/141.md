**PROJECT BACKGROUND**

Total floors: 12 stories

Gross floor area: 57,696 m²

Mingguang City Emergency Hospital has a total construction area of about 57,000 m², covering the Emergency & Outpatient Complex, Inpatient Building, Mental Health Building, and an underground garage. In response to national healthcare reform and the trend toward hospital digitalization, the project targets a modern digital hospital that is intelligent, efficient, and high-quality. Through smart system integration and an information platform, it improves medical service quality, optimizes patient journeys, enables refined operations management, and aims to build a region-leading smart healthcare center.

**SOLUTION & TOPOLOGY**

Build a High-Performance Network Architecture

Using BDCOM S2500 Series access switches together with the existing core switches, the design creates three physically isolated networks—intranet, internet, and device network—to meet the high-bandwidth, high-reliability transmission needs of clinical services, internet access, and security devices, ensuring data security and stable system operation.

Strengthen Security and Behavior Management

Deploy BDCOM F5100 firewall and F5500 behavior management appliances to realize perimeter protection and web behavior control. This ensures compliant medical data flows, prevents external attacks and internal unauthorized access, and supports MLPS (Class III) requirements.

Wireless Coverage & Intelligent O&M

With BDCOM WAP2100 Series APs and WSC6100 wireless controllers, the solution delivers seamless-roaming WLAN that supports mobile healthcare and IoT device access. Coupled with an intelligent integration platform, it enables unified management of security, energy, and equipment systems, enhancing overall user experience.
