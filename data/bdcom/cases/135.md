**Background**

- The first 4F-class civil international airport in Central China
- One of the eight major transportation hub airports in China
- An airport with scheduled international flights
- A Class I aviation port open to foreign countries
- Reaching 12 countries in 4 continents
- 158,400㎡ terminal 2
- 495,000㎡ terminal 3
- 152 civil aviation apron stands
- 404,000 aircraft movements capacity
- 63 million passengers/year

**Solution**

The T3 terminal of Wuhan Tianhe International Airport features a spacious interior and heavy foot traffic, placing much higher demands on wireless concurrency and signal coverage compared to typical scenarios.

As the terminal handles international flights, a large number of inbound travelers may not have access to 4G/5G networks upon arrival, making reliable Wi-Fi connectivity essential.

To ensure efficient travel and intelligent airport operations for global passengers, BDCOM has deployed a full-scenario wireless network architecture throughout the T3 terminal.

**Comprehensive and Precise Coverage**

Over 1,800 dual-band BDCOM APs were deployed with scenario-based customization.

High-density models WAP2100-T630-V2 and T654 were installed in passenger service areas such as check-in counters, security checkpoints, departure lounges, retail zones, corridors, and parking lots — ensuring seamless performance under massive user concurrency.

In office areas, models like WAP2100-T618, W618-V2, and W630-V2 were used to achieve zero blind spots.

**Intelligent Cloud-Based Management**

The WSC6100-NC256 wireless controller centrally manages and dispatches all APs across the network, while the BAS2000-10K gateway provides a 10G uplink, supporting peak data surges from an annual passenger flow of 63 million.

The solution delivers excellent results in overall wireless coverage and seamless roaming performance.
