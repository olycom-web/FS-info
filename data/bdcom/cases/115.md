**Background:**

Al SSAD, a well-established food and beverage supplier based in Baghdad, Iraq, holds a significant market share in the region and operates extensive warehouse facilities that play a crucial role in its supply chain. Given the high temperatures of Baghdad's climate, the operational demands of the warehouse, and the need for reliable network infrastructure, Al SSAD sought a robust and temperature-resilient networking solution. That's why an advanced network solution was deployed with BDCOM for delivering real-time monitoring capabilities and enhancing the reliability of Al SSAD's operations.

**Challenges:**

- Extreme Temperature Conditions: Baghdad can reach temperatures up to 50°C, requiring all outdoor network equipment to be robustly designed to withstand extreme heat and operate reliably.
- Power Reliability: Ensuring continuous power is critical, so all devices needed to be equipped with reliable, high-quality power supplies.
- Network Reliability: The warehouse operations needed a network with redundant loop configurations to prevent downtime and ensure uninterrupted connectivity.
- Low-Latency Requirements: Al SSAD required low-latency networking to support real-time video monitoring, enabling management to oversee warehouse activities and enhance security with minimal delay.

**Results:**

- Temperature-Resilient Operation: Al SSAD's network infrastructure now operates smoothly under Baghdad's extreme heat conditions, ensuring that temperature fluctuations do not impact the reliability or performance of the network.
- Increased Network Reliability: With redundant loop configurations and honor【原文如此】power supplies, Al SSAD's network is highly resilient, ensuring seamless operations and reducing the risk of outages.
- Real-Time Video Monitoring Capability: Low-latency networking allows for real-time video monitoring, enhancing security and enabling effective warehouse management with minimal delay.
- Scalability and Future Expansion: The modular design of the core chassis switch and the flexibility of SFP ports allow for easy network expansion as Al SSAD's operations grow, providing a future-proof solution.
