**Background**

The Instituto Federal de Minas Gerais (IFMG) is one of Brazil's leading federal public education institutions, operating over 20 campuses across the state of Minas Gerais. In line with its digital transformation and smart campus strategy, IFMG launched a large-scale initiative to modernize its campus network infrastructure and deploy a centralized surveillance system. The objective was to strengthen academic and administrative connectivity, enhance campus security monitoring, and prepare for the future integration of IoT devices and smart learning platforms.

**Challenges**

- High-Performance Connectivity Needs: The network had to deliver reliable gigabit access and high-speed backbone connectivity to classrooms, laboratories, administrative offices, and student dormitories.
- Centralized Surveillance Deployment: A scalable PoE-based system was required to power and connect IP cameras and wireless access points, ensuring simplified installation and reduced cabling complexity.
- Network Segmentation & Security: IFMG needed a multi-VLAN architecture to securely separate academic, administrative, and guest traffic while maintaining ease of management.
- Future Scalability: The solution needed to be modular, ensuring smooth expansion across additional campuses as the institution continues its modernization efforts.

**Solutions Provided**

BDCOM designed and delivered a comprehensive and scalable campus network solution that addressed IFMG's requirements:

Aggregation Layer: Deployed BDCOM S2900-24T4X and S2900-48T6X switches as aggregation nodes, providing Gigabit downlink and 10G uplink for high-speed, redundant backbone connectivity between buildings and campuses.

Access & PoE Layer: Installed BDCOM S2900-24P4X and S2900-48P6X PoE switches in key areas such as classrooms, labs, monitoring centers, and entry points. These devices enabled seamless data and power delivery for IP cameras and wireless access points over a single cable, reducing deployment time and cost.

Network Architecture: Implemented a multi-VLAN architecture to separate academic, administrative, and guest traffic, ensuring security, traffic optimization, and efficient management.

Scalability & Reliability: Designed the infrastructure to be modular and scalable, ensuring operational continuity and easy expansion to other IFMG campuses in the future.

**Outcome**

- Reliable, high-speed connectivity was successfully established across selected IFMG campuses.
- The surveillance system with PoE support simplified installation and enhanced campus safety monitoring.
- The segregated VLAN architecture ensured secure, efficient handling of academic, administrative, and guest traffic.
- The solution is scalable and future-ready, supporting integration of IoT devices, e-learning platforms, and further digital services.
