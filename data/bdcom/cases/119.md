#### Project Application Scenario:

China Wireless Valley is located in the southern part of Jiangning Development Zone in the city of Nanjing, with a core area covering 4.6 square kilometers. It is a national innovation base for communication and network industries, supported by the Ministry of Science and Technology, the Ministry of Education, and the Jiangsu Provincial Government. Wireless Valley has attracted more than 200 high-tech enterprises and has established the China Future Network Industry Innovation Alliance, led by the Future Network Innovation Institute, with participation from organizations like the China Communication Standards Association, China Mobile, CCTV, Tencent, and Tsinghua University, among others. The entire park is covered by wireless networks, consisting of a core building and six industrial buildings, which include the Network Innovation Institute, Southeast University Graduate Research Base, wireless communication enterprises, and leisure and entertainment areas. The park accommodates over 10,000 people, with approximately 800 wireless access points per building and 150 outdoor access points.

**Network Topology:**

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144519t65p1z.png)

The core layer of the entire network utilizes the BDCOM S8500 switch, developed by BDCOM, with advanced distributed multi-level switching matrix architecture. The switch is equipped with the BDROS operating system, which integrates high-performance L2/L3/L4 line-speed switching services with features such as IPv6, MPLS VPN, network security, traffic analysis, and virtualization. The access layer in the industrial buildings uses BDCOM S2500 series switches, with fiber connectivity to the aggregation layer and gigabit access to the desktop. Dual-frequency, high-performance access points (APs) are deployed for wireless coverage. The network is managed and maintained through BDCOM's powerful integrated network management platform, BroadDirector.
