### Background

PT Cendikia Global Solusi (CGS) is a leading fiber optic infrastructure provider in Indonesia, operating extensive fiber networks across major cities. The company delivers wholesale fiber infrastructure and network capacity services, enabling ISPs to expand rapidly without significant capital investment. Its shared infrastructure model helps reduce deployment costs while accelerating broadband service availability.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144457d9u72m.jpg)

### Challenges

As CGS continued expanding its infrastructure business, the company required a network solution that could deliver:

- High-capacity and scalable fiber access infrastructure
- Reliable long-term performance with minimal maintenance
- Cost-effective deployment and operation
- Compliance with Indonesian government regulations and certification requirements
- Flexible architectur suitable for wholesale capacity and infrastructure services
- Strong return on investment (ROI)

### Solution

To address these requirements, CGS selected the BDCOM Passive Optical Network (PON) Solution together with Carrier Ethernet Switching Solutions to build a scalable, reliable, and future-ready wholesale fiber access network.

- Deployed Carrier Ethernet switches for interconnection, core, and distribution layers, providing a high-performance and resilient transport network.
- Implemented the PON solution using OLT and WiFi HGU ONU to deliver shared fiber access for multiple ISPs.
- Delivered a high-performance, scalable, and reliable network platform with low operational and maintenance costs.
- Optimized CAPEX and OPEX through a cost-effective PON architecture and centralized network management.
- Ensured compliance with Indonesian regulatory requirements while providing a future-ready infrastructure for capacity service expansion

The BDCOM solution enabled PT Cendikia Global Solusi to strengthen its position as one of Indonesia's leading fiber infrastructure and capacity service providers.
