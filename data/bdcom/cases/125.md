**Background:**

Noble House is a Grade A office building strategically located in Mega Kuningan, a prestigious business district in South Jakarta. As a premium-grade office space, it benefits from a synergistic relationship with Oakwood Apartment Services, offering integrated facilities and services. The building's surroundings include luxury accommodations such as Ritz Carlton, JW Marriott, and Oakwood Premier Cozmo, as well as major shopping centers like Lotte Shopping Avenue, Mall Ambassador, and Mall Kuningan City. This prime location and its world-class amenities demand a robust and reliable network infrastructure to meet the high expectations of tenants and visitors.

**Project Demands:**

The project's primary objective was to replace the obsolete GPON equipment with a reliable and scalable solution. The absence of local support for their existing equipment in Indonesia left Noble House with no choice but to seek new technology that could meet their requirements. Key demands included:

- Continuity and Reliability: The new OLT must ensure seamless service delivery with backup capabilities to support operational continuity.
- Scalability: ONUs were required to accommodate new customers as Noble House expands its tenant base.
- Proven Performance: The replacement equipment needed to pass rigorous Proof of Concept (POC) testing to validate its suitability for triple-play services.

**Solutions:**

To address these demands, Noble House deployed BDCOM's GP3600-16B OLT and GP1705-2GV ONU. These products were selected based on their ability to meet the specific needs of a Grade A office building. The solution provides:

- Advanced GPON Technology: The GP3600-16B OLT offers high bandwidth and low latency, ensuring optimal delivery of Internet, voice, and IPTV services at a competitive cost.
- Customer-Oriented Scalability: The GP1705-2GV ONU is equipped to support new customers for high speed internet with dual-band WiFi and IPTV capabilities, ensuring seamless integration for future expansions.
- Local Support: By choosing BDCOM, Noble House ensures reliable local distribution and technical support, reducing downtime and operational risks.
