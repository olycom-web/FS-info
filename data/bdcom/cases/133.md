**Background**

Salemba Apartment Residence is a premium residential complex in Central Jakarta's prestigious Menteng area, surrounded by government offices, shopping centres, hospitals, and top schools. To meet residents' growing demand for fast, reliable internet, the developer upgraded from an outdated copper network to a modern fiber optic infrastructure, ensuring future-ready connectivity and a higher standard of living.

**Challenges**

- Delivering high-speed, stable internet access to every apartment unit.
- Providing dual-band WiFi coverage (2.4GHz & 5GHz) for optimal wireless performance.
- Supporting 1Gbps LAN port capacity in each unit.
- Enabling optional voice services for residents.
- Ensuring the network design was scalable, secure, and easy to manage.

**Solution**

To meet these requirements, Netstar deployed a Fiber To The Office(FTTO) solution using BDCOM's GPON technology, known for its reliability and scalability.

1. BDCOM GP3600-08B — Optical Line Terminal (OLT)
   - Compliant with ITU-T G.984/G.988 standards.
   - Supports uplink speeds of 1.25Gbps and downlink speeds of 2.5Gbps.
   - High splitting ratio of up to 1:128, capable of serving up to 1024 subscribers.
   - Efficient bandwidth utilization and robust Ethernet service support.
2. BDCOM GP1705-2GV — Optical Network Unit (ONU/HGU)
   - Smart Home Gateway Unit with integrated dual-band WiFi and VoIP functionality.
   - Supports 2.4GHz and 5GHz bands for optimal wireless connectivity.
   - Provides 1Gbps LAN port for wired connections.
   - Advanced network features: VLAN, STP, port isolation, ACL, QoS, and broadcast storm control for enhanced security and performance.

**Results**

- Ultra-Fast Internet: High-speed fiber connectivity to each unit, enabling smooth streaming, remote work, and smart home applications.
- Superior Wireless Experience: Dual-band WiFi delivers broader coverage and better performance.
- Ready Voice Services: Integrated VoIP support for residents who require voice services.
- Scalable and Future-Proof: Easy to expand as the number of users or bandwidth requirements increase.
- Reliable and Secure: Advanced network management features ensure secure, stable, and efficient operations.
