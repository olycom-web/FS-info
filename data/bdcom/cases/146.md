**Project Background**

Linzhou County People's Hospital is a pilot unit of the Lhasa Medical and Health Information Platform in Tibet Autonomous Region, undertaking core county-level medical services. With the overall relocation to a new campus, the hospital expanded to approximately 76,660 square meters with 220 beds and added multiple functional areas such as an infectious disease prevention complex and a Tibetan medicine specialty center. To meet the standardized construction requirements of a Class II Grade A hospital, a stable and efficient network covering the entire hospital was required to support smart medical management systems, telemedicine collaboration, and data interconnection, ultimately achieving a modern medical service integrating routine and emergency care.

**Solution**

Based on the hospital's high-concurrency and multi-scenario requirements, BDCOM designed a hierarchical and highly reliable network architecture. This solution enabled an intelligent network environment for both the hospital intranet and dedicated service networks, laying a solid digital foundation for the hospital's smart healthcare system.

**Topology**

Core Layer

A dual-core architecture is built with high-performance core switches. 10G optical modules are used for backbone interconnection, ensuring high-speed data exchange between the data center and each building.

Aggregation & Access Layer

All-gigabit aggregation switches connect various service areas, while multiple access switches are deployed to provide flexible port access and service isolation for outpatient departments, inpatient wards, and the infection building.

Wireless Coverage

High-density wireless access points (APs) are deployed to enable seamless roaming across the entire hospital, effectively supporting mobile medical services.

Security Protection

A next-generation firewall is deployed to establish a secure network boundary, meeting China's Grade Protection 2.0 (MLPS 2.0) requirements and ensuring the security of medical data.

Surveillance Network

An independent surveillance network is deployed. Dedicated surveillance switches provide stable and reliable transmission for the security system.
