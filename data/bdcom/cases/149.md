**Project Overview**

As the strategic cornerstone of Jiudian Pharmaceutical's "second entrepreneurship" and "leapfrog growth" strategy, the newly built high-end formulation R&D and manufacturing industrial park located in the Liuyang Economic Development Zone covers an area of approximately 69,333 square meters, with a planned total construction area of 200,000 square meters. This modern industrial campus integrates research and development, pharmaceutical manufacturing, and intelligent logistics, and is designed to become a leading intelligent pharmaceutical production base in China, supporting Jiudian Pharmaceutical's ambition to achieve hundreds of millions in annual output value. Realizing end-to-end digital and automated management, from R&D to production, is the key to ensuring the park's long-term efficient operation and sustaining its core competitiveness in the future.

**Core Solution**

To support the construction of Jiudian Pharmaceutical's intelligent factory in Liuyang, BDCOM designed a network solution centered on intelligent manufacturing. The solution establishes a secure and robust network boundary while deeply integrating a high-performance wired network with full-scenario wireless coverage, ensuring high-speed, reliable data transmission from production workshops to warehousing and logistics systems. Through a centralized intelligent management platform, unified and visualized operations and maintenance of all network devices and terminals are achieved. This provides a simple yet powerful digital foundation for intelligent production, flexible expansion, and long-term scalability of the factory.
