**A great summer getaway to fully enjoy the charm of Chengde, the Mountain City.**

The Zhaofeng International Hotel in Chengde covers a total building area of 120,630 square meters, with a green space rate of 30%. The hotel offers 479 rooms and 6 meeting rooms of varying sizes, capable of hosting up to 2,000 people. It is a five-star hotel connected to a four-star hotel, with integrated entertainment facilities provided simultaneously.

**An innovative two-layer and point-to-multi-point architecture, designed for energy efficiency and environmental sustainability.**

BDCOM uses the carrier-grade OLT P8510, ensuring a stable and robust network. The GP1704 integrates wireless and voice functions, with fiber optic connections directly reaching hotel rooms, corridors, banquet halls, leisure and entertainment areas, and other usage scenarios. This overall design effectively reduces wiring for customers, minimizes the need for floor-based server rooms and outdoor cabinets, and lowers the deployment complexity and cost of the entire network.
