# Ensuring Business Continuity for Zentara with a Robust DRC Solution— /case/159.html

> ts:2026-09-11 · 来源:https://bdcomnetworks.com/case/159.html

### Background

To ensure business continuity and data protection, PT Mars Bumi Indonesia (Zentara) leveraged the NDC Moratelindo Data Recovery Center, a high-availability and carrier-neutral facility. The data center provides reliable infrastructure with robust connectivity and redundancy, making it ideal for Disaster Recovery Center (DRC) deployment. By utilizing this facility, Zentara aimed to secure critical workloads and maintain uninterrupted operations.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144505nanhw0.jpg)

### Challenges

Zentara faced several technical and operational challenges while implementing the DRC solution:

- Ensuring zero data loss (RPO = 0) and minimal recovery time (low RTO)
- Establishing a high-speed, stable connection between office and data center over a 40 km distance
- Maintaining network redundancy to avoid single points of failure
- Supporting high-bandwidth server synchronization using 10G connectivity
- Ensuring cost-effective deployment without compromising performance

### Solution Provided

To address these requirements, Zentara deployed a robust and efficient network architecture using BDCOM S5612E switches:

- High-Performance Switching: Deployed higher switching capacity and data forwarding rate along with 10G uplink switches high-speed data transmission and flexible connectivity
- Redundant Fiber Connectivity: Integration of two separate third-party fiber optic links between the office and data center to ensure path redundancy.
- Link Aggregation (LACP): Configuration of Link Aggregation Control Protocol (LACP) to combine multiple fiber links, improving bandwidth utilization and providing automatic failover.
- Long-Distance Connectivity: Reliable 10G connectivity established across a 40 km, ensuring seamless real-time data replication between sites.
- Optimized for DRC Operations: The infrastructure supports hot-standby servers and real-time synchronization, critical for disaster recovery operations.

### Results & Benefits

The implemented solution delivered significant improvements in Zentara's IT infrastructure:

- Enhanced Business Continuity: Reliable DRC system ensures uninterrupted operations during unexpected failures.
- Near-Zero Downtime: LACP-based redundancy minimizes service disruption.
- High-Speed Data Replication: 10G connectivity enables efficient and real-time data backup.
- Improved Network Reliability: Dual fiber providers eliminate single points of failure.
- Cost-Effective Performance: BDCOM S5612E provides enterprise-grade features at an optimized cost.
- Scalable Infrastructure: The solution allows future expansion to accommodate growing data demands.
