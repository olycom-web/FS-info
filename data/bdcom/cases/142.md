**Background**

In recent years, Georgia has experienced a rapid rise in demand for high-speed Internet and digital connectivity. As one of the most progressive nations in the Caucasus region, Georgia's economy and society increasingly rely on stable broadband access, smart home applications, online education, and digital entertainment. However, many areas across the country especially in suburban and rural regions, still face challenges such as:

- Limited access to high-speed and stable Internet
- Insufficient wireless coverage within homes
- Legacy network infrastructure that cannot support modern bandwidth needs

To address these gaps, BDCOM provided GPON solutions along with WiFi ONU at customer premise.

**Solution Overview**

- High-speed data transmission up to 1 Gbps per user
- Passive optical structure, reducing maintenance and power costs
- Reliable and future-ready architecture, ideal for both residential and business users
- Simplified management with BDCOM's advanced network management system

This deployment marked a major step in enhancing Georgia's digital infrastructure, enabling faster, more stable Internet access to a broader population.

**Results and Impact**

- High-speed connectivity reaching new residential and commercial areas
- Stable WiFi performance even in high-density urban environments
- Reduced operational and maintenance costs through a passive design
- Future scalability, allowing easy expansion as demand grows
- Enhanced national connectivity, contributing to Georgia's digital development goals

**Products Used**

- GP1704-2F-E (Dual-Band WiFi ONU, MOQ 3,000 units)
- GP3600-08B-MC (8-Port GEPON OLT)
- GP3600-16B-MC (16-Port GEPON OLT)
