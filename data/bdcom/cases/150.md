**Background**

The Doha Exhibition & Convention Center (DECC) is a world-class events venue located in the heart of Doha, Qatar. It hosts international exhibitions, conventions, conferences, and large-scale corporate events, supporting Qatar's vision of becoming a global business and cultural hub.

Featuring modern architecture, advanced facilities, and flexible event spaces, DECC offers excellent accessibility to hotels, transportation hubs, and major city attractions. As a host to high-profile global events, the venue requires a reliable, secure, and scalable networking infrastructure to ensure seamless operations.

**Business Challenge**

As DECC continued to attract larger and more technology-driven events, the demand on its existing network infrastructure increased significantly. The venue required a network solution capable of:

- Supporting high-density user environments during large exhibitions
- Delivering high-speed and stable connectivity for exhibitors, organizers, and visitors
- Ensuring reliable performance for critical applications, digital displays, and event management systems
- Providing scalability and flexibility to adapt to different event sizes and formats
- Enhancing overall network security and operational reliability

To address these challenges, DECC selected BDCOM switching solutions with S3900 and S2900 Series Switches.

**Solution's Key Features**

- High-Speed Data Transmission: Ensured smooth connectivity for large numbers of simultaneous users and bandwidth-intensive applications.
- Improved Network Reliability: Stable switching performance minimized downtime during major events and exhibitions.
- Scalability & Flexibility: The network can easily adapt to varying event requirements, from small conferences to large trade shows.
- Enhanced Security: Advanced switching features help protect network traffic and critical event systems.
- Operational Efficiency: Simplified network management supports faster deployment and reconfiguration for different events.

**Results & Benefits**

Following the implementation of BDCOM S3900 and S2900 switches, DECC experienced measurable improvements in network performance and operational reliability:

- Seamless and uninterrupted connectivity during high-profile international events
- Improved user experience for exhibitors, event organizers, and visitors
- A future-ready network infrastructure capable of supporting emerging technologies
- Reduced risk of network congestion and performance bottlenecks
- Strengthened DECC's position as a technologically advanced, global-standard events venue
