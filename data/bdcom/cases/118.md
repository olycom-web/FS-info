**Background:**

JYSK Nest, a prominent shopping mall in Užice, Serbia, serves as a key retail hub, offering a variety of shopping and entertainment experiences to locals and visitors alike. To keep pace with the demands of modern retail environments, JYSK Nest required a robust network infrastructure to support its expanding connectivity needs, including high-quality surveillance and operational efficiency.

**Objectives:**

- Enhance Network Reliability: To provide consistent, uninterrupted connectivity for essential systems like IP cameras, digital signage, and POS terminals throughout the mall.
- Simplify Installation and Power Distribution: To implement a PoE solution that combines data and power in a single infrastructure, reducing the complexity of installation and eliminating the need for separate power sources for connected devices.
- Improve Security Monitoring: To enable real-time monitoring and efficient management of the mall's surveillance system, enhancing safety for both retailers and customers.
- Scalability for Future Expansion: To establish a scalable network foundation that supports future growth and the integration of additional devices without extensive reconfiguration.
- Optimize Operational Efficiency: To create a high-performance, easily managed network infrastructure that minimizes downtime, allowing JYSK Nest to maintain smooth, secure, and reliable mall operations.

**Results:**

- Streamlined Power and Connectivity: The PoE switches consolidated power and data connectivity, reducing the need for extra power outlets and cables, which simplified the setup and reduced operational costs.
- Enhanced Security and Operations: By powering IP cameras and providing a stable network for security surveillance systems, the solution improved safety and operational oversight within the shopping mall.
- Scalability and Flexibility: The network infrastructure supports easy addition of new devices as mall operations grow, providing a flexible foundation for future upgrades.
- Improved Network Management: The mall's IT team now benefits from centralized management, allowing for real-time monitoring and quick issue resolution, thus enhancing the reliability of retail operations.
