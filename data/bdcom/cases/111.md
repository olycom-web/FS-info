**Background:**

Noble International School (NIS) is a renowned educational institution in Qatar, dedicated to modernize its network infrastructure to support its expanding student body and staff, while also ensuring robust connectivity for modern learning methods, including online resources and smart classroom technologies. The school required a seamless WiFi connectivity and secure network that could handle increased traffic, multiple devices, and provide Power over Ethernet (PoE) capability for various devices.

**Challenges:**

Noble International School faced several challenges related to their network infrastructure:

- The need for high-speed, reliable connectivity across the entire campus.
- Support for both wired and wireless devices used by students, teachers, and administrative staff.
- Powering devices like IP phones, cameras, and access points without needing separate power lines.
- Ensuring seamless network management for smooth operations and future scalability.

**Results and Benefits:**

- Enhanced Connectivity: The school now enjoys high-speed internet across all areas, improving the learning experience for students and staff.
- Cost Efficiency: PoE technology reduced the cost of installing separate power lines for devices, simplifying infrastructure and saving installation time.
- Scalability: The network infrastructure was designed to support future growth, with the flexibility to add more access points or upgrade to higher speeds as the school's needs evolve.
- Centralized Cloud Management: The cloud-based Access Controller allows real-time monitoring and centralized management of all APs. The IT staff can manage configurations, apply security policies, and troubleshoot remotely, improving efficiency and reducing response times.
- Improved Security: With the Access Controller, security policies such as guest network separation and user access control can be easily implemented, ensuring the network remains secure and compliant with data protection regulations.
