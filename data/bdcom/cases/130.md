**Project Background:**

Shanghai University of International Business and Economics (SUIBE), founded in 1960, is known as "the cradle of talents for China's international trade and economics." The university has three campuses—Songjiang, Changning, and Minhang—with a total area of approximately 670,000 square meters and over 14,000 enrolled students.

In recent years, the classrooms in the Xuesi Building have not been renovated for a long time, resulting in outdated and aging facilities that can no longer meet the functional needs of the university's evolving academic positioning.

**BDCOM Smart Campus Solution:**

BDCOM has built a 10-Gigabit IPv6 campus network for the university, upgrading the information infrastructure and classroom environment of the Xuesi Building. The project involved the renovation of 112 classrooms and one centralized monitoring and command center.

The network solution adopts a clearly layered design based on a standard three-tier architecture: core + aggregation + access, which ensures ease of expansion and maintenance. This hierarchical deployment significantly improves the network's scalability, availability, and manageability.

With BDCOM's support, the newly built smart classrooms at Shanghai University of International Business and Economics now provide seamless wired and wireless integrated network services, offering a modern teaching and learning environment for all students and faculty. The solution not only reconstructs the educational space by integrating technology into teaching but also accelerates the digital transformation of campus education. Empowered by advanced technologies, the university is innovating its talent cultivation model and enhancing overall learning experiences.
