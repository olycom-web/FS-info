**Project Overview**

The Huaneng Shanshan 2.35 GW integrated concentrated solar power (CSP) and photovoltaic (PV) project is located deep in the Gobi Desert of Turpan. It is a large-scale clean energy base that combines CSP energy storage with PV power generation. Facing an extreme environment characterized by high temperatures, strong winds and sand, a vast site area, and widely distributed equipment, the project required a highly reliable, full-coverage, and remotely manageable communication network to support production monitoring, data acquisition, and mobile inspection, ensuring the stable delivery of green power.

**Core Solution**

To address the harsh environment of the Gobi power plant and its multi-service requirements, BDCOM deployed high-performance core switches at the core layer to build a redundant 10GE backbone. Dual-device hot standby ensures uninterrupted service continuity, while the server cluster connects to the core over 10GE links to guarantee high-speed data processing. At the aggregation layer, 10GE aggregation switches are deployed by functional area, connecting upward to the core and extending downward to each operational zone. At the access layer, deployment is tailored to different scenarios. In the PV array area and CSP collector field, industrial-grade switches are used to provide wide-temperature tolerance, dust resistance, and ring-network redundancy, ensuring reliable connectivity for data acquisition terminals. In the central control office area, Gigabit access switches deliver high-speed network access. The wireless network is centrally managed by an AC controller with outdoor industrial APs, enabling full-site wireless coverage and supporting mobile O&M and field inspection. Firewalls are deployed at the network edge to provide comprehensive, layered security protection for the entire network. Built on the design philosophy of core redundancy, reliable access, wireless extension, and secure control, this solution establishes a solid digital foundation for the Gobi clean energy base.
