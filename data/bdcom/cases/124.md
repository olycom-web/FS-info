**Background:**

DMCI Homes, the second-largest real estate developer in the Philippines, is known for creating modern, high-rise residential communities. As part of their commitment to providing residents with top-tier living experiences, DMCI Homes sought a reliable and advanced fiber-optic broadband solution for a condominium project in Manila.

**Project Scope:**

The project aimed to provide high-speed internet, seamless TV connectivity, and integrated communications services to each apartment in the building. The solution needed to meet the growing demand for digital connectivity, supporting not just internet but also advanced services like IP telephony and surveillance.

**Challenges and Solutions:**

- Scalability and Bandwidth Requirements: As a high-rise building, the challenge was to ensure the network could support a large number of users with high data throughput requirements. The BDCOM OLT solution provided the necessary scalability, while the dual-band WiFi ONUs ensured stable and fast internet connectivity throughout the building.
- Seamless Integration of Services: The project required the seamless integration of multiple services, including internet, television, and communication systems. The solution was designed with a robust architecture that allowed for smooth data and video traffic, minimizing any interference between services.
- Infrastructure and Installation Constraints: The limited space for equipment and the need for efficient wiring were addressed by using compact and highly functional devices like PoE switches and ONUs that minimized the need for extensive cabling and offered easy installation.
