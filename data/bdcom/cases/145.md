**Project Background**

Urumqi Evergrande Health Valley is a large-scale integrated cultural tourism and healthcare project developed by Evergrande Group in Urumqi, Xinjiang. It integrates top-tier medical services, health management, elderly care, and tourism resources, aiming to deliver high-quality, multi-dimensional healthcare services for people of all age groups. The project uniquely features four major themed health parks—Wellness Care, Longevity & Happiness, Health Benefits, and Family & Parenting—supported by the Henghe Hospital and integrated with a "Integrated Medical Consortium" hierarchical diagnosis and treatment system. With its diverse business formats and high service standards, the project places extremely high demands on the stability, security, and high-speed data transmission capability of the underlying network infrastructure. A high-performance and highly reliable network solution is the core foundation to ensure stable operation of smart healthcare services, secure interaction of massive health data, and intelligent park management, while also helping the project become a new benchmark for healthcare and wellness in Northwest China.

**Solution**

To meet the smart healthcare requirements of Urumqi Evergrande Health Valley, BDCOM adopts a hierarchical network architecture with the S3956F core switch as the high-speed backbone, ensuring efficient transmission of massive healthcare data. S2528H Gigabit switches are deployed in key service areas to provide stable support for high-bandwidth applications. The S2928E series switches are used for flexible access to meet diverse networking scenarios. For high-density terminal areas, S2510H access switches are deployed to ensure reliable connectivity of IoT devices. The entire solution enables intelligent scheduling and refined management, providing a secure and reliable network foundation for smart healthcare services and ensuring stable operation of critical systems such as healthcare management and security surveillance.
