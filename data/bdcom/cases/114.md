**Project Application Scenario Description:**

The Ningxia Highway Administration Center building has 14 floors. Based on the requirements, three networks will be established: an external network, an internal network, and a dedicated network. These networks are defined as follows:

a) External network: Primarily provides shared internet resource interfaces for resident units, offers web services, and publishes government information.

b) Internal network: Interconnects with transportation industry units across Ningxia, sharing data and improving office efficiency.

c) Dedicated transportation network: A comprehensive business network integrating data transmission, audio, and video, forming an essential part of the traffic administration's external network. Currently, it mainly connects with the Ministry of Transport, and systems like the "Traffic Administration Information System Network Interaction Platform" and the "National Road Transport Management Information System" have already been deployed on the industry network, with other system networking applications also in operation.

d) There are 1,353 information points for the external network, 1,353 for the internal network, and 673 for the dedicated network. The requirement is to build three physically isolated, high-performance, highly stable, and secure office networks.

e) The budget is limited.

**Network Topology:**

Based on the networking needs of the Ningxia Department of Transportation and the Highway Administration Center, the building's local area network (LAN) adopts a two-layer switching architecture—core layer and access layer—forming a high-performance network with gigabit speeds throughout and a backbone that supports upgrades to 10G. Since the network structure and products for the external, internal, and dedicated networks are consistent, the external network topology is used as an example:

a) According to the design principles, the same equipment is used for all three networks. At the network's egress, a high-performance multi-service modular router is configured to access the Internet, the transportation industry units, and the Ministry of Transport's dedicated network. The recommended router is BDCOM7304.

b) At the core layer, a BDCOM S6810 10G routing switch is deployed. To ensure reliability, the core equipment is configured with dual power supplies and dual control planes. Due to budget constraints, 10G modules and redundancy for core switching equipment are temporarily not considered. Two 24-port gigabit fiber interface modules are configured, connected to the floor switches via gigabit multimode fiber, with 50% port redundancy for backup purposes. Additionally, a 48-port gigabit Ethernet module is configured for the service cluster's high-speed access to provide faster service to users.

c) The access layer switches must support 10G modules and be fully gigabit to allow future upgrades to a 10G backbone network. Since the Ningxia Highway Administration Center houses many resident units, network management is challenging. VLAN termination can be achieved at the access layer to reduce the load on the core switch and prevent network disruptions from single point failures. The hardware supports secure port functionality to effectively prevent ARP spoofing and other virus attacks, enhancing network security and reliability. Therefore, BDCOM S3940/3956 routing switches were selected.
