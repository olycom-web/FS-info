**Background**

ISTC is a technology solutions Distributor operating in the telecommunications and networking sector. It specializes in optical network infrastructure projects, enterprise connectivity, and advanced solutions for corporate, hospitality, and residential environments. With a presence in various Latin American and Caribbean regions, ISTC collaborates with manufacturers and strategic suppliers to implement innovative technologies that enhance the efficiency, scalability, and quality of digital services.

**Project introduction**

The hotel project developed by ISTC involves the implementation of a POL (Passive Optical LAN) in a hotel, using PON (Passive Optical Network) technology to provide IPTV, data, WI-FI, and IP telephony integration services. The solution contemplates the use of MDUs model 1702 - 4GPM and an OLT GP3600, enabling an efficient, scalable, and easy - to - maintain architecture.

The objective is to offer a converged network that supports multiple high - quality services, optimizing the traditional infrastructure through fiber optics to the rooms, ensuring speed, stability, and an excellent experience for guests.
