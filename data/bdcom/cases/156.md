### Background

Phase I of the Central and Western Jilin Water Supply Project is a key component of China's national water network infrastructure. It carries the vital mission of addressing regional water scarcity and delivering benefits to 8.2 million people. As a large-scale linear water conservancy project, it features long transmission routes and complex operating environments, placing extremely high demands on the real-time performance and reliability of water quality monitoring, water level measurement, and video security across the entire line. Building a stable, secure, and scalable industrial network has therefore become the essential foundation for smart dispatching and digital twin applications.

### Core Solution

BDCOM built a secure and reliable industrial network for the Jilin Water Network, enabling data to flow seamlessly alongside the water itself. From sensing to decision-making, BDCOM provides a full-chain industrial-grade solution that establishes a solid digital foundation for the Jilin Water Network.

### Front-End Access

At monitoring points along the route, including water quality sensors, water level gauges, and cameras, BDCOM deployed the IES200 Series industrial switches. With an operating temperature range of -40°C to 75°C, IP40 protection, and 6 kV lightning protection, these switches can easily withstand harsh conditions such as humidity and large temperature fluctuations. PoE models also solve power supply challenges over a single cable, enabling fast and efficient deployment of monitoring devices.

### Aggregation and Core

BDCOM adopted the S2500H and S2900 Series switches to build a 10G backbone network across pump stations and the dispatch center. Equipped with 24 Gigabit RJ45 ports and 10G SFP+ uplink ports, the network ensures non-blocking forwarding of water quality data, video surveillance streams, and cloud platform services, providing stable support for digital twin-based dispatching.
