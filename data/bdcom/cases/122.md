**Project Application Scenario:**

The Jilin Meteorological Bureau is a government agency responsible for weather forecasting, climate prediction, artificial weather modification, drought monitoring, lightning prevention, agricultural meteorology, and other services. The province includes 8 prefecture-level cities and 1 autonomous prefecture. The entire province has 54 ground meteorological observation stations, which are widely equipped with 701 wind radars and microcomputer data processing systems. The Changchun meteorological station has already deployed a C-band fully automatic wind radar for operations. In accordance with the "Three Strategies" implemented by the China Meteorological Administration, the province has expanded observation fields.

The network reconstruction project, led by the Jilin Meteorological Information Technology Support Center, aims to establish new network lines for all county stations in Jilin Province, forming a reliable main and backup network. This ensures the continuous and real-time uploading of all meteorological data from each county station to the provincial bureau and the national bureau, as well as the real-time monitoring of weather conditions in Jilin Province.

**Network Solution:**

Provincial Bureau: The provincial bureau's core network consists of two S8510 switches in dual redundant mode, ensuring uninterrupted data transmission on the core link. The core switches are connected to BSR2900-40B and VPN devices (BDCOM 7304) in dual redundant mode.

Prefecture Bureau: Each prefecture-level meteorological bureau deploys two BSR2900-20B routers in dual redundant mode. One BSR2900-20B connects to the provincial bureau's BSR2900-40B, while the other establishes a VPN virtual tunnel connection with the provincial bureau's BDCOM 7304.

County Stations: Each county station has two lines, one SDH link and one VPN link. The BSR2800-20 and BSR2900-20B work together in a dual redundant configuration to ensure uninterrupted transmission of operational data. One BSR2800-20 is directly connected to the prefecture's BSR2900-20B, while the other BSR2900-20B establishes a VPN virtual tunnel link with the provincial bureau's R7304. Each county station has two S3956B aggregation switches in dual redundant mode and two S2228B access switches. This setup ensures continuous and real-time transmission of meteorological data between county stations, the provincial bureau, and the prefecture bureau, guaranteeing network reliability.
