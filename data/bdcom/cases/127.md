**Background:**

Las Piñas City, Philippines, launched a citywide CCTV initiative to enhance public safety in key areas and nearby Barangays. The project required a robust, scalable network infrastructure to support high-quality surveillance systems. BDCOM's S2510-P and S2900-24P4X Layer 3 Lite PoE switches were deployed to address the city's complex requirements, ensuring reliability, compliance, and seamless connectivity.

**Challenges:**

1. Market Competition: Competing against numerous vendors offering similar solutions.
2. Customization: Unique configurations needed for Phase 2 network rehabilitation.
3. Standards Compliance: Required PoE+ and Layer 2/Layer 3 functionalities for multi-location support.
4. Reliability: High-quality connectivity and protocol compatibility essential for uninterrupted operation.

**Solution provided:**

- PoE+ Support: Delivered 30W per port to power IP cameras, simplifying installation.
- Layer 2/Layer 3 Features: Enabled advanced routing, VLANs, and QoS for efficient traffic management.
- Custom Configurations: Tailored setups for seamless integration and Phase 2 readiness.
- Reliability & Scalability: Ensured consistent performance and future-proofing for expansions.

**Results:**

- Enhanced Security: Reliable network infrastructure enabled seamless CCTV operation.
- Cost Efficiency: PoE+ reduced installation and operational costs.
- Future-Proofing: Scalable solutions prepared the network for Phase 2.
- Market Differentiation: BDCOM's tailored solutions outperformed competitors.
