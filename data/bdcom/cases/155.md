### Background

Ospital ng Makati, also known as OsMak, is a local government hospital in Makati with a 300-bed capacity. To maintain its premium reputation, OsMak consistently delivers accessible, affordable, and high-quality healthcare services to its patients through modernized equipment and facilities.

Ospital ng Makati was the first government hospital accredited by the Philippine Health Insurance Corporation for providing excellent services that meet almost all of the required criteria.

### Challenges

- Need for high network redundancy to avoid service interruption
- Deployment of PoE devices in hard-to-wire areas
- Requirement for long-lasting and stable network equipment
- Operation in extreme environments (heat, dust, vibration)
- Complex installation and deployment process

### Solution

- Deployed industrial PoE switch with dual power adapter for uninterrupted operation
- Used IES200-LSTW1140P for easy PoE deployment in difficult locations
- Implemented industrial-grade switches for long life and stability
- Ensured reliable performance with wide temperature (-40°C to 75°C) design
- Simplified installation with DIN-rail and plug-and-play features
- Used S2900-48T4X for intelligent traffic segmentation
- Deployed S1200-24P2G1S for quick and easy IP device integration

### Results

- Improved network reliability with minimal downtime
