**Project Background**

Promoting the construction of the Hedong Community Health Service Center in Qingyuan District is an important initiative for Ji'an City to advance the integration of primary healthcare and community-based elderly care. The project aims to build a modern, comprehensive service complex that integrates basic medical care, health management, elderly care services, and civil defense functions, enhancing community public service capability and emergency support. To meet the demands of intelligent management, efficient operation, and security protection, the project plans to build smart infrastructure covering networking, security surveillance, and nurse-call monitoring systems to achieve resource integration, service optimization, and enhanced safety management.

Site Area: 1,330.9㎡

Construction Area: 13,522.22㎡

**Solution & Topology**

To meet the key requirements for high-speed network coverage, comprehensive security monitoring, and efficient medical response, the solution deploys BDCOM's full series of communication equipment in an integrated architecture. A stable and reliable wired network is built through S6803 core switches, S2900 aggregation switches, and S2500 access switches, while WSC6100 wireless controllers together with WAP2100 access points provide high-speed wireless coverage across the entire facility. For security, an intelligent surveillance platform combined with the S8003E monitoring core creates a seamless HD monitoring system. Meanwhile, the integration of nurse-call and elderly-care monitoring systems ensures rapid response to alerts and emergencies. The final result is a unified intelligent solution featuring high-speed network interconnection, visualized smart security, and efficient service delivery.
