### Background

The Argentine Football Association (AFA) upgraded the network infrastructure at its Ezeiza training facility, the main base for the national team. The project aimed to support a modern IP-based video surveillance system for improved security and operations. It was implemented by Magneto and PowerSA using BDCOM solutions. The upgrade ensures reliable monitoring and protection for the FIFA World Cup Qatar 2022 champion team during training

### Challenges

- Ensuring seamless connectivity for a large-scale IP-based video surveillance system across the training facility
- Maintaining high reliability and stability for continuous monitoring of high-profile athletes and sensitive areas
- Integrating data collection, transmission, storage, and centralized management into a unified system
- Providing a scalable solution to support future expansion and increased surveillance demands
- Enabling real-time monitoring and quick response to incidents within the facility

### Solution Provided

- Deployment of BDCOM S2900-24P4X PoE switches to power and connect IP surveillance cameras efficiently
- Implementation of a high-performance network infrastructure to support uninterrupted video transmission
- Integration of end-to-end solutions covering data acquisition, transmission, storage, and centralized management
- Establishment of a unified and centrally controlled video surveillance system
- Delivery of a scalable, stable, and highly compatible network solution with full technical support

### Results

- Reliable and uninterrupted video surveillance across the entire training facility
- Enhanced security with real-time monitoring and early detection of suspicious activities
- Improved operational efficiency through centralized control and management
- Faster incident response with emergency linkage, playback, and evidence collection capabilities
- A stable and scalable system ensuring long-term performance for a world-class training environment
