**Background**

PT Sarana Integrasi Prima (SIPFiber), founded in 2017, is an Indonesian fiber optic infrastructure company providing last-mile and point-to-point connectivity services. With licensed local fixed network operations, SIPFiber delivers high-capacity fiber solutions for homes, businesses, and service providers across Indonesia. The company focuses on building reliable, scalable, and open-access fiber networks to support growing bandwidth demands

**Challenges**

- High-Speed & High Availability Requirements: Delivering ultra-fast, stable internet connectivity with carrier-grade reliability.
- Scalability & Network Management: Designing a network that could easily scale while remaining secure and simple to manage.
- Complex Last-Mile Deployment: Fiber-to-home and fiber-to-business installations required delicate handling of fiber, efficient slack management, and coordination with property owners.
- Operational Excellence: Supporting a wide infrastructure footprint while maintaining high service quality through skilled technical resources.

**Solution**

- Deployed BDCOM S5800-24X2C L3 Managed Switch for core and aggregation
- Provides high-speed, low-latency fiber connectivity for last-mile services
- Supports BVSS virtualization for simplified and centralized management
- Ensures high availability and reliability with redundancy and advanced routing
- Designed for scalable and future-ready network expansion
