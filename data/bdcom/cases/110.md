**Background:**

In 2023, Brebes, a regency in Central Java, Indonesia, experienced significant progress in internet infrastructure. As part of the region's drive towards digital inclusion, internet connectivity was deployed to thousands of homes using Gigabit Passive Optical Network (GPON) technology. The deployment involved BDCOM Optical Line Terminals (OLT) and WiFi-enabled Optical Network Units (ONU) to provide high-speed, reliable internet access to residential users.

**Objectives:**

- Bridge the digital divide in rural and urban areas of Brebes.
- Provide affordable and fast internet access to local residents.
- Support online education, e-commerce, and digital services in the region.

**Deployment:**

- Fiber Infrastructure: F/O cables were laid from the central hub (OLT) to the designated neighborhoods. Each OLT was designed to serve hundreds of households through a series of splitters connected to the ONUs.
- ONU Installation: Each home was equipped with a BDCOM ONU, which provided a WiFi hotspot for users to connect. The ONUs were pre-configured to ensure plug-and-play ease of installation, reducing downtime and enabling faster service activation.
- Service Activation: Once the ONUs were installed, the OLT was configured to allocate bandwidth and services. Residential users were able to connect to high-speed internet almost immediately.
