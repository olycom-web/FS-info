**Background:**

Philippine Normal University (PNU), the National Center for Teacher Education in the Philippines, required a reliable and scalable surveillance network for its main campus. To modernize its outdated surveillance system, the university aimed to improve IP camera connectivity, ensure centralized monitoring, and prepare for future expansion.

**Challenges:**

- Reliable Connectivity: The legacy system lacked stable access for high-resolution IP cameras.
- Scalability: The solution needed to support future upgrades and additional cameras.
- Maintenance Simplicity: The university sought a system that was easy to manage and required minimal physical maintenance.

**Solution:**

PNU deployed BDCOM 24-Port Layer 2 PoE Switches with SFP uplinks, enabling:

- Centralized fiber backbone connectivity via SFP ports
- Rack-mounted PoE switches in compact cabinets for space efficiency
- Easy expansion with spare PoE ports
- Remote management via Pallas Cloud platform

**Results:**

- Seamless Video Monitoring: Real-time, delay-free viewing at the command center
- Clean, Space-Saving Installation: Rack-mounted units preserved building aesthetics
- Future-Proof Scalability: Additional cameras and higher resolutions supported without infrastructure overhaul
- Centralized Network Management: IT staff can monitor and manage switches remotely
