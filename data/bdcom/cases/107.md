# Uninterrupted Security Surveillance Through BDCOM POL Solution in a Solar Energy Plant(Dynamic Sun Energy Pabna)— /case/107.html

**Background:**

Dynamic Sun Energy Pabna Solar PV Park is a ground-mounted solar project which is planned over 400 acres. The project is expected to generate 193,500MWh electricity to offset 93,654t of carbon dioxide emissions (CO2) a year. The solar power project consists of 274,224 modules. 760 inverters are likely to get installed at the project site

**Challenges:**

1. Long distances between camera locations and the central monitoring station
2. Limited power availability in remote areas
3. Harsh environmental conditions

**Solution:**

To overcome the challenges BDCOM GPON OLT and PoE ONUs are deployed.

1. OLT - The OLT was installed at the central monitoring station. It serves as the core device managing and controlling the data transmission from multiple ONUs. The OLT converts the electrical signals from the monitoring station into optical signals for transmission over fiber optic cables.
2. PoE ONU - From the ONU LAN ports the CC cameras are directly connected to omit the extra PoE switches.

**Results and Benefits:**

1. Enhanced Security: The CCTV system provided real-time, high-definition monitoring of the entire solar farm, significantly enhancing security and reducing the risk of theft and vandalism.
2. Cost Efficiency: The use of fiber optic technology reduced the need for multiple repeater stations and power supplies, lowering both installation and maintenance costs.
3. Scalability: The system is easily scalable, allowing for additional cameras to be added without significant infrastructure changes.
4. Reliability: The fiber optic-based solution is less susceptible to interference, ensuring consistent and reliable performance even in adverse weather conditions.
