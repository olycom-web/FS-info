### Background

NLS Kazakhstan is a leading telecommunications operator providing a comprehensive range of telecommunications and information technology services for businesses across Kazakhstan. With more than 14 years of industry experience, NLS delivers high-speed fiber Internet services up to 10 Gbps, IP telephony, video surveillance, cloud infrastructure, and enterprise networking solutions. To support reliable Internet, WiFi, and CCTV services within office buildings, NLS required a highly available and scalable enterprise network infrastructure built on a resilient switching platform.

### Challenges

- Provide stable Internet connectivity across office environments
- Support enterprise WiFi and CCTV surveillance systems
- Ensure high network availability and business continuity
- Build a scalable network architecture for future expansion
- Minimize service interruption in case of network link failure

### Solution

BDCOM delivered a reliable and scalable enterprise switching solution featuring:

- High-Performance Enterprise Switching
- Ring Network Architecture
- Redundant Network Paths
- High Availability & Reliability
- Seamless Internet, WiFi, and CCTV Connectivity
- Scalable Network Design
- Centralized Network Management
