### Project background

Paraiso Residence is a leasehold service residence in Bukit Jalil, Kuala Lumpur, targeted to provide the secure and private space to the residence. The building will fill with 7-tier security which includes CCTV surveillance, visitor management system, boom gate, card access system, IP intercom system and physical guard patrol in the building.

Paraiso Residence implements a 24-hour security system, including the installation of CCTV, so that residents can live in peace. Other accoutrements include the increasingly important high-speed Internet and WiFi that are installed throughout the project.

### Demand/challenge

Paraiso Residence is sited on the 5.6 acres leasehold land. It consists of 2 towers with 39-storey tall. There are a total of 764 units in the building. The project has 4 types of layout design, ranging from 960 square feet to 1,480 square feet.

1. Provide high-speed Internet services for all residents.
2. Offer TV Box with 100Mbps Unlimited Free WIFI.
3. Third party phones and access points.

### Networking Solution

Paraiso Residence offers clean and modern apartments for up to 4 people with free WiFi, unlimited high speed internet. BDCOM's FTTx solution can perfectly meet the network requirements.

BDCOM OLT GP3600-16B plays a role as data center to connect all ONUs and monitor centrally through BDCOM NMS software. In every unit, PoE ONU is installed to provide Ethernet for IP phones, and IP-TV.
