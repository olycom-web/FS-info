# Connecting the Future of Luxury Living in Iraq with BDCOM FTTH Solutions(Remas City, Erbil)— /case/168.html

> ts:2026-09-11 · 来源:https://bdcomnetworks.com/case/168.html

### Background

Remas City is one of the most prestigious gated villa communities in Erbil, Kurdistan Region, Iraq, located along the Erbil–Pirmam Highway (Masif Road). Designed with European-inspired architecture, the community features luxury standalone villas, landscaped green spaces, intelligent lighting systems, and 24/7 security. To support modern smart-living services and reliable digital connectivity, the project required a high-performance fiber network infrastructure.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144614gwt7v8.png)

### Challenges

- Deliver high-speed fiber connectivity to every luxury villa.
- Ensure stable network performance for security surveillance and smart community services.
- Provide reliable Wi-Fi coverage across public and common areas.
- Build a secure network infrastructure with centralized management.
- Deploy a scalable solution that supports future expansion and additional smart services.

### Solution

- Delivered a complete end-to-end Fiber-to-the-Home (FTTH) network for the entire residential community using GPON technology.
- Deployed a secure and reliable enterprise network infrastructure with integrated network security using an enterprise-grade firewall.
- Built a high-performance fiber and Ethernet backbone to ensure stable, high-speed connectivity throughout the community.
- Enabled seamless support for CCTV surveillance, community Wi-Fi, and intelligent building systems through the deployment of PoE switches.
- Implemented centralized network management for simplified monitoring, operation, and maintenance.
- Designed a scalable and future-ready network architecture to support future expansion and evolving smart community services.

### Customer Benefits

- Gigabit-speed fiber broadband for residents.
- Secure, reliable, and highly available network infrastructure.
- Simplified network operation and centralized management.
- Seamless support for smart community applications.
- Reduced maintenance costs with stable and easy-to-manage equipment.
- Scalable platform ready for future digital services and community growth.
