**Background**

TRANS TV is a leading Indonesian television channel operated by PT Televisi Transformasi Indonesia under TRANSMEDIA, based in Jakarta. Since its launch in 2001, Trans TV has become a trendsetter in the media industry, producing nearly 80% of its content in-house. To support live broadcasting and content production, Trans TV requires a reliable network to interconnect multiple TV studios and enable live streaming of broadcast equipment to internet platforms.

**Challenges**

- Ensuring high-speed and stable connectivity for live streaming
- Maintaining high reliability and uninterrupted internet access
- Seamless integration with the existing network infrastructure
- Scalability to support future expansion and technology upgrades
- Easy configuration, monitoring, and management
- Meeting performance requirements within budget limitations

**Solution**

To address the issue PT Trans-TV deployed full L3 switch solution using BDCOM S5800-24X2C【原文重复 BDCOM】

**Highlight Features**

- High Availability Architecture: With BVSS, allowing multiple physical switches to operate as a single logical system
- Ultra-High Bandwidth Performance: With 24 × 10G SFP+ ports and 2 × 100G/40G QSFP28 uplinks for heavy media traffic
- Carrier-Grade Switching Capacity: Featuring an 880 Gbps backplane and 660 Mpps forwarding rate
- Built-in Redundancy: Through dual power supplies for continuous operation
- Advanced Layer-3 Capabilities supporting dynamic routing protocols for optimized traffic flow
- Proven Reliability Features: Ensuring stable, always-on connectivity for live broadcast services

**Results**

- Ultra-Fast Connectivity - High-speed network performance enables seamless studio interconnection and smooth live content delivery without latency or interruption.
- Scalable & Future-Ready Infrastructure - The solution allows easy expansion to support growing bandwidth demands, additional studios, and future digital broadcasting requirements.
- Reliable & Secure Operations - Advanced redundancy and network management features ensure secure, stable, and always-on performance for mission-critical broadcasting services.
