# Hi-Efficient Network Infrastructure Solution Through BDCOM POL (Passive Optical LAN) in A Large Industrial Area(BEPZA)— /case/102.html

> ts:2026-09-11 · 来源:https://bdcomnetworks.com/case/102.html

##### Background:

BEPZA (Bangladesh Export Processing Zones Authority) is the official organ of the government to promote, attract and facilitate foreign investment in the EPZs. Besides, BEPZA as the competent Authority performs inspection & supervision of the compliances of the enterprises related to social & environmenta issues, safety & security at work place in order to maintain harmonious labour-management & industrial relations in EPZs. The primary objective of an EPZ is to provide special areas where potential investors would find a congenial investment climate free from cumbersome procedures

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144657fgtf4b.png)

##### Challenges:

The authority had to cover the whole industrial area in a single network for easy manageability. With a traditional Ethernet LAN network though UTP cable it was really challenging to cover a large industrial area like 2.5 square kilometers. For CC camera surveillance and internet connectivity POL (Passive Optical LAN) was the suitable, cost effective, managed and effective solution for BEBZA.
