### Background

Chandragiri Municipality, located in Kathmandu District, Nepal, initiated a city-wide surveillance project to enhance public safety, traffic monitoring, and crime prevention. The municipality required a reliable outdoor networking infrastructure capable of supporting hundreds of outdoor surveillance cameras across highways and key public locations while ensuring stable connectivity and centralized monitoring

### Challenges

- Deploy hundreds of outdoor surveillance cameras across the city
- Ensure reliable network connectivity in outdoor environments
- Provide stable power and data transmission for IP cameras
- Support long-distance fiber connectivity between surveillance points and the monitoring center
- Maintain continuous operation under harsh environmental conditions

### Solution Provided

BDCOM delivered a complete surveillance networking solution consisting of:

- Industrial PoE Switches installed at outdoor poles to power and connect IP cameras
- Fiber uplinks using SFP modules for long-distance data transmission
- L3 Fiber Switches deployed at aggregation points
- Core-to-core fiber network topology for reliable and scalable connectivity
- Centralized monitoring through NVR and monitoring stations

### Result & Benefits

- Successfully connected hundreds of outdoor surveillance cameras
- Reliable power and network connectivity through industrial-grade switches in the harsh environment
- Stable fiber backbone for high-quality video transmission
- Enhanced city monitoring and crime control capabilities
- Excellent performance and long-term operational stability of BDCOM industrial switches
- Simplified network management and future scalability
