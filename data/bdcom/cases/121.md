**Background:**

As part of a major smart city initiative, Thailand is adopting advanced technologies to enhance urban safety and connectivity. A critical aspect of this initiative is the deployment of a robust CCTV surveillance system for public spaces, aimed at improving security and monitoring road connectivity.

To meet the stringent requirements, industrial-grade outdoor PoE switches capable of powering and connecting surveillance cameras are provided to operate in challenging environments.

These switches ensure seamless and reliable operation under harsh conditions, offering consistent performance to support the city's smart security goals.

**Challenges:**

- Harsh Outdoor Conditions: The network needed to withstand Thailand's extreme weather, including high heat, humidity, and potential water exposure.
- Reliable Power Supply: CCTV cameras required a dependable power source along with data connectivity, often in areas without easy access to power outlets.
- Scalable Infrastructure: The system needed to accommodate future expansion as the smart city project evolves.
- Low-Latency Performance: Real-time video surveillance demanded a low-latency network to ensure effective monitoring and quick responses.

The deployment of industrial-grade PoE switches in Thailand is a key milestone in the city's journey toward becoming a smart city. This project not only enhances public security but also demonstrates the critical role of reliable network infrastructure in urban transformation. The robust and scalable solution ensures that Thailand is well-prepared to meet the growing demands of modern urban management and security.
