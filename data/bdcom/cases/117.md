**Project Application Scenario:**

The General Armament Department of the People's Liberation Army was established in April 1998 and is responsible for organizing the development, research, procurement, and maintenance of military equipment under the leadership of the Central Military Commission. It also carries significant responsibilities, such as manned space missions and satellite launch and control.

To accelerate the informatization of the General Armament Department and meet the department's requirements for improving office efficiency, efforts were made to build an efficient transmission network capable of file transfer, internal website access, and internet browsing. This network is used by approximately 500 to 600 users, primarily distributed across office buildings and subordinate units, to access the internet and transmit information via core routers. Security is also a critical focus, ensuring that network devices meet security requirements while avoiding vulnerabilities.

**Requirements:**

- Basic Network Requirements: Ensure data transmission according to international standards, achieving good interoperability between different manufacturers and devices.
- Stable Operation Requirements: Equipment must meet business needs and be expandable.
- Business Interconnection Requirements: Enable data exchange and resource sharing, while allowing users to access the internet.
- Network Security Requirements: Security devices must work in tandem with network devices across the entire network.
- Comprehensive Management Requirements: Provide effective management of devices while ensuring secure access to network devices.

**Network Topology:**

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144658jtse2e.png)

- The network egress uses the BSR 2900-40B core router, responsible for transmitting data to subordinate units and routing inter-network data. It connects to each subordinate unit via dedicated operator lines, with dual power supplies for redundancy, increasing the stability and reliability of the core router and ensuring uninterrupted data transmission.
- The core router is connected to a Tianrongxin firewall, which performs NAT conversion, converting private addresses to public addresses while providing network security protection for internal users, ensuring safe data transmission between internal and external networks.
- The core layer uses the company's original S8506 core routing switch, responsible for core data exchange and forwarding. Its high backplane bandwidth and excellent packet forwarding rate provide users with an efficient experience.
- All access switches are directly connected to the core layer. The switches procured by the department, including BDCOM S2952C, S2528-C, S3940, and S3956, are connected directly to the core 8506 switch.
- Servers within the department are connected to switches, which are then connected to firewalls. The firewalls provide secure connections to the core 8506 switch. Servers primarily connect to S3940 and S2528-C switches, which in turn connect to the core 8506, achieving secure equipment stacking and interaction between network devices across the network, ensuring a high level of security.
