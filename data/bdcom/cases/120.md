#### Project Application Scenario:

In recent years, with the rapid development of medical informatization, Xi'an Daxing Hospital's information system has gradually evolved from a management system based on financial operations to a patient-centered clinical information system. Additionally, core medical systems such as the "Picture Archiving and Communication System (PACS)" and the "Electronic Medical Record System" have also been launched. To ensure the stable operation of these systems, as well as the Laboratory Information System (LIS) and Radiology Information System (RIS), Xi'an Daxing Hospital needed a comprehensive upgrade of its network hardware and structure. The total number of information points is about 2,300.

**Network Topology:**

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144747iy45hs.png)

The network is designed with a three-tier structure consisting of a core layer, an aggregation layer, and an access layer. The backbone network uses Gigabit technology, and the core switch equipment supports 10G interfaces. Since the hospital network requires high real-time performance, it is generally required to operate continuously for 24 hours, making the stability and reliability of the equipment critical. The reliability of core equipment and backbone links is essential to the entire network.

- The S6810 metro-grade backbone switch is the core equipment of the entire hospital network, with each unit configured with dual control units and dual power supplies to maximize stability and reliability. The two core switches are set up in a dual-redundant hot-standby configuration, using multiple gigabit links bundled together to increase bandwidth, with the two devices virtualized into one, significantly improving the reliability and stability of the core network.
- The aggregation layer uses the S3940, providing redundant dual power supplies and CPU protection mechanisms, ensuring the necessary structural and functional reliability.
- For the access layer, semi-intelligent layer 2 switches S2528-C and S2952C are selected.
