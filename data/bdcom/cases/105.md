# Core Network Switch Implementation at Atma Jaya University, Jakarta— /case/105.html

> ts:2026-09-11 · 来源:https://bdcomnetworks.com/case/105.html

##### Background

Atma Jaya University is a private Catholic university in DKI Jakarta which was founded by the Atma Jaya Foundation on June 1, 1960. This University is one of the oldest and most prestigious private higher education institutions in Indonesia. In 2019 Unika Atma Jaya received an award from the QS World University Ranking with 5 stars in the Employability, Social Responsibility category, and 4 stars for Inclusiveness.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144403jux0ag.jpg)

##### Objectives

1. Enhance Network Performance: Upgrade the network infrastructure to provide higher bandwidth and faster internet speeds.
2. Improve Scalability: Implement a solution that can easily scale to meet the university's future needs.
3. Strengthen Security: Enhance network security to protect sensitive data and ensure compliance with regulatory standards.
4. Simplify Management: Provide a centralized, user-friendly management system to streamline network administration.

##### Solution

To fulfill this objective BDCOM S5828 core switches were implemented at Atma Jaya University with the solution included:

1. High-Performance Switches: Deployment of high-capacity core network switches to handle the university's data traffic efficiently.
2. Scalable Infrastructure: A scalable network architecture that can grow with the university's needs, supporting additional devices and users.
3. Centralized Management: A centralized management platform that allows for easy monitoring, configuration, and maintenance of the network.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/202608261447414ima0m.png)

##### Implementation

Implemented BDCOM 24 port core switch with 100G uplinks with stacking features for high availability and reliability as well.
