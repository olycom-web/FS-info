> ts:2026-09-11 · 来源:https://bdcomnetworks.com/case/89.html

### Project background

Skynet is an innovative telecom operator in Russia Kazan, which is the capital and largest city of the Republic of Tatarstan. According to the 2023 Russian census, with 1.2million inhabitants, it is the sixth largest city in Russia. Skynet chose GEPON technology to start the construction of a backbone optical network. From the subscriber to the main channel - a continuous optical fiber without active equipment and repeaters. high-speed Internet access at speeds up to 1 Gbps and GEPON Optical Internet provides maximum reliability.

### Challenge: Internet, WIFI and CCTV
