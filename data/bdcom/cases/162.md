### Background

VIPNET is one of the major regional Internet and TV service providers in Bulgaria. In addition to broadband and IPTV services, the company also provides WiFi and CCTV surveillance solutions. To deliver stable and reliable services for both residential and business customers, VIPNET planned to expand its fiber-based network infrastructure with a high-performance EPON FTTH solution.

### Challenges

- Build a stable FTTH/FTTB network for Internet and IPTV services
- Provide reliable WiFi coverage for home and office users
- Support CCTV camera systems for enterprise customers using the same fiber optic network
- Simplify network management and maintenance
- Ensure smooth and stable network performance

### Solution Provided

BDCOM delivered a complete EPON access solution designed to support high-speed FTTH broadband, IPTV, WiFi, and CCTV services for both residential and enterprise users. The solution included:

- Pizza Box EPON OLT for centralized fiber access and network management
- xPON ONU for stable broadband connectivity to home subscribers
- Dual Band WiFi Router for high-performance residential wireless coverage
- PoE ONU with PoE/PoE+ support to directly power wireless Access Points and IP Cameras, simplifying deployment and reducing infrastructure costs
- BDCOM NMS for centralized monitoring, configuration, and management of the entire network

The complete solution provided stable performance, simplified maintenance, and scalable network expansion capability for future service growth.
