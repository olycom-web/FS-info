### Background

The Chery Jaguar Land Rover Changshu production base is Jaguar Land Rover's first complete vehicle manufacturing plant outside the UK and is widely recognized as a "global benchmark factory." With the introduction of the new EOX model and the ongoing intelligent upgrade of its production lines, the plant needed to build a highly reliable industrial network foundation to support dense terminal access across welding, painting, and final assembly workshops, while ensuring real-time data transmission for industrial Internet applications such as the MES manufacturing execution system and RFID material tracking. Traditional Ethernet networks faced challenges such as complex cabling, difficult fault location, and limited bandwidth. The factory urgently required a next-generation network solution featuring a simplified architecture, high reliability, and easy maintenance.

### Solution Provided

BDCOM built a GPON all-optical industrial network for the Chery Jaguar Land Rover Changshu New Energy Plant, using "one fiber" to reconstruct the nervous system of the workshops.

**Simplified Architecture**

BDCOM GPON OLTs are deployed in the equipment room and connected to workshop ONUs through optical splitters, enabling fiber to reach workstations directly and replacing the traditional multi-layer switching network. At the core layer, 10G interconnection ensures non-blocking forwarding for services such as MES and ERP.

**Scenario-Based Deployment**

Compact Gigabit ONUs are deployed in office areas, while PoE MDUs are installed in production areas to provide remote power supply for devices such as IP cameras and industrial APs, significantly simplifying cabling.

**Intelligent O&M**

A unified network management platform enables centralized and visualized management of all-optical devices, allowing fault location to be completed within minutes.

**Solution Value**

With its one-fiber, multi-service capability and two-layer flat network architecture, the solution provides a highly reliable and scalable network foundation for smart manufacturing at the Changshu plant, one of the world's benchmark factories
