**Background:**

Frobel Academy, located in Chattogram, Bangladesh, is a reputable educational institution offering comprehensive learning environments for its students. As a modern academic establishment, it was imperative for the academy to enhance its network infrastructure to accommodate the growing demands of digital education, including the integration of smart classrooms, efficient administrative management, and secure connectivity.

**Challenges:**

- Bandwidth Constraints: Could not effectively support high-speed data transmission for educational applications.
- Device Management: Multiple devices such as IP cameras, wireless access points, and administrative systems required a scalable solution.
- Power Supply for IoT Devices: With the integration of Internet of Things (IoT) devices like security cameras, smart boards, and wireless access points, needed a solution that could power these devices efficiently through the network itself.
- Network Security and Segmentation: The need for separate networks for administration, teachers, students, and security devices was essential to ensure data security and operational efficiency.

**Solution:**

To address these challenges, the network infrastructure at Frobel Academy was upgraded with the deployment of advanced Ethernet switches, Layer 3 (L3) switches, and Power over Ethernet (PoE) switches. These devices provided the academy with a comprehensive solution for improving network efficiency, security, and scalability.

**Results and Benefits:**

- Faster Network Speed: Improved data transfer rates and reliable internet access.
- Better Security: Separated networks for students, staff, and admin for enhanced security.
- Cost optimization: Power for devices like cameras provided through PoE switches, reducing extra power supplies.
- Support for Growth: Network now supports more devices and future expansions.
- Enhanced Learning: Integrated smart technologies for a modern classroom experience.
