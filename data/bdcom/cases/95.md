##### Background

Win Mobile is a mobile and internet operator operating in the Republic of Crimea in capital city Sevastopol. Win Mobile Becomes the First Mobile Operator in Crimea. Crimea has a population of 1.969 million.

Win Mobile chose GEPON technology to start the construction of a backbone optical network. From the subscriber to the main channel - a continuous optical fiber without active equipment and repeaters. high-speed Internet access at speeds up to 1 Gbps and GEPON Optical Internet provides maximum reliability.

##### Customer needs

Internet, WIFI and Voip

##### Solution

GPON OLT: GP3600-16B-2AC

XPON ONU: GP1704-4GV-22A
