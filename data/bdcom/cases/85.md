##### Project Background

Yunnan Vocational College of Culture and Art, founded in 1956 and located in scenic Kunming, is the first provincial public art vocational college in Yunnan Province.

The project for Woyunshan Campus of Yunnan Vocational College of Culture and Art involves 2 teaching buildings, 2 canteens, 15 villas and 6 dormitories.
