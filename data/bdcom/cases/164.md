### Background

Instituto de Olhos de Rondônia is a specialized eye care hospital located in Ji-Paraná, Rondônia, Brazil. Established in 2012, the hospital provides outpatient medical services and diagnostic examinations. To support its growing operations, the hospital required a network infrastructure upgrade and a comprehensive surveillance monitoring system covering the entire facility.

### Challenges

- Upgrade the existing network infrastructure to improve reliability.
- Deploy a hospital-wide surveillance monitoring system.
- Ensure stable connectivity for medical and administrative operations.
- Provide a scalable network for future expansion.

### Solution Provided

A reliable and scalable network architecture was deployed to modernize the hospital's IT infrastructure and support comprehensive surveillance coverage across the facility. The solution was designed to ensure continuous connectivity, simplified management, and future expansion capabilities.

- Implemented a redundant ring network to improve network availability and link stability.
- Deployed a PoE-based surveillance network to support hospital-wide monitoring.
- Established a stable access network for Internet and internal communication services.
- Enabled centralized connectivity for medical, administrative, and security systems.
- Designed the network with scalability to accommodate future growth and additional devices.

### Results

- Successfully upgraded the hospital network infrastructure.
- Achieved complete surveillance monitoring coverage throughout the facility.
- Improved network stability through redundant ring topology.
- Delivered reliable Internet connectivity for daily hospital operations.
- Established a scalable platform for future growth.
