**Background:**

Bangabandhu Sheikh Mujibur Rahman Agricultural University (BSMRAU), one of the prestigious public universities in Bangladesh, required a modern and reliable network solution to enhance connectivity in its newly constructed 10-story residential building for faculty members. Recognizing the need for high-speed, stable internet access in a densely populated environment, BSMRAU implemented the country's first Passive Optical LAN (POL) solution using wall-mounted faceplate GPON ONUs through BDCOM GPON solution making a significant milestone in Bangladesh.

**Challenges:**

- Seamless Connectivity in a High-Density Environment: The university's residential building hosts multiple faculty members and their families, requiring high-quality internet access throughout each apartment.
- Space-Efficient Solution: The solution had to integrate neatly into the living spaces, minimizing clutter while maintaining easy access for users.
- Scalability for Future Demand: With increasing data demands, the solution needed to be scalable to accommodate future technology upgrades and higher usage.
- Ease of Maintenance: The university required a system that would be easy to maintain without needing complex rewiring or extensive setup.

**Solution Provided:**

To address BSMRAU's requirements, implemented a Passive Optical LAN (POL) solution using BDCOM

- GPON OLT: A central GPON OLT was deployed to serve as the backbone of the POL, managing data distribution across the entire residential building.
- Wall-Mount Faceplate ONU: Dual port wall-mount faceplate ONUs were installed in each apartment unit. This design blends seamlessly with interior aesthetics, providing internet access without additional devices or cables cluttering the space.
- High-Speed Data Transmission: GPON technology allows for data rates up to 2.5 Gbps downstream, ensuring fast and reliable connectivity throughout the building.

**Results:**

- Enhanced Connectivity for Faculty: Faculty members now have reliable, high-speed internet access within their apartments, supporting both professional and personal needs.
- Space-Saving Design: Wall-mounted faceplate ONUs deliver high-speed internet without occupying valuable space or adding visible cables, aligning with the residential building's aesthetics.
- Scalable Infrastructure: The GPON-based POL solution is easily scalable, allowing the university to upgrade or expand network capacity in response to future demands.
- Efficient Network Management: The centralized GPON OLT enables simplified network.
