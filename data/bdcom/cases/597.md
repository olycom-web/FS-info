# BDCOM Powers Casablanca Port's Digital Transformation— /case/597.html

> ts:2026-09-11 · 来源:https://bdcomnetworks.com/case/597.html

**Background**

Operated by Morocco's National Ports Agency (ANP), Casablanca Port is the country's largest commercial port and a key MENA trade gateway.

To support smart port development and maritime safety, it requires stronger digital capabilities to meet growing demands and withstand harsh coastal conditions.

![234567.png](https://resource.fs.com/boda/generalImg/20260911110858fucq3j.png)

**Challenge**

- High bandwidth needs for video, radar, and storage
- Moisture, salt spray, temperature shifts, and vibration
- PoE, non-PoE, fiber, and copper compatibility
- Scattered devices hinder fault locating and management
- Support for future devices and services

**Solution**

The project builds a high-load network infrastructure. BDCOM deployed an integrated solution combining high-speed core interconnection, industrial-grade access, hybrid fiber-copper networking, and unified management.

**Core Layer:** An S5864HB high-density core switch connects directly to the storage cluster via 40G/100G links, enabling low-latency data retrieval for video analytics and port systems.

**Aggregation Layer:** S3900-24S8T6X switches use 10G SFP+ uplinks to carry radar and maritime camera traffic between field devices and the core.

**Access Layer:** Indoor S2900-24P4XV1 switches power CCTV cameras via PoE. At maritime sites, IES200-V25 industrial switches provide hybrid fiber-copper access for radar and cameras in salt-spray and vibration conditions.

**Outcomes & Benefits**

- 25G/100G interconnection meets storage needs and enables rapid playback of 30-day video archives
- Ruggedized industrial devices withstand harsh conditions and run continuously from -40°C to +75°C
- Supports PoE, non-PoE, and hybrid fiber-copper access
- Centralized NMS management cuts O&M complexity by 45%
- Layered architecture supports future device and service expansion
