##### Project demands

The customer requested comprehensive high-speed network coverage and high-definition video surveillance across the older office area.

##### Solution

To meet the customer's requirements, the BDCOM team and local partners devised a BDCOM POL-based solution.

**Old Network Assessment and Planning:** A thorough assessment and planning of the existing network were conducted to determine the scope and objectives of the upgrade. This included evaluating network topology, capacity requirements, coverage area, etc.

**Fiber Optic Cabling and Equipment Updates:** Existing fiber optic cabling was inspected, optimized, expanded, and updated as needed. High-reliability dual-power-source GP3600-08 equipment and P1704-4GPM【原文如此】devices with POE functionality were used to power high-definition video cameras.

**Network Security and Management:** Advanced gateway equipment, BDSMS, was employed to provide an effective network management solution, ensuring full management coverage of network transmission devices in operation.

**Project Implementation and Support:** A detailed project implementation plan was developed, and a professional team was organized for project deployment and configuration. Training and technical support were provided to ensure the project's successful completion and satisfactory service.

This solution aims to provide an efficient and reliable network solution for the industrial park's POL project, meeting modern communication needs, improving network performance, and enhancing user experiences. ##### Result

**High-Speed Network Connectivity:** With fiber optic cabling and equipment updates, network connectivity is now faster and more stable. Users can enjoy faster internet speeds and a smoother online experience.

**Robust Scalability:** The renovated POL architecture provides robust scalability for future expansion. Whether increasing the number of users, adding new services, or expanding coverage, the network can easily accommodate these changes.

**Streamlined Management and Maintenance:** Effective network management solutions have simplified network administration and maintenance tasks. Administrators can easily monitor network status, troubleshoot issues, and optimize performance.
