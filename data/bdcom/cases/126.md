**Background:**

Parañaque City Hall embarked on a comprehensive initiative to modernize its network infrastructure and enhance its surveillance system. This project aimed to replace the existing core switches with more efficient solutions and upgrade the existing PoE switches to industrial-grade PoE switches for improved perimeter CCTV surveillance. The ultimate goal was to improve network reliability, reduce operational costs, and ensure seamless connectivity across the City Hall's multiple offices and facilities.

**Key Challenges and How They Were Addressed:**

1. Market Competition
   - Many competitors charge additional fees for modular upgrades, firmware updates, and support, increasing costs.
   - BDCOM Advantage: Offered zero-cost updates and upgrades, reducing expenses and providing excellent value.
2. Seamless Integration
   - Replacing existing Layer 3 and Layer 2 switches required smooth integration without disrupting operations.
   - BDCOM Advantage: Customized solutions ensured compatibility and efficient installation.
3. Standards Compliance
   - The network needed to meet industrial standards to handle complex, multi-location connectivity.
   - BDCOM Advantage: Fully compliant with L2/L3 systems, ensuring reliability and high performance.
4. Reliable Connectivity
   - Reliable communication was essential for improved services and operations.
   - BDCOM Advantage: Delivered high-speed, stable connectivity across all City Hall offices and facilities through Hi-PoE and industrial grade PoE switches.
