### Background

Itahari Sub-Metropolitan City required a modern, centralized network to support its growing digital governance needs. The city needed to connect multiple wards, schools, and health posts under a unified infrastructure. Existing systems were fragmented, limiting efficiency and service quality. A scalable and integrated solution was essential to ensure reliable connectivity, communication, and public service delivery.

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144357bjx29s.jpg)

### Challenges

- Fragmented network across multiple locations
- Unstable and low-speed internet connectivity
- Lack of centralized monitoring and control
- Inefficient communication between departments
- Absence of integrated surveillance system
- Limited scalability for future expansion

### Solution Provided

- PoE switches to support CCTV cameras, IP phones, and wireless devices
- Industrial switches for reliable operation in outdoor and harsh environments
- Enterprise-grade wireless access points for secure and seamless Wi-Fi coverage
- Integration of IP-based CCTV surveillance system powered through PoE switching
- Enablement of IP telephony and data communication over a unified network
- Centralized network monitoring and management for efficient control and maintenance

![](https://resource.fs.com/mall/generalImg/20260826144654i7bpgz.jpg)

### Results

- Reliable and stable network connectivity across all connected facilities
- Seamless Wi-Fi access for users in offices, schools, and health posts
- Improved surveillance reliability with centrally connected CCTV systems
- Efficient power and data delivery through PoE, reducing infrastructure complexity
- Enhanced network resilience in outdoor locations using industrial-grade switches
- Simplified operations through centralized management and monitoring
- Scalable and flexible infrastructure supporting future expansion
