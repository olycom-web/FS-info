**Overview:**

Passive Optical LAN (POL) is an enterprise local area network based on PON technology, providing integrated data, voice, video, and other low-voltage services to users through fiber optics.

BDCOM has introduced the Passive Optical LAN (POL) Solution for office buildings, smart parks, smart campuses, healthcare environments, smart hotels, and other markets. From complexity to simplicity, from design to operation and maintenance, from traditional to upgraded, the journey to an exquisite life begins with a BDCOM POL network.

**Background:**

Fox Lite Hotel Majalaya is a star-rated hotel and the first international hotel in the Majalaya area. This hotel is managed by The Ascott Limited and offers a range of comprehensive facilities.

Located on Jl. Anyar Majasetra, Majalaya, Bandung Regency, Fox Lite Hotel Majalaya features 110 rooms in 3 different categories. The rooms are elegantly designed and equipped with modern amenities to ensure the comfort of every guest.

In addition to premium accommodations, Fox Lite Hotel Majalaya also provides a variety of superior facilities, including The Cafe by Fox, a swimming pool, meeting rooms with a capacity of up to 300 pax, a Kids Club, and many other amenities.

**Requirement:**

Since networking technology has been growing and got advance nowadays, Malajaya Hotel wants to improve the quality of service to customers. One of them is by providing stable and high-speed internet connection services. For this requirement, they try to adopt POL (Multi Service/Triple Play) for the existing hotel network infrastructure.

They use ONU for handling the internet connectivity of customers or hotel guests and also provide VOIP service in each room.

**Solutions:**

To fulfill the requirement of providing service to customers or hotel guests, BDCOM products such as the GP3600-08B and GP1704-4GV are used.

GPON Technology: The BDCOM GP3600 Series complies with ITU-T G.984/G.988 and meets the technical requirements for GPON OLT in the Network Access Technical Requirements. It fully supports CTC2.0, automatic discovery, and cooperation with ONUs from different manufacturers.

Multi-Service Distribution: The BDCOM GP1704 Series is a smart HGU with integrated wireless, VoIP, and CATV functions, designed for integrated multiservice networks. To ensure the secure service-carrying ability of the ONU, BDCOM has developed techniques including VLAN, STP, port isolation, ACL, QoS, and Broadcast Storm Control.
