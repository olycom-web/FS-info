##### Overview

BDCOM cooperate with LOIHENG team to apply BDCOM OLT GP3600-08B and ONU GP1702-2F-E to provide security and reliable network in Asia Mega Link FTTH projects.

##### Background

Asia Mega Link was founded in 2009 and began its operation aiming at the creation of a new telecommunications company with one exclusive and unique intention; the specialization in the field of public applications, with the aspiration to constitute the most important and bigger supplier of relative products and services in Myanmar. Since then, Asia Mega Link work for developing telephone density in Myanmar.

##### Project demands

Asia Mega Link FTTH projects mainly use BDCOM OLT and ONU products to do Yangon small ISP projects, and completely use BDCOM POL solution advantages of Simple and convenient deployment; Smooth upgrade to 10G, 40G; Passive ODN occupies small space, which reduces construction investment and energy consumption.
