**Background:**

Niš Airport, located in south Serbia, is the second busiest airport in Serbia. There are parking lots which need to upgrade the gate control system inside.

**Challenges:**

- Harsh Environment: Extreme temperatures and weather conditions.
- Limited Power Access: Cameras had to be placed far from power sources.
- Reliability: Continuous surveillance with no downtime was critical.
- Scalability: The system needed to accommodate future growth.

**Solution:**

We provided industrial-grade PoE switches to power cameras and transmit data via Ethernet, reducing the need for separate power lines. These switches were designed for extreme conditions, ensured reliable performance, and offered scalability for future expansion.

**Results and Benefits:**

- Enhanced Security: Continuous, high-quality monitoring across key airport areas.
- Cost Efficiency: PoE reduced installation costs by eliminating the need for separate power infrastructure.
- Reliability: Industrial-grade design ensured consistent performance despite environmental challenges.
- Scalability: The system is ready for future expansion.
