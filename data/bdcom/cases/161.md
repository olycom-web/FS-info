# BDCOM Safeguards the West Bund Convention Center— /case/161.html

> ts:2026-09-11 · 来源:https://bdcomnetworks.com/case/161.html

### Project Overview

Located in the core area of Xuhui Riverside, the Shanghai West Bund International Convention Center has a total construction area of approximately 77,000 square meters. It serves as the venue for high-profile events such as the World Artificial Intelligence Conference. Faced with high-density visitor traffic and the need for multi-service integration, this new urban landmark—featuring 6,000 m² and 4,000 m² column-free halls—places extremely high demands on its network infrastructure.

### Core Solution

The West Bund Convention Center has deployed three completely physically isolated networks: a surveillance network, a wireless network, and an equipment network.

### Surveillance network:

It uses the F5100 Series firewall as the network gateway, combined with the F5500 behavior management system for fine-grained terminal control. Behind it, two S6806H switches are stacked to form the core layer, ensuring high reliability. Fiber links connect to S2900 Series aggregation switches, and front-end cameras are finally accessed through the S2528H.

### Wireless Network:

More than 290 WAP2100 Series APs are deployed throughout the venue to deliver seamless full coverage. The APs uplink to the S2500-24P4S, aggregate to the S6806H core, and then connect upstream to the F5500 behavior management system and F5100 firewall as the operator-facing gateway. To meet high-concurrency wireless access demands, a multi-link access strategy is adopted. Different service traffic is isolated by the firewall and connected to the public network through independent dedicated lines.

### Equipment Network:

The equipment network adopts a POL all-optical solution. The network gateway is also built with the F5500 and F5100 Series, connecting downstream to the S6806H core, then to the OLT, and extended through optical splitters to front-end ONUs, which finally connect to various terminal devices.
