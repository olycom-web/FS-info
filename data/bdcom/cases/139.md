**Background**

Tiankai Heping Park · Model-Innovation Community is the first stand-alone tower in the "one core, two wings, multi-nodes" spatial layout of the Tiankai Higher Education Sci-Tech Innovation Park.

The project—planned and upgraded under the Tianjin Heping District Government—adopts a vertical functional zoning design and fully leverages nearby university resources and convenient transport.

- Gross floor area: 30,647 m²
- Floors: 24
- Investment: RMB 200 million

BDCOM undertook the community's end-to-end network build-out, covering structured cabling, office LAN, high-density Wi-Fi, video surveillance, and the building systems network, providing a solid digital foundation for R&D and innovation incubation.

Guided by high-standards planning and construction, the solution adopts a three-tier architecture to ensure scalability and point-level redundancy, flexibly meeting future growth.
