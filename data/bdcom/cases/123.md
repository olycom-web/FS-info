**Background:**

Dipped Products PLC is a global leader in the manufacturing of protective handwear, offering a diverse portfolio of natural and synthetic-latex-based gloves for household, industrial, and medical applications. As a global manufacturer, maintaining a reliable and robust network infrastructure is critical for seamless operations and business growth.

To address network performance issues and enhance data security, Dipped Products PLC upgraded its network infrastructure by deploying high-performance BDCOM switches, ensuring efficiency, reliability, and scalability across its manufacturing plant.

**Challenges:**

- Network Overload: The old network infrastructure could not keep up with increasing data demands, causing frequent slowdowns and outages.
- Operational Inefficiency: Employees struggled to access essential resources due to network delays, impacting productivity.
- Data Security Concerns: The existing network lacked robust security measures, leaving sensitive manufacturing data vulnerable to breaches.

**Solution:**

To address these challenges, DPP installed BDCOM switches, known for their outstanding performance, reliability, and advanced security features. The solution included:

- BDCOM S3900 Series Switches: Deployed for the core network to ensure high performance and seamless communication across the plant.
- BDCOM S2900 Series Switches: Implemented for employee networks, CCTV systems, and phone networks, providing reliable connectivity and improved bandwidth management.

**Results:**

The deployment of BDCOM switches has provided measurable benefits for DPP

- Improved Network Performance: The upgraded network operates at significantly higher speeds, enabling employees to access resources and complete tasks faster.
- Enhanced Reliability: The new network infrastructure is robust and reliable, minimizing outages and ensuring uninterrupted operations across all departments.
- Stronger Data Security: The advanced security features of BDCOM switches protect critical manufacturing and operational data from unauthorized access or cyber threats.
- Optimized Resource Management: Improved bandwidth allocation allows smooth connectivity for servers, workstations, CCTV cameras, and phone systems without overloading the network.
