##### Background

InterContinental Hotels & Resorts, a luxury hotel brand under the InterContinental Hotels Group (IHG), is one of the largest luxury hotel brands in the world. Since opening its first hotel in Belo Horizonte, Brazil, in 1946, InterContinental Hotels & Resorts has established over 200 hotels across nearly 70 countries, offering travelers an unparalleled luxury experience.

##### Challenges

The temperature of Doha in Qatar usually higher and in mid-day in summer season goes very high which effects on data transmission for regular switching solutions

##### Solution Implemented

The InterContinental Hotels & Resorts in Doha has undertaken a significant network upgrade project aimed at providing panoramic wireless network coverage for the hotel's outdoor areas, ensuring that guests can enjoy high-speed wireless internet service anywhere in the hotel, thus achieving comprehensive coverage. To enhance the network's reliability and efficiency, the entire wireless network will adopt a ring design, with redundancy mechanisms to enhance network stability and fault tolerance.

In this upgrade, the hotel specifically chose BDCOM's industrial network management switches to replace the existing network equipment. BDCOM industrial switches support a rich set of ring network and QoS protocols, optimizing network performance and improving energy efficiency. With these technological innovations and equipment upgrades, the InterContinental Hotels & Resorts in Doha is committed to providing guests with a more seamless, efficient, and reliable network experience, ensuring fast internet access in every corner of the hotel. This project reflects the hotel's ongoing commitment to enhancing customer satisfaction and maintaining a leading position in the industry.
