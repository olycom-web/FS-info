**Project Overview:**

San Juan City Hall embarked on a transformative initiative to upgrade its city-wide CCTV infrastructure. With public safety, real-time surveillance, and smart city readiness as top priorities, the city required a robust, high-capacity network backbone capable of handling high-resolution video streams with maximum reliability and scalability.

**Challenges**

- Market Competition:

The city faced numerous vendor options with varying costs. Many competing solutions required additional licensing fees for modular upgrades, annual firmware updates, or technical support, resulting in higher total cost of ownership over time.

- Reliability in Harsh Environments:

Using non-industrial PoE switches for outdoor, large-scale CCTV applications introduces risks of frequent hardware failures, increased maintenance, and reduced network uptime.

- Distance & Scalability Limitations:

Relying on copper cabling for an extensive outdoor CCTV network would create constraints in distance, signal quality, and environmental vulnerability, making it unsuitable for reliable, large-scale surveillance coverage.

**Solutions:**

To address these challenges, BDCOM deployed a comprehensive, future-ready solution:

- Core Switching: BDCOM S5864HB, a 100G high-capacity core switch, was implemented as the backbone of the CCTV network. It delivers ultra-high bandwidth, non-blocking architecture, and low latency for real-time aggregation and transport of video streams city-wide.
- Industrial PoE Switching: BDCOM IES200-V25-2S8P industrial PoE switches provide stable power and data connectivity to outdoor cameras in demanding conditions. This ensures reliable performance, even in harsh environmental scenarios, reducing maintenance costs and downtime.
- Managed Access Switching: BDCOM S2900-24P4X managed switches enable flexible aggregation at distribution points, support intelligent network segmentation, and maintain secure, scalable operations for multiple camera zones.
- Long-Distance Fiber Connectivity: BDCOM GSFP-LX-SM1310-20-BIDI and GSFP-LX-SM1550-20-BIDI SFP modules were used to build a fiber optic backbone for long-distance, high-speed connections between cameras and the core switch. This design guarantees secure, interference-free transmission and overcomes the limitations of copper cabling.
- Zero Hidden Costs: Unlike many market alternatives, BDCOM's solution provides free modular upgrades, lifetime firmware updates, and zero annual licensing or support fees, resulting in significant savings over the network's lifecycle.
