##### Background:

AEON Mall 3 is located in Phnom Penh, the capital city of Cambodia. This is a major commercial and retail development in the city, offering a wide range of shopping, dining, and entertainment options. It's part of the AEON Mall chain, a series of shopping malls originating from Japan, known for their size and the variety of services and shops they offer. Phnom Penh is the political, economic, and cultural center of Cambodia, and the presence of such a large mall highlights the city's growth and development.
