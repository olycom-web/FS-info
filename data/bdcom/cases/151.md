**Background**

Eastern Samar State University (ESSU) is a public university serving students and faculty in Eastern Samar. To strengthen campus security, the university installed a campus-wide CCTV surveillance system and required a reliable network infrastructure to support it.

**Challenges**

- Seamless Connectivity for High-Resolution IP Cameras: The university's existing surveillance system required a stable and high-speed network to ensure clear, real-time video monitoring across all CCTV locations.
- Scalability for Future Demand: With the possibility of adding more cameras and upgrading to higher resolutions, the network needed to support future expansion without major infrastructure changes.
- Ease of Maintenance: The university required a simple and efficient system that could be managed easily by the IT team, avoiding complex rewiring or complicated configurations.

**Solution Provided**

To address the challenges ESSU chose:

- Structured Network Architecture: Implemented a core–distribution–access design to ensure organized traffic flow, better performance, and long-term scalability.
- High-Speed Core Connectivity: Deployed a high-capacity core switch to aggregate network traffic and provide stable, high-bandwidth uplink to the command center.
- PoE-Enabled Access Network: Installed PoE switches to power and connect IP cameras directly, eliminating the need for separate power supplies and simplifying installation.
- Fiber Backbone Implementation: Established a single-mode fiber backbone between switches to ensure reliable, high-speed data transmission with minimal latency.
- Centralized Cloud Management: Enabled remote monitoring and configuration through a cloud-based management platform, allowing the IT team to manage the network efficiently without being physically on-site.

**Result**

- Smooth, delay-free CCTV monitoring at the command center
- Easy addition of new cameras when needed
- Stable and scalable fiber-based backbone
- Simplified remote management for the IT team
