**Background**

Located in Athens, Greece, the tram station project represents a vital step in the modernization of the city's transportation infrastructure. In collaboration with a local system integrator, BDCOM was entrusted with delivering a turnkey industrial networking solution. The project was specifically designed to showcase the reliability and performance of BDCOM's industrial switches and fiber solutions in a demanding, real-world transport environment.

**Challenges**

The project environment presented several demanding conditions:

- Extreme Temperatures: Ambient temperatures reaching up to +85°C and ~ 23 °C【原文如此】
- Harsh Environments: High levels of dust, vibration, and electromagnetic interference.
- Reliability Requirements: Equipment needed a Mean Time Between Failures (MTBF ≥ 800,000 hours) to ensure long-term operational continuity.
- Seamless Connectivity: Fiber connectivity was required across all subsystems to guarantee high-speed and electromagnetic interference-free communication.

**Outcome**

The deployment successfully demonstrated the stability, scalability, and robustness of BDCOM's industrial switches and fiber solutions in a real transport environment.

- Reliable network operations were maintained even under extreme temperature and dust conditions.
- Fiber-based backbone connectivity ensured low latency, high-speed communication across subsystems.
- The project proved BDCOM's capability to deliver mission-critical infrastructure for transportation networks, positioning the solution for broader deployment across Greece's urban transport systems.
