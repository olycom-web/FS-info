## Overview

IES200-LS-P is an unmanaged industrial PoE switch engineered for harsh environments. Its industrial-grade design delivers strong EMC performance and resistance to vibration and shock, while the IP40-rated metal enclosure ensures long-term, stable operation. It offers dual uplinks, including fiber options for long-distance outdoor networking. The standard DIN-rail enclosure enables plug-and-play installation for easy integration into a wide range of industrial communication networking scenarios.
