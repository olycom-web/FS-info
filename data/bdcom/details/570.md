## Overview

BDCOM GP1806-4G series is an intelligent home gateway terminal product launched by BDCOM for multi-service converged networks. It integrates wireless and voice functionalities. It is compatible with the ITU-T G.987.3, G.988, G.989 standards, ensuring excellent interoperability and operability. It can achieve seamless interconnection with OLT from leading industry manufacturers.
