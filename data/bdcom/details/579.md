## Overview

IES200-LS Series is an unmanaged industrial switch engineered for harsh environments. Its industrial-grade design delivers excellent EMC performance and resistance to vibration and shock, while the IP40-rated metal enclosure ensures long-term, stable operation. The standard DIN-rail enclosure supports plug-and-play installation, enabling easy integration into various industrial communication networking scenarios.
