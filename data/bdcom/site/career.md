### Sales Engineer(Thailand, Bangkok)

- **Country/Region:** Thailand
- **City:** Bangkok
- **Category:** Sales Engineer
- **Required Education:** Bachelor's Degree
- **Employment Type:** Full-Time

#### Job requirements

- Bachelor degree or higher in computer science / IT engineering, or relevant experience with comparable qualifications.
- 3+ years of technical experience in the networking field; solid knowledge of switching and routing. Experience with FTTx and WiFi is a plus.
- Excellent oral and written communication skills; English is required.
- Self-motivated to learn and grow.

#### Responsibilities

- Diagnose and resolve BDCOM switching, routing, FTTx, WLAN and other network-related issues reported by partners and customers.
- Collect and analyze market information to identify and develop business opportunities, address customer needs and provide appropriate technical solutions.
- Coordinate with local and HQ sales and support teams to manage account activities and conduct proof-of-concept tests for potential opportunities.
- Provide pre-sales technical consulting and collect new leads for product development and optimization.
- Complete technical documents for projects, training and marketing.
- Travel to meet partners and customers for support and to promote BDCOM products and solutions.
